#### Theme:
Learning how to be adventurers
#### Concept:
Wannabe adventures enrol in the adventurers academy of Wonroth located in Masnyae where they must complete assignments, quests and tests in order to progress through the ranks and earn their rankings before the graduate in 3 years time. Things keep going wrong at the academy which leads to the party investigating the people at the school to see what's going on.
