### What Is It?
Ruvik begins to physically wither and lose his divine connection due to his holy symbol being shattered by Zariel. Over time, his condition progressively worsens while he experiences warning visions and dreams.
### Hook
How do the players learn about it?
- Zariel reveals to the party that he was the one who broke Ruvik's holy symbol.
- Ruvik receives ominous dreams and cryptic warnings telling him that his time is running out ("Do it quick or this will be your last call").
- On the first day of their sea travel to Masnyae, Ruvik's condition noticeably progresses, prompting him to discuss his vision and worsening state with the rest of the party.
### Key NPCs
- **[[Zariel]]** - The antagonist who originally shattered Ruvik's holy symbol.
- **[[Panot (The Bronze Wing)]]** - A dragon located in a cave at Pavv who directs Ruvik toward the fountain needed for his journey.
- **[[Hacklash]]** - A rogue at the church in Pavv whom Ruvik needs to consult regarding his dreams.
- **[[Pharaura (The Fortune Dealer)]]** - A God sealed in a dungeon within the Plane of Fire who performs the ritual to repair the symbol.
### Important Clues
- **The Cryptic Dream:** A white silhouette warns Ruvik of a alignment between Luanris and Aione ("When Luanris and Aione walk side by side their light will shine upon bronze mountains opening 'The Devil's Pathway'").
- **The Time Limit:** A follow-up dream explicitly warns Ruvik to "pave the way to a restored connection before the 48th day".
- **Scrying Mirror:** Daggs hands Ruvik the scrying mirror so he can view the location shown in his vision.
### What Happens?
What is likely to occur if the party investigates?
- The party turns their ship around and heads to Pavv to visit the church and speak with Hacklash.
- They consult Panot in her cave, where she points Ruvik toward the fountain in Pavv.
- The party is transported to the Plane of Fire to explore a dungeon where Pharaura is sealed.
- Ruvik frees Pharaura so she can begin the ritual to fix his broken holy symbol.
- The temple is attacked while the ritual takes place.
### Twist or Revelation
- Restoring the symbol requires travelling to the Plane of Fire and freeing Pharaura to perform the restoration ritual.
### Consequences
#### **If they succeed:**
Ruvik's shattered symbol is successfully repaired, halting his withering condition and restoring his connection to Asmodeus.
#### **If they fail:**
Ruvik's condition will continue to progress, leading to his eventual demise ("this will be your last call") prior to the 48th day deadline.
#### **If they ignore it:**
Ruvik's withering will reach its final stage by the 48th day, killing him.
