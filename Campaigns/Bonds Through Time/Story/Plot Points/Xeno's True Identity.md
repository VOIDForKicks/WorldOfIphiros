### What Is It?
Xeno true identity is discovered causing repercussion based on how its found.
### Hook
Players learn from actions taken by Hadarai (slip of the tongue, disappearances, empty promises)
### Key NPCs
- [[Xeno]] - A personality created through Keldon's corruption of Ouroboros (one of the 7 Heavenly spirits that chose Hadarai) that takes control of Hadarai to do Keldon's bidding
- [[Hadarai Agosto]] - A member of the Chrono Corsairs and Xeno's true identity.
### Important Clues
- **Subtle Slip-Ups:** Hadarai accidentally references future events, uses timeline-inappropriate slang, or speaks of a dead PC's hometown in the past tense.
- **Random Disappearances:** Hadarai vanishes mid-session, returning with altered gear, strange temporal displacement (e.g., aging a week in five minutes), or unexplainable biome dust on his boots.
- **Empty Promises:** As a Life Cleric, he promises healing or divine intervention to NPCs but subtly sabotages the delivery on Xeno's orders
### What Happens?
Hadarai runs away from the party only to reappear in the final battle.
### Twist or Revelation
Hadarai made a deal with an outer-god in order to reunite with his family and friends.
### Consequences
#### If they succeed (Expose him early):
The party confronts Hadarai, forcing Xeno to shed his disguise and flee through a time rift prematurely. The party thwarts Xeno's subversion tactics, securing their timeline's stability. They gain 1-2 levels. Hadarai leaves and spends his time strengthening his forces for his battle against the party.
####  If they fail (Suffer his sabotage):
Hadarai successfully leaks party secrets, steals crucial temporal relics, and defects on his own terms. The party is a lower level than they should be due to Hadarai constantly causing setbacks and building his army.
#### If they ignore it: 
Same as fail.
