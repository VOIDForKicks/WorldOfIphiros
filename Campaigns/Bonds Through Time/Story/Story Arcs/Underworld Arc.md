### Main Problem
What is the big issue the party must deal with?
- The party is trapped in the unforgiving layers of the underworld (Avernus and the Greek Underworld). To escape and rescue an infant named Kenny, they must navigate a high-stakes conflict between archdevils, outsmart the reality-warping defences of the Tower of Urm, and find a way back to the material plane.
### Villain / Threat
- **Name:** [[Bel]] (The Forge Master), [[Alucard]], and [[Keldon (Lord of Chaos)|Keldon]]/[[Xeno]]’s lingering influence.
- **Goal:** Bel seeks to maintain control over Avernus, hunt down the escaped Zariel, and incinerate the party for trespassing in his forge. Alucard acts as a warden within the Tower of Urm, guarding cloning pods and keeping infant Kenny locked in an arcane cage.
### Beginning
How does the story start?
- The party manifests in the bone brambles of Avernus, tracking a map dot leading toward the Tower of Urm. Along the way, Daggs accidentally claims a cursed greatsword that wraps chains around his arm and amplifies his killing instincts. At the Demon Zapper, Sceptrsire coordinates the release of a trapped unicorn, and Johnny uses his warhammer to shatter the structure, freeing the celestial creature.
### Middle
What challenges, discoveries, or setbacks occur?
- **Event 1 (The Liberation of Zariel):** Advancing to a grand Obelisk, the party discovers the white devil Zariel bound in a magic circle. While Ruvik bypasses her guard by communicating with Asmodeus, Daggs strikes a bargain with Zariel to see his sister, Mara, and break his curse. Upon completing the ritual, Zariel is freed, the cursed sword transforms into an ally named Crowley, and the party escapes down the River Styx on Daggs's summoned ship.
- **Event 2 (Infiltrating Bel’s Forge):** The party uses dramatic manoeuvres (pole-vaulting and wall-crawling) to infiltrate Bel’s Forge. After getting caught trying to steal quenching oil, a chaotic brawl breaks out. Johnny and Bel are buried under a collapsed support beam, and Crowley heroically sacrifices himself to restrain the Archdevil so the party can retrieve their powerful Heavenly Spirit weapons and escape the Styx.
- **Event 3 (The Secrets of Urm):** Disguising themselves as fiends, the party talks their way into the Tower of Urm. Inside, Johnny strums his lute, causing nine test specimens in glass pods to briefly morph into past enemies and allies (including Hugh Jackman, Susanoo, and Mara Stormcloak). Ruvik's research reveals the tower's master suffers from split personalities, one trying to give people a second chance, the other plotting a multiversal takeover.
### Climax
What is the final confrontation or major decision?
- Pushing deeper into the tower, the party uncovers Kenny trapped inside an arcane cage, guarded by Alucard. Johnny uncovers Alucard’s phylactery (hidden in a doll) in a nearby chest room and uses it as leverage, threatening the doll and shooting off Alucard's left testicle to force his absolute compliance. After forcing a weeping Alucard to free Kenny, Johnny crafts a makeshift baby carrier out of a crate and rope. The party then returns to Bel’s forge, using invisibility potions, holy oil, and a perfectly placed _Suggestion_ spell from Daggs to command the Archdevil Bel to leave Avernus forever.
### Resolution
What happens after the climax?
- Following a sudden planar shift, the party is pulled out of Avernus and drops directly into the Greek Underworld. After a series of grunted directions from Charon the Ferryman, they navigate past the palace of Hades and Persephone to successfully escape back to safety. Johnny separates to visit Sheylah’s Cabin, while the rest of the Chrono Corsairs head to local shops to procure the specific soil and pots required to grow a magical Nectar Tree.
### Important NPCs

- **[[Zariel]]** - A powerful captive devil who grants the party a massive power boost and breaks Daggs's blade curse in exchange for her freedom.
- **[[Crowley]]** - The entity born from Daggs's cursed sword who valiantly sacrifices his life to pin down Bel, allowing the party to escape the forge.
- **[[Bel]]** - The current ruler and blacksmith of Avernus who is ultimately tricked into leaving the plane entirely via Daggs's magic.
- **[[Alucard]]** - The undead guardian of the Tower of Urm who is utterly defeated and humiliated when Johnny holds his phylactery hostage.
### Rewards
What do the players gain?
- **Treasure:** A temporary level-up to Level 12, the weapons of the Heavenly Spirits, a haul of tower alchemy (3x wyvern poisons, 2x greater healing, 2x invisibility, 2x swiftness), a light blue key belonging to Hadarai, and a Dao's warhammer.
- **Allies:** Kenny is successfully rescued from his black stone crib.
- **Reputation:** Feared by the denizens of Avernus for freeing Zariel, banished an Archdevil, and successfully walked out of Hades' domain alive.
- **Information:**
    - The party receives a disturbing prophetic vision of Schwarzina facing execution at the hands of her sister, Rofs, in a distant palace.
    - The master of the Tower of Urm is running a multiversal timeline cloning experiment.
    - The party pieces together a massive revelation connecting Hadarai and Xeno.
### Consequences
How does the world change after this arc?
- The power vacuum in Avernus is completely destabilized now that Zariel is free and Bel has been magically compelled to abandon his post. By uncovering the cloning pods in the Tower of Urm, the party now knows that gods and their family and friends are being cloned by someone connected to Hadarai.
