### Main Problem

What is the big issue the party must deal with?
- The party must navigate a web of temporal rifts to track down Gerald O'Leary, Daggs old first mate turned a rogue pirate captain under the divine protection of the storm god Susanoo. Along the way, they must secure ancient artefacts, including the katana _Stormbringer_, to neutralize Susanoo's interference and prevent the shadowy schemes of Xeno.
### Villain / Threat
- **Name:** [[Gerald O' Leary]] (backed by [[Susanoo (The Storm-Tamer)]]) and [[Xeno]].
- **Goal:** Gerald aims to maintain his brainwashing control over his crew and exploit divine protection to evade justice. Susanoo seeks total dominance over local temples and powerful relics. Xeno watches from the shadows, waiting for the perfect moment to claim the defeated.
### Beginning
How does the story start?
- Stepping through a rift at the Glaarwater port, Daggs and Johnny manifest on the ship _"The Retribution"_. During their voyage, a second rift drops Ruvik directly into a chaotic naval skirmish. The party successfully rescues two pirates, Gustov and Carlos, before charting a course toward a mysterious orange dot on Daggs's map. After surviving a fierce mid-ocean attack by harpies, they drop anchor at Circe’s Island.
### Middle
What challenges, discoveries, or setbacks occur?
- **Event 1 (Circe’s Bargain & Gerald's Betrayal):** On the island, the Dread Goddess Circe partially transforms Johnny into a pig. To restore him, the party tracks down and brings back Daggs's old crew. However, the crew's unhinged leader, Gerald O'Leary, begins slaughtering Circe's swine and triggers a bloody outpost brawl. The party slays two of his men and frees the brainwashed twins, Aidan and Avery, but Gerald escapes via Susanoo’s divine intervention.
- **Event 2 (The Tortle & The Katana):** Needing an offering for Susanoo, the party sails the _"Scuzzled Paddy"_ to Liltarbrook, leaving _The Retribution_ with Gustov and Carlos. There, Johnny hilariously kidnaps a giant Tortle named Michelangelo to use as a guide. In a nearby cave, Johnny chucks the giant Tortle shell at a horde of goblins, allowing Daggs to claim the legendary _Stormbringer_ katana.
- **Event 3 (Internal Sabotage & The Shipton Crisis):** Sailing toward Othen, a massive argument breaks out on deck. After Daggs slaps Johnny to intervene, Johnny blasts Daggs unconscious with a _Hellish Rebuke_. In retaliation, Daggs burns Johnny’s barrel, prompting Johnny to subtly rig an explosive timer that blows a massive smoking hole in the ship upon arrival at Shipton. While getting the vessel repaired, they learn Susanoo has forcibly seized a nearby temple, threatening the town.
### Climax
What is the final confrontation or major decision?
- The party marches on the seized temple, recruiting a mysterious woodsman named Sceptrsire into their crew, the Chrono Corsairs. In the grand sanctuary, a massive battle erupts. Daggs makes a brilliant tactical gamble: he hands _Stormbringer_ directly to Susanoo, buying the god's departure and stripping Gerald of his divine protection. Suddenly, two mysterious orbs from Daggs's ship fly into the temple, embedding into Aidan and Avery and transforming the twins into the gods Apollo and Artemis. Stripped of his god, Gerald is finally defeated, only for Xeno to step through a portal and snatch Gerald's limp body away.
### Resolution
What happens after the climax?
- With Gerald gone, Daggs temporarily parts ways with the party to guide the newly divine twins to an entity named Donn for training. The remaining party members travel to Northon for a quiet shopping trip, where Ruvik reunites with his son at their old home. After Daggs and an unexpected guest arrive, they face a localized undead assault, successfully rewrite a piece of timeline history, and cleverly extract a relic called the Doss Lute before being suddenly pulled back to Glaarwater.
### Important NPCs
- **[[Gustov the pirate|Gustov]] & [[Carlos the pirate|Carlos]]** - Pirate survivors rescued by the party who seamlessly joined _The Retribution_ and helped pilot the fleet.
- **[[Circe (The Dread Goddess)]]** - A powerful deity who turned Johnny into a swine, setting the party on a quest to hunt down Gerald's crew.
- **[[Aidan]] & [[Avery]] (Apollo & Artemis)** - Twin crew members freed from Gerald's mental thrall who unknowingly absorbed two mystical orbs to ascend as the gods of light and wilderness.
- **[[Michelangelo the Tortle]]** - An aggressively large, turtle-like guild informant who was kidnapped by Johnny and used as a literal throwing weapon against cave goblins.
- **[[Garos Thornton Junior]]** - A key figure who arrived at the Lorvein estate right before an undead ambush, prompting the rewriting of the Freyforest Academy timeline.
### Rewards
What do the players gain?
- **Treasure:** A Bag of Holding, four crates of precious metals (gold, silver, bronze), upgraded Armor/weapons from the Shipton blacksmith, and the ancient Doss Lute.
- **Allies:** Gustov and Carlos, Sceptrsire, and two newly ascended Olympian deities (Apollo and Artemis) who owe them their freedom.
- **Reputation:** Celebrated as the slayers of the pirate tyrant Gerald and the bold adventurers who cleared a rogue deity out of Shipton's sacred temple.
- **Information:**
    - The party’s magical tattoos evolved, explicitly revealing the unique spells they are destined to receive.
    - Informing Garos Jr. about the downfall of the Freyforest Academy actively rewrote history, altering the present timeline.
### Consequences
How does the world change after this arc?
- The balance of power on the high seas has shifted drastically with Gerald O'Leary's criminal empire dismantled and his flagship claimed. Two ancient gods have walked back into the mortal realm via Aidan and Avery. Furthermore, by messing with time and altering the history of the Freyforest Academy, the party has actively caused time to be rewritten, leaving the permanent state of the world highly volatile as they are yanked back to Glaarwater.
