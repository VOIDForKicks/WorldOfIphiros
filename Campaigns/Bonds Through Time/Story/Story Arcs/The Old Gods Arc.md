### Main Problem
What is the big issue the party must deal with?
- The Chrono Corsairs must pierce a massive chronological deception: their long-time ally was an enemy spy in disguise, the real Hadarai is rotting in a high-security capital dungeon, and an ancient timeline is being actively hijacked. They must unseal their divine artefacts, rescue their true handler, and leap blindly into a historic war century in the past to prevent the past from being rewritten.
### Villain / Threat
- **Name:** Rofs Korg and Xeno
- **Goal:** Garos aims to thoroughly misdirect the Chrono Corsairs, study their evolving divine marks from the inside, and ultimately manipulate the time stream to rewrite the outcome of a historic, bloody war. Xeno seeks to break the 7 Heavenly Spirits' influence by shifting the battleground to an era where the party has no footing.
### Beginning
How does the story start?
- The day after the fighting festival the party wake up to discover that their artefacts have solidified there form and are ready for be fully unleashed once the marks' power is unsealed. After some time, the marks the players have morph into the 7 heavenly spirits' respective holy symbol.
### Middle
What challenges, discoveries, or setbacks occur?
- **Event 1 (Hadarai still missing):** After discovering their marks have changed, the party goes looking for Hadarai and discovers the Hadarai they have been with since Pavv is not Hadarai but actually Garos Thornton. Using the scrying mirror, the party finds out that Hadarai is being held at Oxengrad's prison.
- **Event 2 (Mystery Merchant):** In the prison of Oxengrad, the party stumbles across a room with an unknown holy symbol inscribed on it. Stepping inside reveals it is the Mystery Merchant's room, who gives them information on some of the old gods and potentially himself.
- **Event 3 (Garos Thornton jumps through time):** After the party discovers Garos was disguised as Hadarai since they found him at Pavv and rescues Hadarai from prison, Garos forcibly tears a rift in time to 1317 NG with the help of Xeno, immediately sealing it behind him.
### Climax
What is the final confrontation or major decision?
- Fighting the inmates of Oxengrad's prison, the party spot Garos in the crowd and try to stop him but they are unable to stop him, unfortunately causing the prison to become unstable while he escapes. The party search for a way to get to Garos and find out there is a rift open in Othen.
### Resolution
What happens after the climax?
- The party jump through the rift and find themselves in the middle of a battle field between Aclea and Othen.
