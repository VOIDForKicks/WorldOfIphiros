### Main Problem

What is the big issue the party must deal with?
- The party must navigate being suddenly transported to the year 1873, deciphering cryptic warnings of a looming threat named Xeno, and dealing with an rising criminal underworld of bandits operating right under the noses of local settlements.
### Villain / Threat

- **Name:** [[Hugh Jackman]] (Bandit Leader) and the looming figure of [[Xeno]].
- **Goal:** Hugh seeks to run a coordinated bandit operation using local businesses (a merchant shop and a church) as fronts for crime and human trafficking. Xeno's broader goals remain a mystery, but a coded letter implies he is targeting specific bloodlines, including a descendant of Daggs Stormcloak.
### Beginning
How does the story start?
- The party abruptly wakes up in a forest in the year 1873 with mysterious tattoos corresponding to powerful patron entities. After fighting off a pack of wolves, they make their way to the village of Freyforest, register at a newly materialized Adventurers' Guild, and accept a goblin-slaying contract that leads them to a set of ancient ruins.
### Middle
What challenges, discoveries, or setbacks occur?
- **Event 1 (The Ruins & The Prophecy):** While clearing the ruins, they adopt a lone goblin named Schwarz, who is suddenly chosen by one of the 7 Heavenly Spirits. Deep in the ruins, they encounter the spirit of Tital Lorvein, who warns Ruvik that someone named Garos destroyed the ruins before passing on.
- **Event 2 (The Trap at Glaarwater):** Sent to the town of Glaarwater, the party escorts a merchant named Hugh Jackman, only to discover his shop is a front for a bandit hideout. They clear the den, free trapped captives, and kill Hugh, who mysteriously vanishes in a flash of golden light. Daggs recovers a coded letter mentioning "Xeno," "Hugh," and a future descendant of Daggs.
- **Event 3 (The Church & The Mystery Merchant):** Seeking to completely eradicate the bandits for the Glaarwater Guildmaster, Navis, the party uncovers a secondary hideout beneath a local church. While clearing it, they encounter a reality-bending Mystery Merchant who sells them vital secrets: rifts are opening across the realm, a future betrayal is imminent, and 7 Great Powers lay dormant somewhere in the world.
### Climax
What is the final confrontation or major decision?
- After navigating a magical trial room where iron bars trap them, Schwarz successfully strings a heavy bow and shoots an arrow through a row of axes to hit a release switch. Free from the trap, the party tracks down the remaining bandits in the hideout, discovering them playing poker. After a final gamble where the party loses their money, a chaotic brawl erupts as the bandits attack to keep the winnings, resulting in the party completely wiping out the remaining gang.
### Resolution
What happens after the climax?
- With the bandit threat eradicated, the party returns to the Glaarwater Guild Hall to report their success to Navis Quartzcrown and claim their well-earned reward before turning in for a much-needed rest at the liberated shop.
### Important NPCs
- **[[Schwarz]] (Schwarzina)** - A goblin survivor adopted by the party who was chosen by a Heavenly Spirit and saved them during the trial of the bow.
- **[[Hadarai Agosto]]** - The Freyforest Guild handler who gave them the Glaarwater errand and quietly harbours the controversial belief that goblin hunting should be illegal.
- **[[Tital Lorvein]]** - An undead ancestor who delivered a cryptic warning to Ruvik regarding Garos before passing on.
- **[[Navis Quartzcrown]]** - The Guildmaster of Glaarwater who helped identify the repeating names in the coded letter and commissioned the party to destroy the bandits.
- **[[Karios (The Chrono-Sentinel)|Mystery Merchant]]** - A reality-warping shopkeeper hidden in the bandit hideout who traded crucial lore about the rifts, future betrayals, and the 7 Great Powers for copper pieces.
### Rewards
What do the players gain?
- **Treasure:** Gold from wolf pelts/meat, looted items from ransacking Hugh's shop, and gold pieces from completing Guild bounties.
- **Allies:** Schwarz (their goblin companion), Hadarai Agosto, and Guildmaster Navis Quartzcrown.
- **Reputation:** Established themselves as official Guild adventurers with Mid-Gold to Low-Gold rankings, and became localized heroes in Glaarwater for rescuing captives and dismantling the bandit ring.
- **Information:**
    - Garos was responsible for the destruction of the Freyforest ruins.
    - A coded letter proves a figure named Xeno is tracking Daggs' bloodline.
    - Rifts are tearing open across the realm that can transport the party "when they are needed most" to reach Xeno.
    - A warning of a future betrayal and the existence of the 7 dormant Great Powers (The Leviathan, The Reborn, The Guardian, The Origin, The All Knowing, The Trickster, and The Unending).
### Consequences
How does the world change after this arc?
- The local bandit network in the Glaarwater region has been completely dismantled, saving citizens from human trafficking and reclaiming a corrupted church. However, cosmic gears have begun to turn: cosmic rifts are opening worldwide, the 7 Great Powers are awakening, a Heavenly Spirit has chosen a goblin, and the party has firmly placed themselves on the radar of a shadowy threat named Xeno.
