### Main Problem
What is the big issue the party must deal with?
- The party must dismantle a oppressive, racist syndicate known as the Guspacho, investigate a corrupt localized branch of the Adventurers' Guild, and halt a demonic plot involving a hidden gateway to the Nine Hells. This rapidly escalates when a local mayor is assassinated, and his infant son, who possesses major multiversal significance, is kidnapped and taken deep into Avernus.
### Villain / Threat
- **Name:** The [[Guspacho]], the Bone Devil [[Izzamis]], and [[Alucard]].
- **Goal:** The Guspacho seek to hunt down Johnny’s close contact, Sheylah. Izzamis coordinates an underground infernal cult out of a false temple to claim mortal sacrifices. The "Red Man" aims to murder local figures like Mayor Reginald and kidnap infant Kenny for timeline-cloning experiments within the Tower of Urm.
### Beginning
How does the story start?
- Returning to the material plane, the party seeks out Sheylah’s cabin in the town of Thorpes. There, they discover a holy symbol belonging to the newly ascended deity Artemis and learn she is being actively hunted by a racist group called the Guspacho. Infiltrating the Thorpes guild hall, they discover it is entirely run by the syndicate; a brutal gunfight erupts, and Johnny blows the leader's head off, unleashing the latent powers buried within his mark and liberating the town.
### Middle
What challenges, discoveries, or setbacks occur?
- **Event 1 (The False Temple & Hellish Poison):** Exploring further, the party stumbles upon a temple of Asmodeus that drops them straight into a pocket layer of the Nine Hells. Johnny impulsively drinks wyvern poison, suffering massive damage. After stabbing open a treasure mimic to claim a mountain of gems, they encounter Izzamis, the bone devil right hand of Asmodeus. The party uses creative trickery, feeding highly lethal wyvern poison to a sacrificed goblin and an unsuspecting Oni guard before slaying the giant beast in the woods.
- **Event 2 (The Temple Demolition & Party Fracturing):** Seeking a way to permanently crush Izzamis's operation, the party tracks down a Treant named Trent and an Eladrin named Benadryl. They convince the Eladrin to stage an explosive sabotage inside the false temple. However, chaos reigns when a panicking bandit detonates a massive cache of oil and barrels. The resulting cataclysmic explosion buries Izzamis but nearly kills Ruvik, who has to be dragged out of the collapsing structure as the portal closes.
- **Event 3 (The Birth of the Death Church):** Rising from the rubble, a severe ideological argument fractures the party. Ruvik, a devout follower of Asmodeus, is deeply angered over the handling of the temple occupants, while Daggs claims the altar for the god Donn. Ultimately, the party balances the tension by combining their holy symbols on the altar to forge the realm's first official Church of the Death Domain.
### Climax

What is the final confrontation or major decision?

- The party attends a town assembly where Mayor Reginald gives a speech thanking them for liberating Thorpes. Suspicious of his motives, Johnny uses aggressive intimidation to force the mayor to host them for a meal. Arriving at his house, they are shocked to discover the mayor's son is infant Kenny Dollardyne. After leaving to gather supplies and illicitly rob a local shop cash drawer for gold and platinum, they return to find Mayor Reginald brutally murdered. Kenny is gone, and a message written in blood reads: _"Red man"_. Communing with Donn, the party opens a gateway and launches a rescue operation into Avernus.
### Resolution
What happens after the climax?
- Infiltrating the Tower of Urm in Avernus under fiendish disguises, the party discovers that the complex contains cloning pods holding copies of multiversal tyrants and heroes. They track down Alucard, who is keeping baby Kenny locked in an arcane cage. Johnny tracks down Alucard's phylactery and uses it as absolute leverage, shooting off Alucard's left testicle and threatening his life until the undead warden weeps and releases the baby. Johnny creates a makeshift carrier out of a crate and rope to secure Kenny as they map out their exit from Hell.
### Important NPCs
- **[[Sheylah]]** - An artisan and friend of Johnny who crafted his childhood lute and was saved from a racist hit squad.
- **[[Izzamis]]** - A powerful Bone Devil acting as Asmodeus's right hand who is buried alive under a demolished temple.
- **[[Trent]] & [[Benadryl]]** - A local Treant and Eladrin who provided intelligence and explosive magical leverage to help bring down the infernal cult.
- **[[Reginald]] (Reggie)** - The mayor of Thorpes who is assassinated by the "Red Man" after revealing his connection to Avernus.
- **[[Kenny Dollardyne]]** - A baby containing timeline-altering significance who is kidnapped from his home and later rescued by the party.
### Rewards

What do the players gain?
- **Treasure:** 7 sapphires, 6 emeralds, and 9 rubies extracted from a killed mimic; stolen platinum and gold from a Thorpes shop cash drawer; and an assortment of high-tier potions (3x wyvern poisons, 2x greater healing, 2x invisibility, 2x swiftness) from the Tower of Urm.
- **Allies:** Hadarai Agosto assists them during the escape, and baby Kenny is safely recovered.
- **Reputation:** Rebuilt their fractured party dynamics, became celebrated heroes of Thorpes for wiping out the Guspacho, and successfully established the first-ever Church of the Death Domain.
- **Information:**
    - Artemis's holy symbol is actively protecting Sheylah's property.
    - Johnny unlocks the latent magical power within his personal mark.
    - The master of the Tower of Urm possesses two split personalities,  one giving second chances at life, and the other plotting a multiversal timeline takeover using cloned subjects.
### Consequences
How does the world change after this arc?
- The town of Thorpes has been permanently liberated from fascist control, and a historic new religious focal point, the Church of the Death Domain, stands in place of an infernal gateway. However, by removing infant Kenny from his stone crib in Avernus and discovering the multiversal cloning vats, the Chrono Corsairs have directly disrupted a cosmic timeline plot. They have permanently delayed the designs of the "Red Man" and Xeno, setting themselves up as the primary targets for whatever reality-warping entities control the Tower of Urm.
