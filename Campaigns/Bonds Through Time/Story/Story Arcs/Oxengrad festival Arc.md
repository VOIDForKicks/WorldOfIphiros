### Main Problem
What is the big issue the party must deal with?
- The party must investigate the sudden disappearance of their Freyforest Guild handler, Hadarai Agosto. This investigation uncovers a plot by contract assassins targeting the Chrono Corsairs, a direct intervention by Xeno, and ultimately leads the party to disguise themselves to compete in a high-stakes capital fighting festival.
### Villain / Threat
- **Name:** The Assassins, [[Xeno]], and rival festival competitors.
- **Goal:** The assassins aim to collect a bounty on the Chrono Corsairs' heads. Xeno continues to pull strings from the shadows, utilizing mind control on Hadarai. In Oxengrad, rival fighters (including an aggrieved local blacksmith and displacer beasts) seek to knock the party out of the tournament brackets.
### Beginning
How does the story start?
- Upon returning to the Glaarwater Guild Hall, Navis Quartzcrown informs the party that Hadarai has gone missing from the Freyforest branch. At the same time, Johnny receives his first divine task from the Matron of Death, the Raven Queen, and claims a church in her name. The party returns to a chronologically restored Freyforest and enlists a group of young magic students to help them break into Hadarai's office.
### Middle
What challenges, discoveries, or setbacks occur?
- **Event 1 (The Scrying Mirror & Pavv):** Inside the office, the party discovers a coded letter and a scrying mirror. Discovering that the local blacksmith, Eermiaw, cannot read or write, Daggs utilizes the scrying mirror and witnesses Hadarai locked in a prison cell. Cross-referencing his magical map, Daggs finds a matching dot located in the mountain village of Pavv.
- **Event 2 (The Mine Ambush & Xeno's Summoning):** Arriving in Pavv, Daggs uses Thieves' Cant to learn from local contacts, Hacklash and Lorion, that contract assassins have been paid to eliminate the party. The party falls back to a nearby mine to wait out the threat, where they defeat the ambushers and break the mind control enslaving Hadarai. In a bold move, Ruvik draws a pentagram on Hadarai's head to successfully summon Xeno directly to them.
- **Event 3 (Seducing a Dragon & Tournament Setup):** The next day, the party returns to the mine and encounters its resident dragon, Panot. Johnny attempts to seduce her, only to suffer a panic attack upon realizing Panot is actually a Young Golden Dragon. They negotiate a contract: Panot will protect Pavv in exchange for fair compensation and a renovated den. Following this, the party travels to the capital city of Oxengrad for the annual fighting festival.
### Climax
What is the final confrontation or major decision?
- After progressing through the brackets, the Chrono Corsairs face off against a devastatingly powerful opponent named Alucard in the final bout of the tournament. The party lands some heavy hits, forcing Alucard to call upon Keldon, the Lord of Chaos, who grants him dark strength explicitly to crush the party because they are the chosen of the 7 Heavenly Spirits. Alucard unleashes a brutal assault that nearly kills Johnny, forcing Johnny to desperately call on the Raven Queen for aid. Through sheer grit, the party narrowly avoids a lethal defeat and manages to overcome Alucard to win the festival.
### Resolution
What happens after the climax?
- With Alucard defeated and the tournament officially won, the battered party heads straight to the local bookies to cash in their massive betting slips and collect their hard-earned earnings.
### Important NPCs
- **[[Navis Quartzcrown]]** - The Glaarwater Guildmaster who alerts the party to Hadarai's sudden disappearance.
- **[[Hacklash]] & [[Lorion]]** - Underworld contacts in Pavv who speak Thieves' Cant and tip Daggs off about the active assassination contracts.
- **[[Panot (The Bronze Wing)|Panot]]** - A Young Golden Dragon residing in the Pavv mines who agrees to protect the town under a fair business contract.
- **[[Hadarai Agosto]] / "Magic Mike"** - The rescued Guild handler who entered the tournament alongside Schwarzina, forcing a massive clash with the party in the earlier rounds.
- **[[Alucard]]** - The final tournament boss who channelled the Lord of Chaos and nearly slaughtered Johnny in the arena.
### Rewards
What do the players gain?
- **Treasure:** A massive payout of platinum and gold collected from the bookies after winning the tournament at 25/1 odds.
- **Allies:** Hadarai is safely rescued, and Panot the Young Golden Dragon is secured as a powerful guardian for Pavv.
- **Reputation:** Crowned the undisputed champions of the capital's annual fighting festival, even if they did it under false names.
- **Information:**
    - Johnny establishes a direct divine link to the Raven Queen, proving powerful enough to save his life in the final bout.
    - The party learns that powerful entities are actively hiring contract killers to terminate the Chrono Corsairs.
    - Keldon, the Lord of Chaos, is actively targeting the party because they are linked to the 7 Heavenly Spirits.
### Consequences
How does the world change after this arc?
- The town of Pavv is now under the direct, contractual protection of a Golden Dragon. Hadarai has been safely returned to the physical world. However, the stakes have escalated from mortal threats to cosmic ones: by winning the tournament and defeating Alucard, the Chrono Corsairs have officially drawn the ire of Keldon, the Lord of Chaos. The dark gods now know exactly who the chosen of the 7 Heavenly Spirits are, and a divine target has been painted squarely on the party's backs.
