### What happened
Dean decides to take the party to the Greek underworld.
They "speak" to Charon about where they need to go to escape and get grunts in response.
Party makes their way up to the palace of Hades and Persephone.
They meet the people working in the palace.
Party escape the Underworld and Johnny heads off to [[Sheylah's Cabin]].
The rest of the party go to the shops to get a pot and soil to grow an nectar tree.
