### What happened
On the last day of the journey the party manage to make it to a mysterious island in the middle of the ocean.
While talking to Circe, Johnny gets partially transformed into a pig.
In exchange for Johnny being turned back the party must bring Circe someone from Daggs old crew.
The party head off to get Daggs crew and manage to negotiate with them to follow them to Circe's Island.
Avery is brought back to Circe for her to check for any enchantments.
Gerald and the crew begin slaughtering the pigs on the island and start arguing with Ruvik.
