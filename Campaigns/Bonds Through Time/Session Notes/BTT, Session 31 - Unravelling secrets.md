### What happened
Sceptrsire plants nectar tree on ship so it can wrap around the crews nest as it grows.
Party start to interrogate Hadarai about the [[Tower of Urm]] and his connection to it.
Ruvik and Daggs are suspicious of Hadarai.
The party have an aside and Ruvik says he can set his people to keep an eye on him.
Daggs catches Hadarai casting a spell and calls it out.
Hadarai leaves the ship and heads into town.
Ruvik gets his 3 scouts to tail Hadarai (Daggs is bewildered).
In town the party see people running away from a certain party of town and they decide to head There and see what's going on and find a hoard of undead.
A banshee escapes from the combat to return with reinforcements another day.
After the combat Sceptrsire loots 2 sets of rusted armour.
Daggs uses message to tell Hadarai to get to the Residential area.
Ruvik touches a pillar marked with symbols of gods opposing Asmodeus shattering his holy symbol.
Ruvik breaks the pillar releasing [[Zariel]] into the material plane.
