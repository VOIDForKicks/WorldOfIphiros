### What happened
On the trip to Susanoo's temple the party come across a man in the woods who introduces himself as Sceptrsire.
Sceptrsire joins the Chrono Corsairs.
Party discuss what they are going to do in order to get to Gerald.
During the battle Daggs manages to give Stormbringer to Susanoo and he leaves with the promise that he will not stop them from killing Gerald.
The two orbs come flying into the temple and into Aidan and Avery's backs being absorbed by them making the twins the gods Apollo and Artemis.
After defeating Gerald, Xeno appears and tosses Gerald's limp body through the portal he created.
