### What happened
The year is 1873
The party wakes up in a forest between a river and a set of tower ruins.
They notice that they all have tattoos based on the being that claimed them.

| Character           | Tattoo                                             |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Ruvik Lorvein       | A mace/morningstar with fiery wings behind it      |
| Johnny Goldenfinger | A shield with 3 wolf heads on it                   |
| Daggs Stormcloak    | A scimitar with tentacles wrapped around the blade |
A mage captain and 2 guards get to the pond the players are at.
Ruvik asks where the closest town/village is.
The party is informed about Freyforest.
One of the guards gets insulted by Johnny before they leave.
A pack of wolves attack the party.
After beating the wolves the party skin and cut up the wolves to be sold later.
The party regroups and introduces themselves before heading to the nearest village
After heading to the blacksmith they meet the black smith in charge Eermiaw Xaldreth.
