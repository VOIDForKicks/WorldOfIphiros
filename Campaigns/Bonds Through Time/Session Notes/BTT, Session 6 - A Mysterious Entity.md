### What happened
The following day Daggs is missing but Ruvik and Johnny don't seem too concerned.
Continuing their sweep of the hideout they come across two doors one leading into a big room and the other into a smaller room away from where they want to go.
They decide to enter the smaller room.
Inside the room it looks like a shop and behind the counter is a mysterious figure.
Ruvik and Johnny begin talking to the merchant about the wares they are selling.
The merchant informs them that they also sell information while pointing at a jar on the table.
Ruvik buts a copper piece into the jar and the merchant tells them about the rifts that are opening up around the world that the party must use to get to [[Xeno]].
After a few more copper pieces the merchant gives them all the info they correct have to offer
	Rifts are opening around the realm. Going through them will get the party to when needs you the most
	The party may get betrayed in the "future"
	7 great powers lay dormant nearby A deep ocean ([[Mariana (The Leviathan)|The Leviathan]]), A rejuvenating flame ([[Pyra (The Reborn)|The Reborn]]), A hellish rage ([[Hytos (The Guardian)|The Guardian]]), A thunderous power ([[Ormu (The Origin)|The Origin]]), A trove of information ([[Anare (The All Knowing)|The All Knowing]]), A rush of energy ([[Yuki (The Trickster)|The Trickster]]), A never ending cycle ([[Ouroboros (The Unending)|The Unending]]).
After leaving the room the ground begins to shake and the door to the shop disappears.
Going into the big room iron bars fall down trapping them in the room where they must complete a trial.
A message appears on the wall "Whoever can string the bow and shoot through the axes may be set free."
After Struggling to string the bow Schwarz is able to fire an arrow through the axes hitting a button at the other side of the axes setting them free.
Heading through the rest of the hideout the party come across more bandits playing poker and decide to join in.
After losing all their money the bandits attack the party in order to get it back.
Finishing off the bandits the party decide to head back to the guild hall to claim the rewards from the quest before turning in for the night.
