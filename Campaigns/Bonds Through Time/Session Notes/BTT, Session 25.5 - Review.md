Removed Rules
No more bingo cards

Combat Rules Added
Time turns: 
Each player much decide what they are going to do with in 2 mins of their turn starting.

Over-Hit:
Getting double the targets AC causes damage to be 1.5x more than normal (After all rolls including crits)

Exploration Rules Added
Initiative: 
Rolled at start of a journey or dungeon and rerolled after each rest.

Marching Order:
If the party is going through a hallway the people behind the person in the front cannot make attacks past them without disadvantage.

Roll Playing Rules Added
Reputation:
Scale -10 to 10
High infamy (+4) with a group can allow a party to have an easier time with negotiations and maybe get discounts.
Low infamy (-4) with a group or town can cause them to hike prices or even start combat on sight.
