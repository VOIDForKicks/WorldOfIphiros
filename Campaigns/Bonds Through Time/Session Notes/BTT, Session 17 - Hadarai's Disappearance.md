### What happened
The party begin to search for clues on the whereabouts.
They first head into the guild hall where they see a group of people studying magic.
While talking to them they ask them to help them get into [[Hadarai Agosto|Hadarai]]'s office.
Inside they find a letter that is written in code as well as a scrying mirror.
The party head to Eermiaw blacksmith hoping he knows how to read what's on the letter.
Ruvik discovers that Eermiaw cannot read or write.
After their discussion Daggs decides to use the scrying mirror they got from the guild hall.
While peering through it Daggs spots Hadarai locked up in a cell.
Daggs then looks at his map to see if there is any dots that could lead to Hadarai.
He notices that there is a dot currently located in a mountain village known as Pavv.
