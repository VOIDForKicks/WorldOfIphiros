### What happened
Ruvik and Gerald are arguing causing Gerald to tell the his crew to attack the Circe's house.
During the battle the party kill Gregory and Terrance but are able to free Aidan and Avery from Gerald's control.
Gerald escapes with the help of Susanoo.
Circe informs the part that they need to find an offering in order to get to Gerald.
The party take the "[[Scuzzled Paddy]]" will Gustov and Carlos take "[[The Retribution]]" and head off for [[Liltarbrook]] on a 4 day journey to find the offering for Susanoo and magic items for themselves.
