### What happened
Dean was running late so Daggs stays safe at the camp.
Matt believes Izzamis is weak due to him sending the oni after them.
The party head back towards the dead oni to find clues on beating Izzamis.
They find the EXPLODED corpse and Ruvik tries to listen for a heartbeat in his legs falling on top of them.
Johnny finds tracks leading towards a glade.
Party follows tracks with a treant at the end of them.
Ruvik speaks to it to get info about the tracks they followed and the treant (Trent) points them towards the eladrin of the forest which was dubbed Benadryl by the party.
The party convince the eladrin to blow up Izzamis and the temple with it.
One of the treants is slow due to a chunk of one of its legs being missing.
Ruvik and Johnny get into an argument about blowing up the temple.
Ruvik talks to one of the members of Izzamis cult and tells them to get everyone out of there.
The eladrin casts suggestion on a goblin to blow up the goblins. (one round of combat before detonation)
Daggs uses his mage hand to grab a torch off a wall hovering over a line of oil and potentially causes a tpk.
A bandit panics and tosses a barrel at the goblin detonating all but 3 barrels in the false temple.
Rumble buries Izzamis in the temple.
Ruvik barely survives the explosion but getting knocked unconscious being carried out of the temple by the eladrin and Hadarai.
Upon getting out of the temple the portal to the nine-hells closes behind them.
Ruvik and Johnny discuss who the temple should go too, while Daggs goes up to the alter to claim it for Donn.
Ruvik debates leaving the party and calls Asmodeus to settle the discussion.
The party place their holy symbols on the altar creating the first [[Church of Death]].
The party separate for the night Daggs by the river, Johnny goes back to the camp and Ruvik gets a room at the local inn.
