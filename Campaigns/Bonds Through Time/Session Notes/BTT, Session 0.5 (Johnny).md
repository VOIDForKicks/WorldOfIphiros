### What happened
Johnny Goldenfinger is preforming on the streets to make some money.
Guards come up to Johnny and tell him to pack up and go home.
While wandering the city Johnny goes into an inn and sees Kenny Dollardyne talking to a guard captain.
Johnny follows Kenny to his room and confronts him about what he was saying to the guard finding out that [[Barty McFoap]] and [[Judy Vespa]] have been targeted by the city guard.
Johnny wanders Oxengrad until he is ambushed by a guard captain and 2 guards.
Johnny gets protected from some of the guards strikes from a mysterious shield.
A blinding light engulfs Johnny transporting him to the "main" time period (1873).
In the blinding light the Strength of hell builds in him.
