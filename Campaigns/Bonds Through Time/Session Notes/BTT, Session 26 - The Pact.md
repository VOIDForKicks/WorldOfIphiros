### What happened
Party decide to head to [[The Obelisk]] to commune with the white devil Zariel.
At the pit of Shummrath Sceptrsire speaks to two imps where point out that Bel is the current ruler of Avernus and Zariel is up at the Obelisk.
While speaking to the imps Sceptrsire discerns that the imps are there to kill Zariel.
The imps kneel before the party and hand them a sickle.
At the Obelisk the party investigate the pillars surrounding it
The chain devil begins to chant a spell to dispel the binding circle holding Zariel.
Ruvik casts tongues on Daggs so he can understand the Sword.
Daggs speaks parts of the incantation as to not dispel the circle.
Ruvik speaks with Asmodeus to allow them move closer to Zariel.
Once the spells power decreases Zariel wakes up and stares at Daggs.
Ruvik and Sceptrsire speak to Zariel while Johnny stays with Daggs who is communicating with the sword.
Sceptrsire strikes a deal with Zariel.
Daggs makes his own deal with Zariel for a to have a sword that is lighter than the cursed blade he has and to see his sister ([[Mara Stormcloak]]).
Daggs speaks the incantation freeing Zariel.
Zariel frees Daggs from his cursed sword and it becomes a chain devil (Crowley) before giving the players a power boost.
Daggs summons his ship onto the [[River Styx]] and the party jump onto it with devils behind.
Johnny is reunited with his one true love the barrel.
Johnny speaks with the [[Raven Queen (The Matron of Death)|Raven Queen]].
The Raven Queen answers Johnny's questions.
On the way to Bel's Forge they are attacked by a group of fiends.
The party makes it to the forge.
Temporarily levelled up to lvl 12.
