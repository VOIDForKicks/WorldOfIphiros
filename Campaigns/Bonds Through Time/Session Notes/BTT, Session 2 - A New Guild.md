### What happened
After waking up the following day the party notice a new building has popped up.
The party head to the building and realise it is a guild hall.
Inside is Hadarai.
After speaking with him the party register at the guild and take the skill assessment test to determine what difficulty of mission they can take on.

| Character | Rank     |
| --------- | -------- |
| Johnny    | Low-Gold |
| Daggs     | Mid-Gold |
| Ruvik     | Mid-Gold |

After this the party look at the job board and see that they have completed on of the quests already and hand it in.
They also decide to take on a goblin slaying quest.
The Party head off to the river they woke up at to find the goblins.
While approaching the ruins by the river the goblins appear and attack them.
After a hard fought battle there is only one goblin left which they decide to adopt and name Schwarz.
Daggs discovers a trapdoor down to a basement of the ruins.
