### What happened
The party find themselves in the bone brambles of Avernus.
Laying against one of the bone trees is Sceptrsire.
Daggs checks his map to see where they should be going.
A dot points them to the Tower of Urm.
Daggs finds a shambling mound at a dead end.
Sceptrsire sticks an arm into the mound and pulls out a melting arm.
The party run from the mound before it does anything.
Daggs feels the gaze of multiple pairs of eyes.
On the way to the [[Demon Zapper]], Daggs picks up a Cursed great sword and it wraps chains around his arm.
The party arrive at the Demon Zapper.
Ralzala appears.
Sceptrsire wants to free the unicorn in the orb above and use it to remove the imps from the material plane.
The Chain Devil in Daggs sword builds his killing instincts.
Sceptrsire tries to convince the party to take the unicorn to the material plane with them.
The chain devil "forces" Daggs to attack the Doa.
Combat begins.
After combat the Dao turns to dust leaving only its warhammer behind.
Daggs goes away from the party to commune with the chain devil.
Johnny picks up the warhammer and strikes down the tower (2x nat 20's world did not implode) freeing the unicorn.
Daggs fights back the urge to kill the unicorn.
The party discuss what to do before heading to the Tower of Urm.
The party make a plan to head to the [[Tower of Urm]] ([[Alucard]]), then to [[Bel's forge]] ([[Bel]]) and finally [[The Obelisk]] ([[Zariel]]).
