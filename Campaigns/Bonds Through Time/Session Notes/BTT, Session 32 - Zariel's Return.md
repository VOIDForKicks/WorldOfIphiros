### What happened
Johnny appears in town with Schwarz in tow.
Zariel begins telling Zariel about how he wants to take control of The Nine Hells AND the Material Plane.
Zariel reveals that he was the one to break Ruviks holy symbol.
The Knights of Helm show up and Zariel runs.
Lankath spots Daggs sneaking around the alley's.
Daggs walks up to Lankath and makes an enemy of the group.
Daggs uses the scrying mirror to observe Zandrun and sees them moving toward Wheldrake.
Hadarai then uses the mirror to scry on Zariel but gets attacked and falls unconscious.
Ruvik and Daggs stabilise him and bring him to a bed before rummaging through his stuff.
They find a journal on him with entries on the markings everyone has.
The guys find out that [[Terry]] and [[Xeno]] may be connected or the same person.
Daggs takes all Hadarai's weapons.
The party make their way to Wheldrake and head to the church in town.
Lankath again insults Ruvik and Daggs gets angry.
Daggs tries to strike a deal with Zandrun but Zandrun wants Schwarz to join the knights of helm.
Sceptrsire talks Zandrun out of taking Schwarz.
Ruvik drinks a mystery potion and becomes a fire genasi for 1 day.
The party go shopping for supplies before heading back to the ship.
After half a day of travel the party arrive at Dhagrol and head toward Zariel.
The party defeat Zariel and take his two weapons before being pulled back to the arena.
