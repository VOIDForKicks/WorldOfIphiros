Johnny learns that the dragons name in Panot and tries to seduce her. Has panic attack because Panot is just a Young Golden Dragon (17 years old).
They come to an agreement with Panot that she will protect the town of Pavv if her den gets renovated to allow here to come and go as she pleases and she must be fairly compensated.
