### What happened
Party make it to the entrance of [[Bel's forge]].
Ruvik pole vaults into the forge while Daggs misty steps in and Johnny Spiderman's in.
Johnny nearly gets the party caught by strumming his lute.
Ruvik heads upstairs causing Bel to break his weapon and heads upstairs to grab some quenching oil.
While this is happening Daggs hears a weapon calling to him and he heads over to it to take it.
Ruvik tries to steal a barrel of the quenching but gets caught.
Bel confronts Ruvik on why he is there and is informed that Zariel has been freed.
Ruvik lies to Bel about the party that freed Zariel
Johnny drags Bel to the anvil using the sword he was crafting and Daggs shoots a poorly maintained support beam.
Johnny and Bel get buried under rubble.
Bel thinks Ruvik caused the walls collapse and attacks him.
During this time Ruvik grabs a hilt and it transforms into a great hammer.
Combat starts.
Crowley sacrifices himself to restrain Bel and tells the party to leave.
The party get their weapons of the heavenly spirits. 
Daggs and Sceptrsire test out their new weapons. Daggs goes flying and Sceptrsire is restrained.
The party try to get Daggs out of the [[River Styx]].
Daggs is pulled out of the water.
