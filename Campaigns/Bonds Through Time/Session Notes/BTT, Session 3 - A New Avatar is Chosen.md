### What happened
Down in the Freyforest ruins the party begin to explore the ruins where they come across some undead creatures.
After one of the fights within the ruins Schwarz there new goblin companion gets chosen by one of the 7 heavenly spirits.
Within a hidden room stands an undead creature that resembles Tital Lorvein.
Before passing on Tital warns Ruvik that Garos was the one that caused the ruins to be the way they are today.
After this interaction the party decide to leave the dungeon as it is too dangerous for them to explore.
Once back at Freyforest the party head straight to the guild hall to claim the reward they have earned.
While speaking with Hadarai, he says that goblin hunting is illegal as goblins and the like are normal citizens of society.
The party does not pick up on this as it is said under his breath.
Hadarai askes the party to head to Glaarwater to run some errands.
The party then head to the black smiths in order to get new equipment before setting off for the next town.
