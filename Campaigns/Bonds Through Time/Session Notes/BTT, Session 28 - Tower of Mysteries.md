### What happened
The party make their way towards the Tower of Urm where they presume Kenny is.
Daggs scries on Aiden and Avery.
Aidan is playing his lyre in an inn unaware he is being watched.
Avery is out hunting with her nymph friends where she stops to look for what's staring at her.
Before the party drift off to sleep they get a vision of Schwarz in a palace being put to execution by her sister Rofs.
On the walk to the [[Tower of Urm]], Johnny gets curious on where Daggs' ship goes.
Daggs tells him it goes back to his home town of Old Ashton.
Johnny scries on Kenny and gets a glimpse of red cloth moving away from Kenny in a black stone crib.
Johnny interrogates Zariel 
Daggs uses his spyglass to recon the area.
Johnny and Daggs decide to disguise themselves as fiends and pretend to take Ruvik into the person that's in there.
The party convince the demons to let them in and deliver Ruvik to the boss.
Inside the tower the party find 3 books "[[Storm cloaked pirate]]", "[[Rise of Norton]]", and "[[The Exiled Princess]]".
In the room there are 9 specimens contained in pods around the room.
Johnny strums his lute and the specimens begin to take form as "[[Hugh Jackman]]", "[[Susanoo (The Storm-Tamer)|Susanoo]]", "[[Judy Vespa]]", "[[Kenny Dollardyne]]", "[[Mara Stormcloak]]", "[[Gerald O' Leary]]", "[[Coal Lorvein]]" and "[[Garos Thornton]]"
Johnny stops playing and the specimen return to normal.
Searching through the table again Ruvik decides to read the notes and journal finding out that the person who lived here had 2 personalities one trying to give people a second chance at life and the other is trying to take over the multiverse/timelines.
The party go through the portal into a room with an arcane circle, a bookshelf and a potion rack.
Daggs takes the potions on the rack (3x wyvern poisons, 2x greater healing, 2x invisibility, 2x swiftness)
Ruvik checks the shelf and finds a used remove curse scroll and a key of light blue hue (Hadarai)
Johnny opens the pot in the middle of the room and releases a jinn.
Daggs persuades the jinn to look at a wall spinning his head 180 degrees snapping his neck.
Daggs jumps through a red portal going to a sacrifice room.
The party make it to the treasure room and Daggs pulls Johnny out of his bag of holding.
Waking up all the mimics
Ruvik and Daggs makes a run for it through the portal ending up in a room with a cage and Kenny in the middle of it.
Alucard shows up in the room and threatens Daggs and Ruvik if they don't leave.
Johnny finds Alucard phylactery in the chest room and he rushes into the room to stop him.
Alucard pleads for his life while Johnny threatens his doll.
They take him to the room with Kenny in it and make him free Kenny.
Party have a revelation about Hadarai and Xeno.
Ruvik speaks to Zariel about the current objective.
