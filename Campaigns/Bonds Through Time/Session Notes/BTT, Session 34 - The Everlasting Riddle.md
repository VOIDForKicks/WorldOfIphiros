### What happened
Party collects prize money.
Daggs checks map for the dots they were following.
Party goes to an inn and get rooms.
Daggs tries to intimidate the barkeep for free drinks but his glass eye pops out, Johnny seduces the barkeep and is promised sex and free drinks.
Ruvik gets a dream of a white silhouette repeating the same words to him "When [[Luanris (The Walking Night)|Luanris]] and [[Aione (The Absent Night)|Aione]] walk side by side their light will shine upon bronze mountains opening "The Devil's Pathway". Convince the priest or the village will fall. Do it quick or this will be your last call."
Down in the bar Sceptrsire walks out of the inn to put on his hat of disguise and walk back in as "Johnny's evil twin".
The party talk to the barkeep the next day and take a calendar from him to figure out about Luanris and Aione.
Daggs gives Ruvik the scrying mirror for him to see the place from his dream.
Johnny argues with the party about the dots moving.
Daggs goes looking for any suspicious activity outside and speaks to a homeless man. Finding out that the kings and queens from other nations are gathering at Oxengrad in a weeks time.
Daggs grabs a flag from his home nation of Masnyae.
Daggs searches for a boat and finds a row boat.
Daggs, Johnny and Schwarz make it onto the boat quietly but Ruvik and Sceptrsire come barrelling down the road alerting the guards.
Party set sail for Masnyae.
The first day of travel Ruvik's condition progresses and he gets another dream "Where shadows talk and winds whispers, where gold is hoarded and threats extinguished. Pave the way to a restored connection before the 48th day."
Discusses it with the others.
On Hytorion 26th Ruvik studies the moons and finds nothing.
Figures out riddle and turns ship around to go back to Pavv.
Talks to Hacklash at church.
Headed to Panot's cave.
