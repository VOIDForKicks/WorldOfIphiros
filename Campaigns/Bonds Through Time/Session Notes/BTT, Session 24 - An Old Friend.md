### What happened
Daggs goes looking for Johnny and Ruvik after they head their separate ways.
Johnny shares info with Daggs about a job he was told about in the inn.
They discuss what to do about Ruvik and his potential departure from the group.
Party head to the church after they see the towns people heading there.
The mayor (Reginald/Reggie) gives a speech about how they have been freed from the [[Guspacho]].
Reggie is a little suspicious about why the party was there and how they managed to clean the place up over night.
Ruvik communes with Asmodeus trying to find out what to do next and is told to use the party to spread the word of "The one true god".
Daggs convinces Ruvik to put the past behind him and look forward so that he can save people in the future from turning on Asmodeus.
Johnny interrogates Reggie about why he wants to know about the party.
Johnny intimidates Reggie and convinces him to take the party to his house for some food.
They get to his house and notice he has a son.
The son is Kenny.... Kenny Dollardyne.
Reggie informs the party of a man in red that resides in [[Avernus]] the first layer of hell.
Daggs communes with Donn to try and get into Avernus and save [[Sheylah]] from her fate.
The party plan on getting supplies before heading to Avernus.
Johnny tries to get a fire resistance breastplate for cheap.
Johnny distracts the shopkeeper while Daggs steals some platinum and gold from the cash drawer.
The party buy a few items from the shop and head to Reggie's house.
Inside they find a dead Reggie and no Kenny to be found.
Johnny and Daggs see a message written in blood "Red man".
Party heads into Avernus.
