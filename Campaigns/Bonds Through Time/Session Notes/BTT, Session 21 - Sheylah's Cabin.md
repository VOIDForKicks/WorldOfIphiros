### What happened
Party stand outside Sheylah's Cabin
Ruvik Knocks on the door
Sheylah opens the door and gets confused as she looks over Ruvik shoulder at Johnny
Sheylah invites them in and offers them tea
After introducing themselves to Sheylah the party head upstairs to meet "H" the travelling merchant.
Leaving the shop the party see "lil" Johnny holding the lute Sheylah made him.
While everyone went back downstairs Johnny stayed behind and played one last song on the lute.
Ruvik speaks to Sheylah about the holy symbol above her front door.
Ruvik finds out it belongs to [[Avery]]/[[Artemis (The Mistress of the Wilderness)|Artemis]].
Johnny and Daggs have a heart-to-heart.
Sheylah informs the party of the [[Guspacho]] a racist group hunting down Sheylah.
The party begins to travel towards the town of Thorpes.
In the town the party head to the guild hall to try find out more information.
While there they realise that the [[Guspacho]] are running the guild hall.
A fight breaks out leading to Johnny shooting the leader of the group in the head and tapping into the powers within his mark.
[[Guspacho]] is wiped out leading to the towns freedom and Sheylah's safety.
