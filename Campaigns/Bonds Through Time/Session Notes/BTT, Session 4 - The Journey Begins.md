### What happened
On the way to Glaarwater the Party come across a broken down carriage at the side of the road.
While investigating the carriage a man walks up to them claiming to be the one who owns the carriage and its contents.
after a brief discussion the party agree to escort the man to his shop in Glaarwater.
Once there the party head out to the back of the shop where they find a hidden door leading down into the shops basement.
After heading down into the basement they find a that the shop is run by bandits and is run by Hugh Jackman, the merchant they met but the damaged carriage.
While exploring Daggs finds a letter that is written in code.
The party head through the hideout taking out the bandits as they're going.
After Hugh is killed a golden light engulfs him and he disappears.
The party then go around the hideout freeing the trapped people.
Back upstairs Daggs ransacks the shop as Hugh no longer needs it.
The party leave their horses at the shop then head to the guildhall where they meet the guild master Navis Quartzcrown.
After finishing their business the party head back to the shop to rest for the night.
