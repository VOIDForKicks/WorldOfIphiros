### What happened
The Party arrives at The capital Oxengrad were the annual fighting festival is about to begin.
The party head to the guild hall were they ask about the festival.
- up to 3 members per team.
- tournament brackets arranged based on average ranking of participants.
Daggs faints twice after hearing the prize amount 100PP.
