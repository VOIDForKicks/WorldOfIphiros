### What happened
The party head down to the port where a rift has opened.
Daggs and Johnny step through the portal and find themselves on "[[The Retribution]]".
While travelling they come across another ship that is under attack.
During the fight Ruvik comes flying through a rift and immediately joins the battle
The party are unable to save the entire crew but manage to save Gustov and Carlos and take them onboard "The Retribution".
The two pirates join the party on there journey.
After looking at the Daggs' map the party decide to head to the orange dot on the map beginning a 4 day journey.
On the 2nd day they are attacked and manage to fend them off but the top deck sustains damage in the battle.
Ruvik manages to take a harpy in and calls it Desmerelda.
