### What happened
The following day the party head back to the guild hall where they speak to Navis once more.
While speaking to Navis, Daggs hands her the letter he found and asks her if she knows anyone who can translate it.
Going through the letter Navis spots 3 repeating names "[[Xeno]]", "[[Hugh Jackman|Hugh]]" and [[Gerald O' Leary|"Daggs Stormcloak"]].
Johnny and Ruvik think the Daggs Stormcloak mentioned in the letter is a descendent of the Daggs with them.
During their conversation Navis askes them if they would be willing to get rid of the bandits from the town entirely.
The party accept the quest and head off to investigate the area to find their hideout.
While search for the bandits main hideout the party head towards the church to the north of the town as Ruvik wants to take it in the name of [[Asmodeus (The Lord of the Ninth)]].
At the church Johnny discovers a trapdoor hidden under a stack of boxes in the priests prep room.
Moving through the hideout the party take out a lot of the bandits before barricading themselves in a room to rest.
