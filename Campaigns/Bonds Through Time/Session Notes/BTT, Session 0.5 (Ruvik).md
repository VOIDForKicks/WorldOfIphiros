### What happened
Ruvik Lorvein stands on the battle field that'll end the war he has been fighting. The only thing standing in his way are 2 kobolds, 2 goblins and a hobgoblin.
By his side is his elder son Tital Lorvein.
After a fierce battle Ruvik collapses from exhaustion.
Waking up in his home Ruvik now has the energy to wander the halls
After eating with his family (Estara (wife), Tital, Jerr Lorvein(youngest son) and Garos Thornton Ruvik heads back to bed.
The next morning Ruvik is unable to get out of his bed.
His family is called together to say goodbye.
He passes.
While being consumed by death the warmth of new life fills Ruvik
