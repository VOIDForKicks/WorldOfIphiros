#### Backstory
Born into the lush boughs of an influential and wealthy family, the young fairy lived a life gilded in splendour and political intrigue. His kin were respected leaders, their voices carrying weight among the courts and councils of their homeland. Yet power breeds enemies, and in the shadows of their influence stirred a tyrant, a cruel and cunning warlord who sought to bend the region to his will. Accusations, bribes, and assassins followed, until at last, the tyrant struck with fire and steel.

One night, under a sky choked with smoke, the family estate fell. Guards were overwhelmed, allies turned traitor, and blood ran over polished marble. But in their final moments, the fairy’s father enacted a desperate plan, sealing his child in an enchanted seedpod and sending it soaring across the forest on the wind. It landed in a quiet hamlet far from the capital, into the hands of an old friend, an eccentric hermit with ties to the wild and an ancient, creaking mech hidden beneath the roots of an oak tree.

This guardian raised the child in secret, teaching the forgotten ways of nature and song. From whispered lullabies to druidic rites beneath the moon, the fairy learned to hear the voice of the world and answer it with music and magic. In time, he also learned of his past, of the noble blood that ran in his veins and the evil that had taken everything. But the hermit knew: a name alone would not bring justice. The young one would need strength, cunning, and most of all, companions.
Donning a Hat of Disguise to conceal his true face, the fairy took to the road. Few could imagine that behind the grinning bard's façade stood the last heir of a fallen house. Riding his patched-up mech, a towering machine of bark and brass shaped like a forest guardian, he wandered from village to village, tavern to battlefield, earning coin and favour with songs that soothed the sorrowful and spells that stirred the earth beneath tyrant boots.

As he travelled, he absorbed the world: new languages sung in marketplaces, forgotten magics traded for favours in smoky dens, and secrets passed between leaves on the wind. Every ally he made, every lesson he learned, brought him one step closer to reclaiming his heritage.

And though he plays the fool, the tyrant's fall is ever on his mind. Behind the songs and disguises lies a burning purpose: to return home, rally the people, and bring down the shadow that consumed his family.

The fairy’s legend has only begun. But already, in the corners of the land, rumours stir, of a masked wanderer in a walking forest, whose songs stir rebellion and whose eyes burn with ancient fire.
