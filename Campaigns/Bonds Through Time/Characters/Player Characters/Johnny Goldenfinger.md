#### Backstory:
In the year 2010, a famous bard roamed the land of Abrax. His name was Jonathan Goldenfinger. He bore a distinctive birthmark of a golden middle finger hence the name Golden Finger. One night while Jonathan was walking home after a performance, he was approached by a hooded figure that challenged him to a musical contest. Jonathan refused at first but then the stranger revealed a Golden Lute and offered to place it as a wager. Blinded by the sheer beauty of it Jonathan grabbed the lute out of the stranger's hands to examine it. He then asked what should he offer as a bet, "Your soul . . . ". The stranger pulled his cloak off to reveal himself to be the Devil himself. Jonathan however was not afraid as he believed to be "the best to have ever been". The two locked in an epic musical duel that reverberated across the whole Island. ultimately the Devil dropped down to his knees and admitted his defeat. Johnathan snatched the Golden Lute from the Devil's hands and told him he was welcome to try again in a few years time then turned around and left the Devil alone on the road where he began to plot his revenge.

10 years after Jonathan's wager, he settled down with a wife and had a son he called Johnny. One night however, Jonathan had a visit form an old "friend". The Devil returned to challenge Jonathan to yet another duel for the Golden lute which had been gathering dust for the past few years. Jonathan, still proud and confident or maybe cocky, accepted the Devil's challenge. He went to fetch the Golden Lute and stepped outside to to play the Devil once more. The two of them just like before, locked in an epic musical duel which was heard all over the island. This time however, while Jonathan was distracted, some demons snuck into his home and killed his wife and kidnapped his son who bore the exact same birthmark as his father. After Jonathan beat the Devil once more, Jonathan turned around to find his home ablaze, the Devil then proclaimed that no one beats him before vanishing. Jonathan dropped the lute and ran into the fire in search of his family but all he found was his wife's dead body with a message carved on her chest "he is my son now". Jonathan ran out of the crumbling house holding his wife and collapsed to his knees a few yards out front. Filled with grief and anger Jonathan's mind was racing before going completely silent once he set his eyes upon the Golden Lute. Jonathan picked it up and instinctively knew the exact notes to play despite not knowing them. A hellish fire engulfed the Lute before summoning a demonic crack of thunder which opened a portal leading to Hell. Jonathan went through the portal with one goal on his mind, get his son back. The following morning a local Elven Huntress by the name of Sheylah came across the charred ruins of Jonathan's home and among them found baby Johnny embraced by his dead mother along with a mysterious Golden Lute lying beside them. The Huntress organized a search party for her old friend Jonathan. After weeks of searching, The Huntress concluded that Jonathan must have burned to ash along with his home trying to save his family. Sheylah decided to adopt Johnny and raise him as her own. She also sold the mysterious lute to a traveling merchant to have enough money to raise him.

Just like his father before him, Johnny showed a great interest in music which is why Sheylah whittled out a makeshift lute out of some old oak. The lute was built up to absolutely no professional standards and sounded like a drowning horse but Johnny was able to play it masterfully nevertheless. When Johnny was about 16 years old his adopted mother fell victim to racist hate crimes and was murdered in their own home. Johnny found her in the evening when he came home. His agonizing shriek of grief was heard in the whole village with crowds of people gathering around. Some of Sheylah's friends organized a hunter's funeral pyre for her. At the funeral Johnny placed the lute his mother made for him atop the pyre and watched it burn all night long. When only ash remained, Johnny went off to track down the animals that took away his family from him. After following them home one night, Johnny broke in and slaughtered them all with a frying pan before disappearing into the night.

Johnny then walked aimlessly ahead for days before reaching Abrax's capital. There Johnny collapsed in the gutter and passed out. He was found the following morning by some gangsters who took pity on him and took him in. Johnny was then inducted into the Oxes street gang. For the next two years Johnny became an alcoholic and a drug addict while doing a lot of dirty work for the gang which became his new family of sorts. One job however went sideways and Johnny was picked by the city guards. The court decided to punish Johnny by conscripting him into the military where he would be forced to serve abroad. Johnny served for the 10 years before being dishonourably discharged for openly defying his superiors. Johnny then returned to Abrax's capital. While strolling around he came across a tavern in which he heard someone's performance and was reminded of his love for music. After the performance Johnny chatted up the bard who revealed himself to be Kenny Dollardyne and the two stayed up all night talking. The following morning they decided to start performing together. For the next year the two performed all over the island becoming brothers. Eventually they were joined by an elf Judy Vespa and a gnome Barty McFoap. Together they formed a band called "Crusaders". They performed for years and travelled across the Island in tour. However just like any other band they became addicted to different things. Drugs took hold of Judy, Barty racked up an unpayable debt with Abraxes most dangerous loan shark and Johnny once more took to the bottle with only Kenny staying sober. One drunken night Kenny and Johnny got into a heated argument over the future of the band. Furniture went flying, bottles were shattered and punches thrown. Kenny wanted to help Johnny and the others but Johnny was too ignorant and self centred. That night the Crusaders broke apart. Kenny left Abrax, Barty and Judy disappeared and Johnny started to perform in the streets alone to make enough coin to afford the next bottle, until one night it all changed . . .
