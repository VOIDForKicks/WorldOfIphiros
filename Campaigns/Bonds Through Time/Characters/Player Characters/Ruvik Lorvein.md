#### Backstory:
Born 11XX-13XX.
Unskilled as an artisan, Lorvein focused his efforts on worship to the god his tribe once followed. 
Lorveins tribe Integrated into a human, elf society for protection against another growing hostile tribe that pushed them out of their original settlement, in turn for them crafting equipment for the war.
Lorvein participated on the battlefield instead, slowly rising through the ranks to become a nobleman, gifted leadership over land of his own through 200+ years of service. 
Lorvein began a family, with 5 children who he would go on to lead in multiple battles after pushing them down a similar path to his own, despising the ways of his fellow contemporaries, despite this contempt, after their deaths, Lorvein would became more apathetic towards life, having missed the opportunity to fully gain their respect.
On his death bed, Lorvein is filled by a warm embrace, closing his eyes and opening them again, he finds himself to be some place new, with a rejuvenated sense of life and a tattoo of a mace with fiery wings stretched out behind it.
