Tital was the first son of the family and was thus raised with the most enthusiasm, Ruvik spent much time training him in battle, strategy and the families religious practices. Tital would also spend time in the company of other Noblemen studying culture, history and language. When he was ready he would join his father's army and eventually rise to become a knight himself through protecting the united settlements from invaders. In his early years during times of peace Tital would spent his leisure time in the local town with other rock gnomes tinkering on different projects to improve the living standards of the towns people.
