Once Travelled the Archipelago hunting things and saving people with her older sister. After decades of protecting people from countless monsters, on one hunt her sister was killed by a lich. Narrowly escaping death herself Sheylah fell into a great depression and ended up as a local hunter in a small village. After years of not being able to cope with the loss of her sister, Sheylah went to the woods to take her own life. However just before she end it all she heard a baby crying in his dead mother's arms with his father nowhere to be found. At that moment Shelyah discovered a new reason to keep living.
