This elf has lived in [[Oxengrad]] slums her whole life barely making it by stealing food. Became a cat burglar and ran with a small crew of thieves before getting literally stabbed in the back by her partner Torin. Has not trusted anyone since until she met and joined the Crusaders playing her mother's harp. She Developed a special bond with Barty but before anything happened the band broke up. When everyone went their separate ways she ended up back in the slums. There she stumbled upon her old partner Torin who had become the leader of the biggest gang in Oxengrad The Crimson Moon. As Judy was in a bad place in her life she ended up getting back with her ex in a haze of drugs and alcohol. By default Judy also became a member of the gang and eventually took over as the leader when Torin was killed in a gang war.
