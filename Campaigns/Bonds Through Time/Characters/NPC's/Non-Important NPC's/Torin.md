Grew up in the Slums. Was part of a small group of thieves with Judy. Decided to evolve the group into a gang but backstabbed Judy on order to assume full control. As years went by the gang grew in power and became one of the largest in Oxengrad. One day some scouts brought over a young boy they had found in a gutter. She took a liking to this particular stray. She fed him, clothed him, and made him her personal bard. She often sent him off on jobs with the rest of her goons doing some grunt work at first but the older he got the dirtier the jobs got until one day a job went sideways and the kid got caught and sent away by the guards. Some decades later Judy ended up back in Torin's life. She found her all alone, drunk, high and homeless. She took pleasure seeing her like this so she took her in as her sort of "plaything". However, a gang war broke out and Torin was killed after her lair was infiltrated. The gang became lost without its leader but Juby sobered up and yanked the reigns and became the new leader of the gang.
