**Who made the marks**
You look at the world and you see the gods of the modern tongue, Greek, Celtic, Faerûnian and Japanese. They are real, yes. They hold sway over the empires of today. But they are newcomers to Iphiros. Before their names were ever whispered, there was a war that broke reality itself. The gods you serve now built their temples on the ruins of a **Shattered Realm**. The entities who wore these seven powers did not just rule the world... they were the world.

Your marks were not stamped by a divine hand from across the sea; they are keys forged right here, beneath the soil of Iphiros. They are not brands of servitude, they are anchors. They keep your souls tethered to this reality while you drift through the currents of time.

The seven are the anchors holding the world together. If even one anchor slips... the madness bleeds in.

**The Final Clue**
You have met the entity Xeno. You have seen the shadow he casts. He wields the power of the never-ending cycle because Ouroboros chose him to be his hand upon the world. But a cycle cannot exist with only one side, my friends. A coin must have a front and a back. Xeno is only half of the loop. The other half is out there, waiting, walking a path shrouded in absolute secrecy. Do not look too hard for the other side of the wheel... when the time is right, the circle will close itself.
