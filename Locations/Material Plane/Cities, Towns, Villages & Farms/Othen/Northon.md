#### **Type:** 
Large Town
#### **Elevator Pitch:** 
A rugged northern settlement on the continent of Othen renowned for its stone quarries, ancient family estates, and deep ties to historical military veterans.
#### **History**
##### Founded By & Why: 
Established as a northern inland settlement by pioneer warrior clans and stone-working families seeking to extract mineral and quarry resources from Othen.
##### Major Past Event: 
The gathering of the Lorvein family following the passing of Ruvik Lorvein, culminating in a fierce nocturnal ghoul attack on the estate.
##### The Town's Secret: 
Ancient temples hidden within the region house sacred relics like the Doss Lute, which was successfully retrieved by infiltration.
##### Historical Figure: 
Ruvik Lorvein, a veteran warrior and family patriarch whose legacy and passing are still widely discussed by locals and kin.
#### **Population:** 
2380
#### **Ruler/Leadership:** 
Regional Noble Council / The Lorvein Family Estate
#### **Party Reputation:**
[[Chrono Corsairs]]: 0 (Neutral standing, respected for honouring Ruvik's memory though viewed with caution due to clandestine temple infiltrations)
#### **Region:**
Northern continent of Iphiros (Othen), positioned inland near northern mountain ranges and quarry sites.
#### **Climate & Atmosphere:**
Bitter northern chill, heavy mountain air smelling of pine and damp stone, and the distant echoes of quarry work.
#### **Nearby Locations of Interest:**
[[Shipton]] (to the southwest), [[Redwater]] (to the east), and local stone quarries.
#### **Notable NPCs**
[[Garos Thornton Junior]], [Noble / Heir]: The son of Garos Thornton who visited the Lorvein estate prior to the outbreak of the undead attacks.

[[Tital Lorvein]], [Family Heir / Ally]: A member of the Lorvein household who assisted and interacted with the party during their stay at the family estate.
#### **Factions & Groups**
The Lorvein Clan: An ancient noble and military lineage holding deep roots in Northon's history and estate defence.

Quarry Guild: Industrial workers and traders managing the region's stone and material exports.
#### **Buildings & Locations in Town** 
The Landmark: The Ancient Temple of Northon, a grand holy structure housing sacred artifacts like the Doss Lute.

The Tavern/Inn: The Stonecutter’s Rest, a heavy timber inn serving hot stews and strong spirits to weary miners and travelers.

The Market/Shops: The Northon Trade Bazaar, where regional stone, tools, and basic adventuring equipment are bought and sold.

The Sketchy Corner: The Lorvein Estate Outskirts, a shadowy, frost-bitten perimeter heavily targeted by roaming ghouls, ghasts, and restless undead.
