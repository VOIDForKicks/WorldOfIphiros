A bustling southwestern coastal port town on the continent of Othen that hosted emergency municipal meetings regarding Susanoo and witnessed explosive dockside mishaps.
#### **History**
##### Founded By & Why: 
Established as a southwestern maritime port on Othen to facilitate trade and travel across the western sea lanes.

The emergency town meeting held by the mayor regarding Susanoo taking over a nearby temple, alongside the dockside explosion caused by Johnny's ticking barrel.

The nearby woods and regional trails conceal routes toward divine sanctuaries and temples taken over by descending planar entities.

Southwestern coast of the continent of Othen, positioned west of Redwater and across the ocean from Abrax.

Chilly northern sea breeze, the scent of burning wood and salt, and the echoing sounds of dock repair crews putting out fires.
#### **Nearby Locations of Interest:**
[[Redwater]] (to the east) and Susanoo's Temple.
#### **Notable NPCs**
The Mayor of Shipton, [Ruler]: The town leader who convened the emergency public meeting to discuss Susanoo's arrival and the takeover of the nearby temple.

Port Hand Foreman, [Worker]: A local dock worker who interacted with Daggs regarding the repair of the ship and the damage caused by the barrel explosion.
#### **Factions & Groups**
Shipton Town Council: The local governing body managing civic defense and responses to divine disruptions.

Dockworkers & Shipwrights Guild: Skilled laborers operating the port, repairing vessels, and dealing with unexpected dock fires and explosions.
#### **Buildings & Locations in Town** 
The Landmark: The Shipton Town Hall, a grand municipal building where emergency public meetings and town assemblies are held.

The Tavern/Inn: The Seafarer's Rest, a lively local tavern where sailors gather over warm ale to gossip about temple takeovers and harbour blasts.

The Market/Shops: The Ironport Blacksmith & Ship Repair Yard, where adventurers go to upgrade gear, repair equipment, and fix specialized items like Daggs' pistol ship key.

The Sketchy Corner: The Smoking Harbour Docks, the blast-scarred pier where Johnny's timer-detonated barrel blew a massive hole near the water.
