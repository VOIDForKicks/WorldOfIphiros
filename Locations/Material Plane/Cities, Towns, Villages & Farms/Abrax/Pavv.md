#### **Type:** 
Village
#### **Elevator Pitch:** 
A remote mountain settlement in central-southern Abrax harbouring assassin secrets and a cavern housing a young golden dragon protector.
#### **History**
##### Founded By & Why: 
Established as a high-altitude mountain settlement taking advantage of the rugged peaks and surrounding mineral-rich terrain.
##### Major Past Event: 
The rescue of Hadarai Agosto from mind-controlling assassins, the negotiation with the young golden dragon Panot to protect the town, and the restoration of Ruvik's holy symbol via Pharaura's ritual.
##### The Town's Secret: 
The local village fountain and nearby dragon caves conceal secret pathways and temporary gateways leading directly into the Plane of Fire.
##### Historical Figure: 
(One famous dead person everyone in town still talks about or has a statue of.)
#### **Population:** 
454
#### **Ruler/Leadership:** 
Mayor Lorion and Guild Master Hacklash.
#### **Party Reputation:**
[[Chrono Corsairs]]: 3 (Recognized for dealing with assassins, brokering an agreement with Panot, and handling local threats).
#### **Region:**
Mountainous region located in central-southern Abrax, nestled among rugged peaks.
#### **Climate & Atmosphere:**
Crisp, thin mountain air smelling of pine and stone, featuring cold winds and sweeping views of high-altitude rock faces.
#### **Nearby Locations of Interest:**
Panot's Cavern, the village fountain, and nearby mountain passes leading toward Oxengrad and Helmfirth.
#### **Notable NPCs**
[[Panot (The Bronze Wing)|Panot]], [Dragon]: A 17-year-old young golden dragon who protects the town of Pavv in exchange for a renovated den and fair compensation.

[[Hacklash]], [Guild Master]: A local guild leader operating in Pavv who interacts with the Chrono Corsairs in thieves' cant before traveling to Oxengrad.

[[Lorion]], [Mayor]: The mayor of Pavv who provides local rumours, warnings about missing royals, and administrative oversight.
#### **Factions & Groups**
Thieves' Guild / Underworld Contacts: Local operatives operating in the shadows of the mountain village who use thieves' cant and monitor regional movements.

Village Guard & Citizens: Mountain folk trying to keep the settlement secure amidst looming threats from assassins and missing royals.
#### **Buildings & Locations in Town** 
The Landmark: The Village Fountain, a central stone landmark connected to ancient riddles and planar shifts.

The Tavern/Inn: The Frost Peak Tavern, a cosy, heated mountain drinking house where travellers gather over warm stews to exchange rumours about capital events.

The Market/Shops: The Mountain Brewery & Magic Goods Emporium, where villagers and adventurers buy potions, brewing supplies, and protective gear.

The Sketchy Corner: The Shrouded Mountain Cave Outskirts, where assassin groups previously laid low and stalked targets before being rooted out by the party.
