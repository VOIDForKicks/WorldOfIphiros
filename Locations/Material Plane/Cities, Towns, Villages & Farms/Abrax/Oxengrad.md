Large City
#### **Elevator Pitch:** 
A sprawling central capital city hosting annual fighting festivals, high-stakes tournaments, and gatherings of royalty.

Established as the central governing capital and defensive stronghold of the region.

Kings and queens from neighbouring nations gather in the capital ahead of major political shifts and missing royal crises.

Central inland plains and river valleys of Abrax.
#### **Climate & Atmosphere:**
Bustling, high-energy metropolitan atmosphere filled with cheering arena crowds, clanging Armor, and the murmurs of bookies and gamblers.
#### **Nearby Locations of Interest:**
The central river networks and trade routes connecting to [[Astrakhan]], [[Windrip]], and [[Helmfirth]].
#### **Notable NPCs**
[[Alucard]], [Tournament Competitor / Threat]: A powerful combatant in the tournament final who called upon Keldon for strength and possessed a hidden phylactery.
#### **Factions & Groups**
The City Guard: Law enforcement officers who patrol the streets, police street performers, and target individuals like Johnny and Barty McFoap.

Tournament Bookies & Syndicate: Financial organizers managing heavy bets, odds, and pay-outs for the grand fighting festival.
#### **Buildings & Locations in Town** 
The Landmark: The Grand Oxengrad Colosseum and Arena, where massive annual fighting tournaments and high-stakes bouts take place.

The Tavern/Inn: The Golden Goblet Inn, a lively, crowded tavern where gamblers gather to hear rumours about missing royals and where Johnny charmed the barkeep for free drinks.

The Market/Shops: The Capital Blacksmith & Equipment Exchange, where teams upgrade their gear, purchase protective Armor, and prepare for tournament bouts.

The Sketchy Corner: The Back-Alley Bookie Dens, where high-risk wagers are placed and shady underworld contacts trade secrets away from the patrolling city guards.
