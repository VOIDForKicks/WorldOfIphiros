#### **Type:** 
Large Town
#### **Elevator Pitch:** 
A bustling coastal town on the southwestern shore of Abrax that hides deep criminal networks beneath its quiet streets and shops.
#### **History**
##### Founded By & Why: 
Established as a strategic southern port and trade outpost due to its direct access to the sea.
##### Major Past Event: 
The coordinated sweep and eradication of an underground bandit syndicate operating out of local shops and the northern church.
##### The Town's Secret: 
Multiple local establishments, including Hugh Jackman's general store and the church to the north, were serving as connected fronts for underground criminal operations.
##### Historical Figure: 
(One famous dead person everyone in town still talks about or has a statue of.)
#### **Population:** 
3786
#### **Ruler/Leadership:** 
Guild master / Town leadership (navigated alongside Guild Master Navis Quartzcrown)
#### **Party Reputation:**
[[Chrono Corsairs]]: 5 (Trusted by guild leadership after clearing out local bandit elements and helping secure the town)
#### **Region:**
Southwestern coast of the main continent of Abrax, situated south of dense forests and west of Clayrest.
#### **Climate & Atmosphere:**
Breezy coastal climate with salty sea winds, temperate air, and the constant hum of harbour activity.
#### **Nearby Locations of Interest:**
[[Freyforest]] (to the northwest), [[Clayrest]] (to the east across the southern bay), [[Erast]] (to the north).
#### **Notable NPCs**
[[Navis Quartzcrown]], [Guild Master]: The guild master of Glaarwater's adventurer branch who coordinates contracts, aids in translating coded letters, and enlists party help to handle town threats.

[[Hugh Jackman]], [Merchant / Bandit Leader]: A former local merchant operating a general shop fronting a hidden basement hideout, secretly running bandit operations before being defeated by the party.
#### **Factions & Groups**
The Guild: The local adventurer branch providing contracts, regional coordination, and information processing.

Bandit Network: An underground criminal ring utilizing hidden basements beneath shops and church backrooms as hideouts before being dismantled.
#### **Buildings & Locations in Town** 
The Landmark: The Grand Adventurers Guild Hall, a prominent structure where contracts, bounty rewards, and regional reports are processed.

The Tavern/Inn: The Salty Anchor Tavern, a popular coastal drinking spot where harbor hands and travelers gather, offering cold ales and local fish stew.

The Market/Shops: Hugh Jackman’s Shop (formerly a general goods store fronting a hidden bandit basement filled with trapped citizens and stolen goods).

The Sketchy Corner: The Church to the North, featuring a hidden trapdoor under a stack of boxes in the priest's prep room leading into a major bandit stronghold.
