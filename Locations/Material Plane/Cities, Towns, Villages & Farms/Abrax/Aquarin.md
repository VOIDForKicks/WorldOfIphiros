#### **Type:** 
Hamlet
#### **Elevator Pitch:** 
A secluded coastal hamlet located off the south-eastern coast of Abrax. Completely cut off from mainland roads, its people survive entirely on deep-sea fishing and utilize the neighbouring southern island to cultivate crops. Life here moves to the rhythm of the tides, wind, and salt.
#### **History**
##### Founded By & Why: 
Founded over a century ago by a crew of shipwrecked sailors and political exiles from the mainland city of Wheldrake who realized the isolated southern waters were rich with rare, valuable deep-water fish and safe from mainland taxation.
##### Major Past Event: 
An unseasonably brutal winter froze the shallow channel between Aquarin and its agricultural island for a full month, cutting off food supplies and leading to a desperate famine that nearly wiped out the settlement.
##### The Town's Secret: 
The southern farming island isn't just used for crops; deep beneath its soil lies a sunken, half-buried pre-cataclysmic ruin that the founders accidentally uncovered. They deliberately kept it hidden and sealed off because the strange artefacts found there seemed to attract unnatural marine predators.
##### Historical Figure: 
_Captain Marisa Voss_ - The legendary first harbourmaster who successfully navigated the settlement through its first decade without a single loss at sea. A weathered bronze statue of her holding a steering oar stands at the edge of the main dock, looking out toward the horizon.

#### **Ruler/Leadership:** 
- **Leadership:** Governed by the _Harbour Council_, a three-person elected body consisting of the master fisher, the chief agriculturalist of the southern island, and a rotating citizen representative.
- **Style:** Generally fair and community-driven, as isolation breeds interdependence. However, they can be fiercely protective and deeply distrustful of mainland authorities.

#### **Region:**
Southern coast of Abrax - isolated sea cliffs, rocky shores, roaring surf, and a secondary fertile volcanic island just a short boat ride to the south.
#### **Climate & Atmosphere:**
- **Weather:** Crisp, breezy, and maritime. High humidity, heavy morning fogs rolling off the ocean, and frequent salt spray.
- **Sensory Details:** The sharp, briny smell of drying fish and rotting kelp mixed with wood smoke; the constant, rhythmic crashing of waves against the cliffs; the shrill cries of gulls wheeling overhead.
#### **Nearby Locations of Interest:**
- **The Southern Farm Island:** A smaller, sheltered island featuring terraced crop fields and greenhouses protected from the harsh sea winds.
- **Misty Grove Gardens:** The closest mainland landmark to the north-west.
- **Mermon:** The closest major mainland settlement across the bay.
#### **Notable NPCs**
- **Barnaby Saltwood, Master Fisher & Council Head:** A grizzled man with skin like tanned leather and a thick, white beard. Blunt-spoken and pragmatic, he cares more about the daily catch and net-mending than mainland politics.

- **Talia Vane, Botanist & Island Caretaker:** A sharp-eyed woman who spends most of her time managing the crops on the southern island. She is obsessed with strange root mutations caused by the island's mysterious soil and is secretly looking for brave souls to help her investigate the sealed ruins beneath the farmland.
#### **Factions & Groups**
- **The Net-Makers Guild:** An informal coalition of elders and weavers who control the production of specialized deep-sea nets. They hold immense social sway over who gets to fish the richest currents.

- **The Salt-Tide Brotherhood:** A loose brotherhood of younger sailors who manage the cargo boats running between the main settlement and the farm island. They are adventurous, restless, and occasionally tempted by smuggling gigs.
#### **Buildings & Locations in Town** 
- **The Landmark:** _The Anchor Shrine_ - A massive, rusted iron anchor salvaged from the settlement's very first wreck, planted upright in the town square as a monument to survival and unity.
- **The Tavern/Inn:** _The Gilded Shell_ - A cozy, timber-built inn that smells perpetually of clam chowder and stale ale. **Local Rumour:** Someone pulled a glowing, unrusting brass gear out of the fishnets last Tuesday, and a stranger from the mainland has been offering a small fortune to buy it.
- **The Market/Shops:** _The Pier-Head Bazaar_ - A collection of open-air stalls built along the wooden docks where fresh catch, salted meats, and island-grown root vegetables are traded. Rare items include deep-sea pearls and waterproof seal-hide garments.
- **The Sketchy Corner:** _The Sea-Cave Tunnels_ - A series of natural sea-level caves beneath the main cliffside dwellings where contraband is occasionally stashed away from the prying eyes of the council.
