#### **Type:** 
Small Town
#### **Elevator Pitch:** 
A quiet north-eastern settlement celebrated globally as the sole sanctuary of master artificers, though it harbours hidden infernal cult temples and was recently freed from oppressive faction rule.
#### **History**
##### Founded By & Why: 
Established as an inland trading and farming community in the north-eastern region of the continent by early pioneers who discovered unique arcane-mechanical crafting secrets.
##### Major Past Event: 
The liberation of the town from the oppressive Guspacho group, followed by the infiltration and subsequent explosion/collapse of the hidden temple to the Nine Hells.
##### The Town's Secret: 
Thorpes is the only place in the world where true artificers exist, keeping their arcane-mechanical innovations strictly guarded while a terrorist group secretly operate out of the local Guildhall.

(One famous dead person everyone in town still talks about or has a statue of.)

#### **Region:**
North-eastern inland region of Abrax, situated near dense woodlands and northern mountain borders
#### **Climate & Atmosphere:**
Cool, crisp regional air filled with the faint scent of ozone, polished brass, and the rhythmic ticking of clockwork machinery from local artificer workshops.
#### **Nearby Locations of Interest:**
[[Sheylah's Cabin]] (nearby woodland retreat), [[Silverkeep]] (to the northeast).
#### **Notable NPCs**
[[Izzamis]], [Bone Devil / Cult Leader]: Asmodeus's right-hand man who oversaw the hidden infernal temple beneath the town before it was brought down.
#### **Factions & Groups**
The Artificer Guild of Thorpes: The exclusive collective of master inventors, tinkers, and enchanters who make Thorpes the only town in the world capable of producing artificer magic and machinery.

The Guspacho: A racist group that previously hunted down individuals like Sheylah and ran the local guild hall before being completely wiped out by the party.
#### **Buildings & Locations in Town** 
The Landmark: The Great Artificer Spire, the central workshop tower where the world's only true artificers craft wondrous items and experimental technology.

The Tavern/Inn: The Clockwork & Kettle Inn, a cozy local establishment where visiting travelers and artificer apprentices gather to hear rumors about local governance and rare inventions.

The Market/Shops: The Thorpes Artificer Emporium, the only place globally where specialized magical gizmos, infused gear, and unique artificer components can be purchased.

The Sketchy Corner: The Guspacho-run Guild Hall and the Temple that lead to the Nine Hells.
