#### Elevator Pitch

A mysterious, god-ruled island in the middle of the ocean where visitors risk transformation and dark bargains are struck.
#### Location
[[Asmark Expanse]]
#### The Hook (First Impression)
- Visuals (100 ft away): An isolated, mystical island rising out of the deep sea, dotted with swine roaming the coastline around an enigmatic estate.

- Sensory Details: The heavy smell of roasted feast meats and ozone, punctuated by the unsettling, human-like squeals of pigs.

- The Atmosphere: Bewitching, Perilous, Tense.
#### Core Identity
- Category: Religious Site

- Current Status: Occupied by [[Circe (The Dread Goddess)]]

- The "Why": The party was driven here by a dot on Daggs' map during their sea journey, ultimately needing Circe's magic and guidance to track down Gerald O'Leary.
### Exploration & Gameplay
#### Key Areas (Pick 3–4)
- Circe's House: The central estate where the Dread Goddess holds court, receives offerings, and checks visitors for lingering enchantments or curses.

- The Swine Grounds: The surrounding island grounds populated by transformed individuals and livestock, where Gerald's mutinous crew slaughtered pigs and sparked a battle.

- The Shoreline Anchorage: The beachhead where ships like the _Scuzzled Paddy_ and _The Retribution_ make landfall before embarking toward places like Liltarbrook.
#### Threats & Complications
##### Hazards/Traps:
Polymorphing divine magic that threatens to transform trespassers into swine upon displeasing the goddess.
##### Denizens:
- 🔴 _Hostile:_ Mutinous crew members under mind control (Gerald O'Leary, Gregory, Terrance)
- 🟡 _Neutral/Negotiable:_ [[Circe (The Dread Goddess)]]
- 🟢 _Friendly/Allies:_ Aidan, Avery, Gustov, Carlos
#### Secrets & Rewards
##### The Twist/Hidden Discovery:
Gerald O'Leary was actively controlling members of Daggs' former crew until the party intervened and killed Gregory and Terrance to free Aidan and Avery.
##### Loot & Lore:
Reclaiming key crew members (Aidan and Avery) and acquiring the knowledge that a proper offering was required to track down Gerald via Susanoo.
### DM Note / Regional Rule (Optional):
**Transmutation Curse:** Guests who anger or fail to strike a proper deal with the island's divine ruler risk being partially or fully polymorphed into pigs.
