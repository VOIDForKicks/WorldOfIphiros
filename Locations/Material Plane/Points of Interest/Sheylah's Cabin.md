#### Elevator Pitch 
A secluded, peaceful woodland retreat near Thorpes belonging to the hunter Sheylah, targeted by a hostile faction due to its secret connections to divine entities.
#### Location
[[Thorpes]] (Northeastern Abrax)
#### The Hook (First Impression)
- Visuals (100 ft away): A cosy, rustic wooden cabin nestled near the forest edge with a prominent holy symbol crafted above the front entryway.

- Sensory Details: The comforting aroma of warm brewed tea mixing with fresh forest pine, accompanied by the gentle strumming of a lute.

- The Atmosphere: Warm, Sanctuary, Vulnerable
#### Core Identity
- Category: Private Residence / Sacred Refuge

- Current Status: Occupied by Sheylah (Safe following the defeat of the Guspacho)

- The "Why": The party travelled here against their will but ultimately defend Sheylah from a ruthless local hate group.
### Exploration & Gameplay

#### Key Areas (Pick 3–4)
- Sheylah's Tea Room: The welcoming ground-floor living area where guests are received and offered hot tea.

- The Front Threshold: The main entry marked by a holy symbol dedicated to Avery/Artemis, offering spiritual significance and subtle protection.
#### Threats & Complications
##### Hazards/Traps: 
Vulnerability to regional hate groups hunting down the owner; nearby forest travel harbours hostile ambushes.
##### Denizens:
- 🔴 _Hostile:_ The Guspacho (a racist group hunting Sheylah, later eliminated by the party)

- 🟡 _Neutral/Negotiable:_ [[Karios (The Chrono-Sentinel)|Mystery Merchant]] ("H")

- 🟢 _Friendly:_ [[Sheylah]], "Lil" Johnny
#### Secrets & Rewards
##### The Twist/Hidden Discovery: 
The holy symbol mounted over the cabin's front door belongs directly to [[Avery]] / [[Artemis (The Mistress of the Wilderness)]].
##### Loot & Lore: 
Johnny's custom lute made by Sheylah and critical intelligence regarding the Guspacho's oppressive presence in the region.
### DM Note / Regional Rule (Optional):
**Haven of Solace:** Standard long rests taken within the cabin restore full health and remove one level of exhaustion due to its warm, protective atmosphere.
