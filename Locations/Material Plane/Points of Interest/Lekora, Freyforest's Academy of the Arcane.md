#### Elevator Pitch 
A temporal-shifting arcane academy that was once reduced to ruins, now standing restored after events in the timeline were rewritten.
#### Location
[[Freyforest]]
#### The Hook (First Impression)
- Visuals (100 ft away): A grand, functioning wizarding academy sitting adjacent to ancient tower ruins and an overgrown riverbed.

- Sensory Details: The faint humming of practiced arcane spells, the smell of fresh ink and ozone, and a subtle shimmering in the surrounding air.

- The Atmosphere: Historic, Mystical, Restored
#### Core Identity
- Category: Arcane Academy

- Current Status: Formerly Abandoned Ruin; currently Active & Occupied by students and mages

- The "Why": The party originally explored its ruined basement to uncover warnings about Garos Thornton, later returning to search for clues regarding Hadarai Agosto's disappearance.
### Exploration & Gameplay

#### Key Areas (Pick 3–4)
- Tower Ruins & Riverbank: The outer grounds where the party originally woke up with mysterious tattoos and encountered wolves near the pond.

- The Basement Dungeon: A dangerous subterranean complex beneath a trapdoor in the ruins where undead creatures and hidden rooms reside.

- The Restored Halls: The main academic building filled with students relaxing and studying magic after time was rewritten to prevent its fall.
#### Threats & Complications
##### Hazards/Traps: 
Hidden dungeon trapdoors and magical security locks on administrative offices.
