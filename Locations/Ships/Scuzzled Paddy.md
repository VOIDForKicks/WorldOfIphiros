#### **Type:**
Sailing Ship / Brigantine
#### **Elevator Pitch:**
A swift, multi-deck sailing vessel utilized by the Chrono Corsairs for quick transport, naval movement, and coastal operations.
#### **History**
##### Built By & Where:
Crafted in the coastal shipyards of Abrax.
##### Major Past Event:
Sailed the sea lanes between Circe's Island and Liltarbrook, transporting rescued crew members and contraband across the Asmark Expanse.
##### The Ship's Secret:
The lower cargo hold conceals hidden smuggling spaces tucked behind rowboat mounts and cargo crates.
##### Famous Captain:
[[Daggs Stormcloak]]
#### **Vessel Stats & Specs:**
- **Hull / Size:** Three-deck wood-hulled vessel featuring a Top Deck, Main Deck (living quarters), and a spacious lower Cargo Hold.
- **Speed & Propulsion:** Twin-mast design for high-speed coastal sailing and open-water manoeuvres.
- **Armament:** Deck-mounted swivel points and personal arms stored in quarters.
#### **Crew & Leadership:**

- **Current Captain:** Daggs Stormcloak / Chrono Corsairs Command
- **Key Crew Members:** Gustov, Carlos, and recruited pirate hands
#### **Party Reputation / Ownership:**
- **Affiliation:** [[Chrono Corsairs]]
#### **Onboard Locations & Features**
- **The Top Deck:** Open weather deck featuring the forward bowsprit, main hatch, anchor controls, and aft helm station for steering the ship.
- **The Main Deck:** Accommodates officer quarters, captain's desk/study with plush rugs, crew sleeping berths, and dining tables.
- **The Lower Cargo Hold:** Reinforced storage hold housing supplies, barrels, crates, and storage racks for rowboats.
- **The Sketchy Corner:** The forward hold corner beneath the bow, ideal for concealing ticking barrels or high-risk contraband.
