#### **Type:**
Heavy Sailing Ship / Airship
#### **Elevator Pitch:**
A sister vessel of similar dimensions to the _Scuzzled Paddy_, outfitted for heavy naval engagements and party operations across Iphiros.
#### **History**
##### Built By & Where:
Constructed in the shipyards of Masnyae.
##### Major Past Event:
Served as Daggs Stormcloak's primary command vessel during voyages across the Asmark Expanse and regional waters.
##### The Ship's Secret:
Magic runes etched into the hull deck allow it to be unsummoned and recalled to its home harbor at Old Ashton.
##### Famous Captain:
[[Daggs Stormcloak]]
#### **Vessel Stats & Specs:**
- **Hull / Size:** Three-deck timber warship matching the layout of the _Scuzzled Paddy_ (Top Deck, Main Deck quarters, Cargo Hold).
- **Speed & Propulsion:** Twin-masted rigging tuned for both open ocean speed and tactical maneuvering.
- **Armament:** Reinforced broadsides, swivel mountings, and armoury racks.
#### **Crew & Leadership:**
- **Current Captain:** [[Daggs Stormcloak]]
- **Key Crew Members:** Aidan, Avery, and trusted corsair hands
#### **Party Reputation / Ownership:**
- **Affiliation:** [[Chrono Corsairs]] (Primary airship owned and commanded by Daggs)
#### **Onboard Locations & Features**
- **The Top Deck:** Rigged top deck with full lines of sight across the bow, main hatchway, and raised aft helm.
- **The Main Deck:** Features Daggs' captain's quarters with desk, sea charts, luxury rugs, and bunk spaces for primary crew members.
- **The Lower Cargo Hold:** Expansive cargo bay fitted with heavy timber supports, supply barrels, and rowboat storage bay.
- **The Sketchy Corner:** The dark forward bilge beneath the bow deck, used for holding prisoners or secret magical artefacts.
