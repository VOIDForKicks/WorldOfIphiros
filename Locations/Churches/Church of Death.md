#### **Type:**
Distributed Network of Sanctuaries / Temples
#### **Elevator Pitch:**
A collection of sacred churches scattered across the continent of Iphiros dedicated to the solemn management of souls, mortality, and the transition of the afterlife under a tripartite divine pantheon.
#### **History**
##### Founded By & Why:
Established across multiple regions of Iphiros to serve populations dealing with grief, war, mortality, and the delicate balance between life and the beyond.
##### Major Past Event:
The sanctification of local branches, including the notable site established over the former portal ruins in Thorpes following the party's defeat of infernal forces.
##### The Church's Secret:
Certain remote branches conceal forbidden archives and subterranean connection points linking the mortal realm directly to planar underworlds and soul repositories.
##### Patron Deities:
[[Raven Queen (The Matron of Death)]]
[[Asmodeus (The Lord of the Ninth)]]
[[Donn (The Lord of the Dark House)]]

#### **Clergy & Leadership:**
- **High Priest / Leader:** The Council of Death Watchers / Local Regional Prelates
- **Notable Clerics/Paladins:** Specialized death clerics trained to shepherd souls and ward against unholy necromancy.
#### **Party Reputation / Standing:**
- **Affiliation:** [[Chrono Corsairs]] (Deeply respected as allies and founders following their cleansing of corrupt sites and dealings with divine and infernal powers).
#### **Location & Environment:**
- **Region:** Scattered across various settlements and remote domains throughout **Iphiros** (including a prominent branch in [[Thorpes]]).
- **Atmosphere:** Solemn, quiet, and reverent; smelling of burning incense, dried herbs, and cold stone, lit by flickering obsidian-trimmed candles.
#### **Onboard / Interior Locations & Features**
- **The Sanctum / Altar Room:** A tripartite altar space featuring symbols representing the Raven Queen, Asmodeus, and Donn, where funeral rites and soul invocations are performed.
- **The Crypts / Catacombs:** Vast underground burial vaults extending deep beneath the church foundations to house the honored dead.
- **The Reliquary:** A heavily warded vault containing funerary urns, soul tokens, and sacred artefacts linked to the domains of death.
- **The Sketchy Corner:** Deep basement archives or sealed threshold chambers where lingering planar energies or experimental soul-bindings are kept under watch.
