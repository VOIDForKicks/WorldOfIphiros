#### Elevator Pitch 
A lethal infernal defence landmark in Avernus featuring a trapped celestial whose pure energy vaporizes approaching fiends.
#### Location
[[Avernus]]
#### The Hook (First Impression)
- Visuals (100 ft away): A towering, ominous metallic structure topped with a glowing, suspended orb holding a trapped unicorn.

- Sensory Details: The intense smell of fried lingering ozone, a high-pitched humming vibration in the jaw, and sudden bursts of blinding white light.

- The Atmosphere: Volatile, Sacred-Corrupted, Dangerous
#### Core Identity
- Category: Planar Construct / Celestial Trap

- Current Status: Formerly Occupied by Ralzala (Destroyed/Freed by the party)
   
- The "Why": Traversed by the party during their journey across Avernus toward the Tower of Urm, presenting a choice to free the captive celestial.
### Exploration & Gameplay

#### Key Areas (Pick 3–4)
- The Perimeter Wastes: The scorched barren field surrounding the structure where stray imps and low-tier fiends are regularly zapped into dust.

- The Orb Platform: The elevated focal point holding the trapped unicorn whose divine power fuels the zapper's destructive rays.

- The Tower Base: The grounding base of the structure guarded by planar entities like Ralzala the Dao.
#### Threats & Complications
##### Hazards/Traps: 
Automated divine discharge beams that disintegrate fiendish entities and deal massive radiant damage to nearby targets.
##### Denizens:
(Who or what is currently here?)
- 🔴 _Hostile:_ [[Ralzala]] (Dao guardian, slain and turned to dust)

- 🟡 _Neutral/Negotiable:_ Cursed Chain Devil (trapped inside Daggs' sword)

- 🟢 _Friendly/Allies:_ The Trapped Unicorn (freed by Johnny)
#### Secrets & Rewards
##### The Twist/Hidden Discovery: 
The tower can be completely brought down by striking it with sufficient force (e.g., Johnny's critical warhammer strikes), liberating the celestial entity within.
##### Loot & Lore: 
Ralzala's warhammer left behind in the dust and a freed celestial unicorn.
### DM Note / Regional Rule (Optional):
**Fiend Erasure:** Any fiend ending its turn within range of the active zapper must succeed on a high-DC saving throw or take massive radiant damage.
