#### Elevator Pitch 
An ancient, isolated monument in Avernus serving as the magical binding site and imprisonment chamber for the archdevil Zariel.
#### Location
[[Avernus]]
#### The Hook (First Impression)
- Visuals (100 ft away): A massive, dark stone obelisk ringed by towering stone pillars that hum with pulsing abjuration magic.

- Sensory Details: The crackle of unbinding arcane energy in the air, the cold draft of high-tier magic, and the imposing, silent gaze of a dormant archdevil.

- The Atmosphere: Desolate, Ancient, Arcane
#### Core Identity
- Category: Planar Monument / Magical Prison

- Current Status: Formerly Active Binding Site; currently Abandoned (Zariel freed by the party)

- The "Why": The party travelled here following tips from imps at the pit of Shummrath to locate, commune with, and ultimately make a deal to free Zariel.
### Exploration & Gameplay

#### Key Areas (Pick 3–4)
- The Binding Circle: An intricate set of runic seals holding Zariel suspended in place, requiring incantations to dispel.

- The Outer Pillars: Heavy stone pillars surrounding the obelisk that maintain the barrier's magical structural integrity.

- The Perimeter Trench: The surrounding Avernian wastes where Bel's imps gather to monitor or attempt the destruction of the seal.
#### Threats & Complications
##### Hazards/Traps: 
Powerful wards and binding magic capable of backlash if incantations are spoken incorrectly.
##### Denizens:
(Who or what is currently here?)
- 🔴 _Hostile:_ Infernal Fiends & Imps (sent by Bel to target Zariel)

- 🟡 _Neutral/Negotiable:_ [[Zariel]] (archdevil freed after striking deals with Sceptrsire and Daggs)

- 🟢 _Friendly/Allies:_ None
#### Secrets & Rewards
##### The Twist/Hidden Discovery: 
Reciting the binding circle's incantation dispels the seal, transforming Daggs' cursed blade into the chain devil Crowley and granting the party a permanent power boost.
##### Loot & Lore: 
A divine power boost from Zariel, a lighter replacement sword for Daggs, and a vision/lead regarding his lost sister, Mara Stormcloak.
### DM Note / Regional Rule (Optional):
**Abjuration Field:** Spells that attempt to dispel or modify magical effects within 50 feet of the Obelisk are cast at disadvantage unless the incantation is known.
