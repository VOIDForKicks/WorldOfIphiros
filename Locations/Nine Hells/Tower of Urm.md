An enigmatic, multi-planar stronghold in Avernus containing dark biological experiments, specimen pods, and secrets spanning multiple timelines.

[[Avernus]]

- Visuals (100 ft away): A soaring, dark stone monolith standing ominously amid the jagged bone brambles of Avernus.

- Sensory Details: The faint humming of arcane containment fields, the smell of dark alchemy, and cold metallic drafts.

- The Atmosphere: Foreboding, Lab-like, Uncanny
#### Core Identity
- Category: Arcane Citadel / Laboratory / Dimensional Hub

- Current Status: Occupied by Alucard

- The "Why": Trailed here by a dot on Daggs' map to locate Kenny Dollardyne, uncovering planar secrets and timeline clones along the way.
### Exploration & Gameplay

#### Key Areas (Pick 3–4)
- The Specimen Chamber: Contains 9 biological pods holding specimens of notable individuals (e.g., Hugh Jackman, Susanoo, Judy Vespa, Kenny Dollardyne, Mara Stormcloak, Gerald O'Leary, Coal Lorvein, and Garos Thornton).

- The Arcane Vault & Portal Room: Features rare texts (_"[[Storm cloaked pirate]]"_, _"[[Rise of Norton]]"_, _"[[The Exiled Princess]]"_), potion racks, an arcane circle, and red dimensional portals.

- The Treasure Room: A deceptive hoard area filled with active Mimics guarding valuable loot and Alucard's phylactery.

- The Cage Holding Room: A secured chamber housing Kenny Dollardyne in a central cage, where Alucard confronts intruders.
