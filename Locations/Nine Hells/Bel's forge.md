#### Elevator Pitch
A fiery, industrial inferno in the Nine Hells where high-tier weapons are crafted amidst heavy mechanical defences and infernal overlords.
#### Location
[[Avernus]]
#### The Hook (First Impression)
- Visuals (100 ft away): A colossal, smoke-belching fortress forge glowing with rivers of molten metal and towering anvils.

- Sensory Details: Scorching heat radiating against skin, the rhythmic, deafening clanging of heavy hammer strikes, and the acrid smell of burning oil and brimstone.

- The Atmosphere: Industrial, Oppressive, Explosive
#### Core Identity
- Category: Infernal Forge / Planar Stronghold

- Current Status: Formerly ruled by Bel (Defeated/Cast out by the party)
  
- The "Why": The party infiltrated the forge to acquire heavenly artifact weapons, confront Bel, and ultimately clear a path through Avernus.
### Exploration & Gameplay

#### Key Areas (Pick 3–4)
- The Forge Entrance: The heavily guarded perimeter where intruders must sneak, misty step, or leap inside to avoid immediate detection.

- The Anvil & Rubble Floor: The central crafting area featuring massive anvils, support beams, and heavy machinery that can be collapsed onto targets.

- The Upper Quenching Vault: A elevated storage area housing barrels of rare quenching oil and weapons calling out to prospective wielders.
#### Threats & Complications
##### Hazards/Traps:
Collapsible overhead support structures, boiling pools of quenching oil, and intense infernal heat hazards.
##### Denizens:
- 🔴 _Hostile:_ [[Bel]] (former ruler of Avernus), infernal forge guards
  
- 🟡 _Neutral/Negotiable:_ [[Crowley]] (cursed chain devil who sacrificed himself to restrain Bel)

- 🟢 _Friendly/Allies:_ None
#### Secrets & Rewards
##### The Twist/Hidden Discovery:
The forge houses dormant heavenly spirit weapons that call out directly to chosen individuals, transforming upon touch.
##### Loot & Lore:
Weapons of the heavenly spirits, rare quenching oil, and holy oil.
### DM Note / Regional Rule (Optional):
**Extreme Heat & Blinding Strikes:** Unprotected creatures take passive fire damage each round, and heavy weapon impacts on the main anvil roll with increased crit threat against nearby targets.
