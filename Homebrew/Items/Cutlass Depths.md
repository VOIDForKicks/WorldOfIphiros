#### Type:
Scimitar (Cutlass)
#### Rarity:
Artefact
#### Description:
A curved scimitar forged from a strange, deep-sea alloy that constantly drips with freezing saltwater. Its blade resembles hardened black coral, and the guard is shaped like the gaping jaw of a prehistoric leviathan. When swung, the blade hums with the low, terrifying frequency of a shifting continental shelf.
##### Weight of the Deep
You gain a **+2 bonus** to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit a creature with it, the scimitar deals an extra **1d8 cold damage and 4d6 slashing damage** as spatial pressure crushes the target.

##### The Crushing Abyss
The weapon has **7 charges** for the following properties. It regains all expended charges daily at dawn.
- **Undertow (1 Charge):** When you hit a creature with an attack, you can force the target to make a **DC 16 Strength saving throw**. On a failure, a phantom current yanks them up to 15 feet closer to you, and they are knocked prone.
- **Mariana's Trench (3 Charges):** As an action, you slam the blade into the ground, causing gravity to heavily warp in a 20-foot radius centred on you. For 1 minute, that area becomes difficult terrain for your enemies. Any enemy that starts its turn in the area or enters it for the first time on a turn must succeed on a **DC 16 Constitution saving throw** or take **3d6 bludgeoning damage** from the sudden, immense pressure and have their speed reduced to 0 until the start of their next turn.
##### Artefact Curse: The Cold Solitude
The abyss does not share its floor. While attuned to this weapon, you have disadvantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks against anyone who does not worship a deity of the sea, as your presence radiates the unsettling, crushing isolation of the deep ocean.
##### True Form (Artefact Release)
When released, the scimitar's blade violently shatters into six distinct shards of black coral and deep-sea alloy. These pieces do not fall; they hover in a perfectly calculated, rotating orbit around the wielder’s arm, dripping with freezing saltwater and humming with intense gravitational pressure.

**Duration:** 1 minute (or until the wielder drops to 0 hit points).

**True Form Enhancements:**
- **The Six Shards (Abyssal Reach):** Your melee attacks now have a reach of 30 feet as you telekinetically hurl the six floating shards at your targets. When you take the Attack action, the weapon's extra damage scales up to **3d8 cold damage and 8d6 bludgeoning damage** as the pressurized shards strike in rapid succession.
- **Crushing Orbit:** The swirling shards form a protective, high-pressure boundary around you. You gain a **+2 bonus to AC**, and any creature that starts its turn within 5 feet of you must succeed on a **DC 16 Strength saving throw** or take **2d6 bludgeoning damage** and be pushed 10 feet away by the rotating currents.
- **Hex-Trench Execution (One-Time Use):** As an action, you can command all six shards to simultaneously pin a single creature you can see within 60 feet, driving into the ground around them to form a localized cage of absolute pressure. The target must make a **DC 16 Constitution saving throw.**  _On a failure:_ They take **8d6 force damage**, are knocked **Prone**, and are **Restrained** beneath an unyielding gravitational weight. _On a success:_ They take half damage and are not prone or restrained. The six shards then fly back together, seamlessly reforming the solid blade, and the True Form ends immediately.
