#### Type:
Shield
#### Rarity:
Artefact
#### Description:
A heavy tower shield forged from blackened iron that seems to radiating a low, volcanic heat. The front of the shield is embossed with the terrifying visage of a three-headed canine silhouette. The eyes of the hound silhouettes flicker with a faint, embers-red glow that flares violently whenever the wielder or their allies take damage.

##### The Unyielding Bastion
While holding this shield, you gain a **+2 bonus to AC** in addition to the shield's normal defence. You are also immune to being knocked prone or moved against your will while your feet are planted on the ground.
##### Guardian's Retaliation
The weapon has **7 charges** for the following properties. It regains all expended charges daily at dawn.
- **Hellish Rebuke (1 Charge):** When a creature within 60 feet of you hits you or an ally with an attack, you can use your reaction to force the attacker to make a **DC 16 Dexterity saving throw**. On a failure, they take **3d10 fire damage** as a phantom hound head lunges from the shield to snap at them.
- **Cerberus Interception (2 Charges):** When an ally within 30 feet of you is targeted by an attack, you can use your reaction to instantly leap to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of that ally, intercepting the blow. The attack roll is made against your AC instead, and you gain resistance to all damage dealt by that attack.
##### Artefact Curse: The Savage Mind
The hellish rage of Hytos demands an outlet. Whenever you drop to less than half your maximum hit points while holding this shield, you must succeed on a **DC 14 Wisdom saving throw** or enter a feral battle-trance for 1 minute. While in this trance, you have advantage on melee attack rolls but cannot cast spells or give tactical commands, driven purely by the urge to bite and tear at the enemy.
##### True Form (Artefact Release)
When released, the blackened iron of the shield violently splits down the middle, transforming into three independent, floating shields of molten magma and spectral chains that orbit the wielder like snapping jaws.

**Duration:** 1 minute (or until the wielder drops to 0 hit points).

**True Form Enhancements:**
- **The Three Jaws:** Your AC bonus increases to **+4** as the three shields block incoming attacks telekinetically. As a bonus action on your turn, you can command the floating shields to slam into a creature within 15 feet. The target must make a **DC 16 Strength saving throw** or take **2d8 bludgeoning damage plus 2d6 fire damage** and be grappled by spectral chains.
- **Terrifying Howl:** Upon activation, the spectral hounds release a coordinated, deafening howl. Every enemy within 30 feet must succeed on a **DC 16 Wisdom saving throw** or be **Frightened** of you for the duration of the release.
- **Gatekeeper’s Climax (One-Time Use):** As an action, you can command the three floating shields to slam together into the earth, creating an inescapable, flaming cage around yourself and up to three adjacent enemies. A 20-foot-radius ring of fire erupts. Any creature that attempts to cross the ring must make a **DC 16 Constitution saving throw**, taking **8d6 fire damage** on a failure, or half as much on a success. The cage lasts until the end of your next turn, at which point the shields snap back into your hand, and the True Form ends immediately.
