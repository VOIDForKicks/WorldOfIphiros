#### Type:
Quarterstaff
#### Rarity:
Artefact
#### Description:
A perfectly symmetrical quarterstaff carved from seamless, white marble that never cracks, gets dirty, or scratches. Etched along its length are glowing, geometric lines of sapphire light that constantly calculate the structural matrix of the room. The staff behaves with absolute precision; swinging it leaves trailing, crystalline equations in the air that hum with unyielding force.
##### Structural Matrix
You gain a **+2 bonus** to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit a creature with it, the quarterstaff deals an extra **1d8 force damage and 1d6 radiant damage** as reality corrects itself to break the target's physical composition.
##### The Architect's Decree
The weapon has **7 charges** for the following properties. It regains all expended charges daily at dawn.
- **Spatial Realignment (1 Charge):** When you hit a creature with an attack, you can warp the geometry beneath their feet. Choose one ally within 30 feet of you. The target and your ally instantly swap places in a flash of sapphire light. This teleportation does not provoke opportunity attacks.
- **Unyielding Anchor (2 Charges):** As a reaction when an enemy tries to move, fly, or teleport within 60 feet of you, you can slam the base of the staff into the ground. The target must succeed on a **DC 16 Charisma saving throw** or have its movement instantly reduced to 0 and its teleportation spell fail, locked into its current spatial coordinates until the start of its next turn.
##### Artefact Curse: The Rigid Mind
Anare demands perfect structure and predictability. While attuned to this weapon, you find chaotic behaviour, messy environments, or sudden changes in plans profoundly distressing. You have disadvantage on Charisma (Deception) checks, as your speech and posture automatically default to absolute, uncompromising truth and symmetry.

##### True Form (Artefact Release)
When released, the marble staff shatters into a floating array of dozens of transparent, crystalline blueprint panes and glowing measuring lines that wrap around the wielder like an external nervous system. The ground in a 60-foot radius transforms into a glowing grid of absolute cosmic law.

**Duration:** 1 minute (or until the wielder drops to 0 hit points).

**True Form Enhancements:**
- **Geometric Armament:** The weapon no longer acts as a blunt object. When you take the Attack action, you telekinetically command the structural panes to slash at targets up to 15 feet away. The extra damage scales up to **6d8 force damage and 3d6 radiant damage** as space itself folds to crush your enemies.
- **Architectural Sovereignty:** You gain a flying speed of 30 feet (hover). Additionally, you can choose to ignore any difficult terrain, walls, or magical barriers (like a _Wall of Fire_) within the 60-foot grid; you and your allies can walk right through them as if they do not exist.
- **The Prime Equation (One-Time Use):** As an action, you can rewrite the physical laws of the grid. Choose up to three creatures you can see within 60 feet. They must make a **DC 16 Constitution saving throw** as you attempt to telekinetically deconstruct their atomic structure. _On a failure:_ They take **8d6 force damage** and are **Paralyzed** until the end of their next turn as their bodies are locked in a structural stasis. _On a success:_ They take half damage and are not paralyzed. The geometric grid fades back into the marble staff, and the True Form ends immediately.
