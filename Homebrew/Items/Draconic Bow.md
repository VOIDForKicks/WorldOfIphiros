#### Description:
A massive, recurve longbow carved from a single piece of storm-fractured petrified wood, bound together by arcing bands of copper. The bow string is a literal crackling thread of pure, blinding white electricity that hums with the concussive bass of a distant tempest. It does not require physical arrows; drawing the string creates a localized jolt of compressed atmospheric plasma.
##### Primordial Inception
You gain a **+2 bonus** to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit a creature with it, the longbow deals an extra **1d8 lightning damage and 3d6 thunder damage** as the shockwave deafens the target.

##### The First Strike
The weapon has **7 charges** for the following properties. It regains all expended charges daily at dawn.
- **Arcing Inception (1 Charge):** When you hit a creature with an attack, you can command the lightning to jump. Choose up to two other creatures within 15 feet of the original target. They must succeed on a **DC 16 Dexterity saving throw** or take lightning damage equal to the lightning damage dealt to the primary target.
- **Concussive Gale (2 Charges):** As a bonus action after hitting a creature with a ranged attack from this bow, you detonate the localized thunder around them. The target and all creatures within a 10-foot radius of it must succeed on a **DC 16 Strength saving throw** or be blown 15 feet away from the target centre and knocked **Prone**.
##### Artefact Curse: The Groundless Conduit
Ormu’s power demands constant, kinetic grounding. While attuned to this weapon, your body acts as a cosmic lightning rod. Whenever you are surprised or enter a state of deep, quiet rest, small static discharges snap from your fingers, rendering you incapable of hiding or maintaining a stealthy presence while the weapon is on your person.
##### True Form (Artefact Release)
When released, the bow violently snaps straight, transforming into a massive, 7-foot-long iron-bound lightning rod that hovers perfectly in place behind the wielder. Storm clouds manifest instantly in a 60-foot ceiling above, and lightning rods of pure energy arc from the wielder's eyes.

**Duration:** 1 minute (or until the wielder drops to 0 hit points).

**True Form Enhancements:**
- **Galvanic Armament:** The weapon no longer acts as a traditional bow. When you take the Attack action, you telekinetically direct bolts from the hovering rod. The extra damage scales up to **3d8 lightning damage and 8d6 thunder damage** as the thunderclap shatters stone with every launch.
- **Storm-King’s Ascent:** You gain a flying speed of 30 feet, and your ranged attacks ignore half cover and three-quarters cover as the lightning automatically bends and tracking its target through air currents.
- **The Origin Point (One-Time Use):** As an action, you can point to a location within 120 feet, calling down the absolute first thunderbolt. A cataclysmic column of white-hot lightning strikes in a 20-foot-radius, 100-foot-high cylinder. Every creature in that area must make a **DC 16 Constitution saving throw**. _On a failure:_ They take **8d6 lightning damage**, are **Blinded** and **Deafened** until the end of their next turn, and are pinned to the floor. _On a success:_ They take half damage and suffer no additional conditions. The localized storm instantly dissipates, the rod snaps back into a longbow, and the True Form ends immediately.
