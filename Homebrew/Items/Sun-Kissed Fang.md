#### Type:
Pistol
#### Rarity:
Artefact
#### Description:
A sleek, elegant pistol carved from shimmering white elderwood and wrapped in green, living vines that never wither. Instead of a standard barrel, the weapon features an intricate, swirling silver muzzle shaped like a fox's tail. The pistol does not require physical ammunition; it fires condensed bolts of emerald green nature energy that crackle with laughter when discharged.
##### The Untamed Spark
You gain a **+2 bonus** to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon. When you hit a creature with it, the pistol deals an extra **3d8 force damage and 1d6 psychic damage** as chaotic energy scrambles the target’s focus.
##### Trickster's Misdirection
The weapon has **7 charges** for the following properties. It regains all expended charges daily at dawn.
- **Foxfire Blink (1 Charge):** When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can expend 1 charge to instantly teleport up to 15 feet to an unoccupied space you can see before or after making an attack. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.
- **Kitsune's Double (2 Charges):** As a reaction when a creature hits you with an attack, you can dissolve into a burst of green leaves and cherry blossoms, causing the attack to miss. You teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see, leaving behind a mocking illusion of yourself that explodes into a flash of light. The attacker must succeed on a **DC 16 Wisdom saving throw** or be **Frightened** of you until the end of their next turn.
##### Artefact Curse: The Drifting Heart
Yuki's wild energy cannot be anchored down. While attuned to this weapon, you have disadvantage on saving throws against being charmed or compelled to follow rules, as your soul constantly yearns to break free, laugh, and sprint toward the next thrill.
##### True Form (Artefact Release)
When released, the wooden frame of the pistol splits and extends, wrapping around the wielder's arm like a gauntlet made of living silver thorns. Six ghostly, green-flamed fox tails erupt from the gauntlet, weaving through the air telekinetically to fire barrages of energy.

**Duration:** 1 minute (or until the wielder drops to 0 hit points).

**True Form Enhancements:**
- **The Wild Barrage:** The weapon no longer needs to be fired shot-by-shot. When you take the Attack action, the ghostly tails fire simultaneously. The extra damage scales up to **6d8 force damage and 3d6 psychic damage**.
- **Joyous Sprint:** Your movement speed increases by 20 feet, and you can move through enemy spaces as if they were difficult terrain. If you pass through an enemy's space, they must make a **DC 16 Dexterity saving throw** or take **2d6 fire damage** from the trailing foxfire.
- **Prankster's Mirage (One-Time Use):** As an action, you can unleash all nine tails of energy into a blinding vortex of shifting lights and illusions. Up to six creatures of your choice within 60 feet must make a **DC 16 Intelligence saving throw**. _On a failure:_ They take **8d6 psychic damage** and are **Charmed** by you, viewing their own allies as monsters and attacking them on their next turn. _On a success:_ They take half damage and are not charmed. The gauntlet then snaps back into its elegant pistol form, and the True Form ends immediately.
