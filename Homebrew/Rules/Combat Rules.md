Initiative: 
Nat 20 - Two turns first round 
Nat 1 - Lose of first turn 

Critical hits: 
Damage Crits - Add max dmg roll (no modifier) then roll normal damage e.g. a crit with dagger 4 + 1d4 + 2 (max roll + normal roll + modifier) 

Cleave through (modified): 
If more damage is dealt than enemy HP you can deal the excess damage to nearby creatures (5ft) as long as the initial to hit roll beats their AC. Spells are able to continue along their path if the damage they would have done exceeds the target creatures HP with same condition as melee.

Time turns: 
Each player much decide what they are going to do with in 2 mins of their turn starting.

Over-Hit:
Getting double the targets AC causes damage to be 1.5x more than normal (After all rolls)
