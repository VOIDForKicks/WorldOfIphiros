**Level:** 9
**Cast Time:** 1 Action
**Hit/DC:** DEX save
**Damage/Healing:** 22d12 fire damage, 3d8 fire damage for 2 turns after

**Description:**
You channel the primordial heart of Pyra to detonate the atmosphere at a designated location. A massive, superheated pillar of flame erupts at a point you can see within range, consuming a 20-foot-radius sphere.

Each creature in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw.

On a failed save, a creature takes 22d12 fire damage and is ignited by cosmic embers. While ignited, the target takes an additional 3d8 fire damage at the start of each of its next two turns.

On a successful save, a creature takes half as much initial damage and is not ignited by the embers.
