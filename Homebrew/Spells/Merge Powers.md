**Description:**
The user is able to draw two souls closer to each other in order to boost their power. The caster can only merge with people or creatures that are willing.

The caster is able to use some abilities of the person or creature they have merged with. If the caster merges with a creature they gain certain features of that creature for the duration of the merge. If the creature has claws the caster will have "phantom claws" that will allow them to use the claw attack using the creatures rolls and the casters modifiers. When merging with a person, they gain that persons proficiencies for the duration of the merge. If this person is able to cast spells the caster of merge powers gains 4 spells (Discuss with DM) belonging to that person (1 plus your proficiency bonus)
