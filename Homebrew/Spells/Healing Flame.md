**Level:** 6
**Cast Time:** 1 Bonus Action
**Hit/DC:** N/A
**Damage/Healing:** 16d8 + your spellcasting ability modifier healing

**Description:**
Mystical, cleansing flames engulf a target creature you can see within range, empowering them with the protective warmth of new life. The target instantly regains hit points equal to 16d8 + your spellcasting ability modifier.

Because the target's body is purged by the divine embers of Pyra, this healing also ends any blindness, deafness, paralysis, or poisoning affecting the target. Additionally, the lingering warmth grants the target resistance to fire damage until the start of your next turn.
