**Level:** 7
**Cast Time:** 1 Action
**Hit/DC:** STR save
**Damage/Healing:** 8d10 bludgeoning damage, 4d10 fire damage, 2d10 fire damage until freed

**Description:**
You slam your hands or your weapon into the earth, fracturing reality to call forth the binding gates of Hytos. Up to three targets you can see within range are suddenly targeted by massive, magma-dripping chains that burst from the earth.

Each target must make a Strength saving throw.

On a failed save, a target takes 8d10 bludgeoning damage plus 4d10 fire damage, is pulled up to 15 feet toward a central point of your choice within the spell's range, and is Restrained by the chains. While restrained, the creature takes 2d10 fire damage at the start of each of its turns. A restrained creature can use its action to make a Strength check against your spell save DC, breaking free on a success.

On a successful save, a target takes half as much initial damage and is not pulled or restrained.
