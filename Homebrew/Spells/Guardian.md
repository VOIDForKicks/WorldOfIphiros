**Level:** 8
**Cast Time:** 1 Reaction
**Hit/DC:** N/A
**Damage/Healing:** N/A

**Description:**
You call upon the unyielding bastion of Hytos to manifest an absolute protective barrier. A towering, spectral shield woven from obsidian iron and volcanic embers manifests to hover around a chosen target you can see.

For the duration, the target gains a +10 bonus to Armor Class, and any attack rolls made against them have disadvantage.

Additionally, the shield radiates Hytos's hellish wrath; whenever a creature hits the protected target with a melee attack, the attacker takes 4d10 fire damage as magma splashes back at them, and the attacker cannot take reactions until the start of its next turn.
