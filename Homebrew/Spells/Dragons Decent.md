**Level:** 8
**Cast Time:** 1 Action
**Hit/DC:** CON save
**Damage:** 8d6 bludgeoning, 22d6 lightening

**Description:**
Dark clouds gather above the target area, condensing large amounts of lightning into the shape of a dragon before releasing the lightning down onto the ground. On impact creatures at the impact area 10ft will take 8d6 bludgeoning damage before the lightning disperses. Creatures in a 40ft radius must succeed a CON saving throw taking 22d6 lightening damage on a failed save or half as much on a success. Creatures within the impact area of the spell must make a DEX saving throw to avoid the bludgeoning damage.
