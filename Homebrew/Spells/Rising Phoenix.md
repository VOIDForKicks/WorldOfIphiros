**Level:** 8
**Cast Time:** 1 Action
**Hit/DC:** DEX save
**Damage/Healing:** 21d10 fire damage

**Description:**  
Incandescent, blinding flames burst violently from the ground beneath a point you choose within range, erupting upward into the magnificent form of a roaring Phoenix.

Each creature within a 10-foot radius of the spell's centre point must make a Dexterity saving throw.

On a failed save, a creature takes 21d10 fire damage and is knocked prone by the concussive, ascending thermal updraft. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and is not knocked prone.
