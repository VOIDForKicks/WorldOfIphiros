**Level:** 7
**Cast Time:** 1 Action
**Hit/DC:** DEX save
**Damage:** 6d6 piercing, 2d6 necrotic (total divide by 3 for each head)

**Description:**
You target up to three creatures you can see within range. A ring of rusted, molten chains violently erupts from the earth around each target, and a massive, ash-covered hellhound head emerges from the links, biting down savagely.

Each target must make a Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a target takes 6d6 piercing damage and 2d6 necrotic damage per head, and it is Restrained by the molten chains until the end of your next turn. On a successful save, a target takes half as much damage and is not restrained.
