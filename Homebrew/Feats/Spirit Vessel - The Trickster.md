With this feat you have been chosen by the Heavenly Spirit known as "[[Yuki (The Trickster)|The Trickster]]", anchoring an erratic, enigmatic weave of illusion and force to your very soul. This deep spiritual bond manifests a unique artefact called [[Sun-Kissed Fang]], which you can use as a spellcasting focus for your abilities.

While bearing the trickster's favour, your social presence and force of personality become preternaturally overwhelming; you gain advantage on all Charisma saving throws and Charisma ability checks (such as Deception, Intimidation, Performance, and Persuasion).

Additionally, you gain 6 spells: [[Merge Powers]], [[Spirit Projection]], [[Artefact Release]], [[Kitsune Rush]], [[Nine-tailed Assault]], and [[Trickster's Gambit]].
