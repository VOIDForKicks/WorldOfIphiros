You have been chosen by the Heavenly Spirit known as "[[Hytos (The Guardian)|The Guardian]]," anchoring its protective and punitive might to your very soul. This deep spiritual bond manifests a unique artefact called the [[Aegis Hades]], which you can use as a spellcasting focus for your abilities.

While bearing The Guardian's favour, your reflexes are supernaturally heightened; you gain advantage on all Dexterity saving throws and Dexterity ability checks.

Additionally, you gain access to 6 specialized spirit spells: [[Merge Powers]], [[Spirit Projection]], [[Artefact Release]], [[Guardian]], [[Hells Chains]], and [[Cerberus' Meal]].
