With this feat you have been chosen by Heavenly Spirit known as "[[Anare (The All Knowing)|The All Knowing]]", anchoring an infinite library of cosmic insight and absolute clarity to your very soul. This deep spiritual bond manifests a unique artefact called [[Staff of Knowledge]], which you can use as a spellcasting focus for your abilities.

While bearing the favour of the all-knowing, your mind operates at a divine velocity; you gain advantage on all Intelligence saving throws and Intelligence ability checks (such as Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature, and Religion).

Additionally, you gain 6 spells: [[Merge Powers]], [[Spirit Projection]], [[Artefact Release]], [[Rays of Many]], [[Full Awareness]], and [[Multiply]]
