You have been chosen by the Heavenly Spirit known as "[[Mariana (The Leviathan)|The Leviathan]]", anchoring the crushing power of the endless deep to your very soul. This deep spiritual bond manifests a unique artefact called [[Cutlass Depths]], which you can use as a spellcasting focus for your abilities.

While bearing The Leviathan's favour, your physical might becomes unstoppable; you gain advantage on all Strength saving throws and Strength ability checks (such as Athletics).

Additionally, you gain access to 6 specialized spirit spells: [[Merge Powers]], [[Spirit Projection]], [[Artefact Release]], [[Abyssal Grasp]], [[Oceanic Pressure]], and [[Tidal Wave]].
