With this feat you have been chosen by the Heavenly Spirit known as "[[Ormu (The Origin)|The Origin]]", anchoring the primordial, storm-born clarity of the first dragons to your very soul. This deep spiritual bond manifests a unique artefact called [[Draconic Bow]], which you can use as a spellcasting focus for your abilities.

While bearing the origin's favour, your instinct and spiritual perception are elevated to cosmic levels; you gain advantage on all Wisdom saving throws and Wisdom ability checks (such as Insight, Medicine, Perception, Survival, and Animal Handling).

Additionally, you gain 6 spells: [[Merge Powers]], [[Spirit Projection]], [[Artefact Release]], [[Dragons Decent]], [[Origin Dragons Breath]], and [[Lightning Skin]].
