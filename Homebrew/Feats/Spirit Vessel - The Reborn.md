With this feat you have been chosen by the Heavenly Spirit known as "[[Pyra (The Reborn)|The Reborn]]", anchoring the eternal, undying cycle of vitality to your very soul. This deep spiritual bond manifests a unique artefact called [[Life Forger]], which you can use as a spellcasting focus for your abilities.

While bearing The Reborn's favour, your physical resilience and vitality become supernaturally fortified; you gain advantage on all Constitution saving throws and Constitution ability checks.

Additionally, you gain 6 spells: [[Merge Powers]], [[Spirit Projection]], [[Artefact Release]], [[Rising Phoenix]], [[Healing Flame]], and [[Flashfire]].
