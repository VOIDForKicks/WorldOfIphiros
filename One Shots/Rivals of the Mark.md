The enemies of the Bond Through Time characters are in the base of Xeno in an interdimensional plane.
Xeno addresses them and tells them they must go out and recruit the final member to the Court of Infinity Rofs Korg from the country of Aclea.

### What happened
Garos and Kenny are addressed by Xeno and are told that they must go to Vrerin Stragaad in order to recruit Rofs Korg.
Gerald wakes up causing Kenny and Garos go to help him.
The party walk through the temple and end up at the the capital of Aclea.
The party begin to search the city to find gifts for the queen.
Uriberos appears at the Jewellers.
Kenny, Garos and Uriberos head to the blacksmith in order to get boots for the queen while Gerald waits outside.
The party then head to the knights tavern in the capital.
In the tavern Gerald spots 2 high ranking guards discussing the exiled princess Alt "Schwarz" Korgs.
Gerald convinces the guards to get them an audience with the queen the following day.
Once inside Garos tells the queen that Xeno would be willing to marry her if she joins their cause.
Rofs Korgs joins the Court of Infinity but doesn't marry Xeno.
