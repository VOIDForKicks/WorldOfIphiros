##### Description/Background
Deneir (pronounced _deh-neer_), known as the Scribe of Oghma, is the divine personification of the written word, literacy, and the systematic preservation of knowledge. While his superior, Oghma, represents the abstract concept of raw knowledge and thoughts, Deneir is dedicated to the physical translation of those thoughts into books, maps, glyphs, and artistic manuscripts. He believes that truth, beauty, and wisdom are codified within a legendary, cosmic equation known as the "Metatext", a flawless, evolving divine script that details the true framework of the multiverse. Deneir's eternal pursuit is to record and analyse every scrap of written language to eventually complete this supreme manuscript.

Deneir is a deeply benevolent, highly intellectual deity who views literacy as a primary tool for mortal elevation, self-improvement, and social progress. He firmly believes that information should never be hoarded, locked away, or weaponized for tyrannical control. Instead, it should be shared openly to dispel ignorance, cure epidemics, and foster artistic expression. He demands that his followers meticulously copy down fading histories, translate texts into common tongues, and aggressively protect libraries from fire, war, or the deliberate suppression of oppressive regimes.
##### Worshippers
Scribes, librarians, historical researchers, cartographers, clockwork inventors, wizards who design magical scrolls, and knowledge domain clerics. His holy spaces are pristine, quiet scriptoriums, public libraries, and vast archival vaults attached to prominent universities, where the air smells faintly of parchment, dried ink, and melting candle wax.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Preserve and Transcribe: Actively save books, maps, and historical scrolls from destruction or decay; spend your downtime transcribing copies of rare works to distribute them to the public.
- Banish Ignorance: Teach the illiterate to read, share your tactical and magical knowledge freely with your party, and stand firmly against tyrants who seek to burn books or censor truth.
- Seek the Metatext: Read between the lines of ancient texts, analyse cryptic runes, and treat every newfound spell scroll or historical record as a holy piece of a grander cosmic puzzle.
##### Favoured Weapon
