##### Description/Background
Gond, known as the Lord of All Smithies, is the divine heart of innovation, mechanical engineering, and physical creation within the Forgotten Realms. He governs the brilliant, often chaotic spark of invention, transforming abstract theoretical concepts into physical reality through metallurgy, woodwork, alchemy, and masonry. He is a relentlessly focused, detached deity who views the cosmos not through a lens of moral good or evil, but as an infinitely complex, evolving machine waiting to be upgraded. To Gond, the act of inventing a tool, refining a clockwork engine, or constructing a grand fortress is the highest form of spiritual expression.

His relationship with his following is defined by a push for constant progress. He does not care if an invention is used to heal a sick village or lay waste to a city wall; he only cares that the device functions beautifully, pushes the boundaries of current engineering, and solves a structural problem. This single-minded focus famously led to the introduction of _lantanese_ smoke powder and firearms to the world. He expects his clergy to be constantly building, testing, and dismantling prototypes, viewing a broken or failed experiment not as a disappointment, but as a critical, logical step toward absolute perfection.
##### Worshippers
Artificers, forge domain clerics, rock gnome inventors, structural architects, siege engineers, weapon smiths, and shipwrights. His holy places are sprawling, smoky foundry-temples filled with waterwheels, bellows, and roaring blast furnaces, where the ambient noise of clanging anvils and grinding gears serves as a continuous hymn.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- **Build Beyond Yesterday:** Do not rely on ancient techniques or stagnant blueprints; actively invent, modify, and strive to create a better tool, weapon, or machine than what came before.
- **Value the Process of Failure:** Treat a failed mechanism, broken prototype, or misfired spell as a valuable lesson; systematically analyse what went wrong and rebuild it with greater precision.
- **Let the Machine Speak:** judge your allies and enemies by the utility, craftsmanship, and functionality of their actions and gear rather than their empty rhetoric or titles.
