##### Description/Background: 
Mask is the patron deity of thieves, scoundrels, spies, and beggars across Faerûn. Known as the Lord of Shadows or the Master of All Thieves, he is a consummate planner, double-crosser, and stealth archetype who views the world as a grand stage for daring heists and intricate schemes. Mask does not steal out of basic greed, but rather for the absolute thrill of the challenge and to prove that no vault is truly impenetrable. He is a master of disguise and misdirection, once famously walking the material plane as the sentient sword _Godsbane_ to manipulate key events during the Time of Troubles. While his fortunes have fluctuated wildly over cosmic history, at one point losing a massive chunk of his power and his divinity to Cyric and Shar, Mask's core essence is as slippery as shadow itself, allowing him to inevitably reform, reclaim his mantle, and return to guiding the hands of rogues from the dark corners of the multiverse.
##### Worshippers:
Rogues, burglars, information brokers, assassins, and undercover agents. His shrines are never found out in the open; instead, they are hidden in back-alley safehouses, concealed compartments within thieves' guild headquarters, or integrated subtly into the decor of high-stakes gambling dens.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets: 
- Own the Night: Shadows are your armor and your greatest ally; use the darkness to bypass obstacles and keep your true intentions hidden from the light. 
- Take Only What Matters: Value the skill of the take over the material hoard; look down upon crude vandalism or senseless slaughter, as a true master leaves no trace behind. 
- Trust Your Own Shadow: Rely on your own wit, speed, and discretion above all else, keeping your secrets close and your escape routes closer.
