##### Description/Background
Oghma, known as the Binder or the Lord of Knowledge, is the supreme divine force governing all thought, memory, and raw information within the Forgotten Realms. He stands as the patron of all who think, learn, and pass on wisdom, occupying the absolute centre of the intellectual pantheon. Oghma is unique in that he does not merely govern the accumulation of facts; he oversees the genesis of ideas themselves. Whenever an innovator conceives a brand-new theory, a philosopher pieces together a fresh moral worldview, or an archmage designs a new spell formula, they are tapping directly into Oghma's vast cosmic archive. He acts as the eternal gatekeeper of the supreme balance of ideas, ensuring that knowledge continues to flow and evolve through the multiverse rather than stagnating.

Operating with a strictly detached, True Neutral worldview, Oghma prioritizes the purity and preservation of information entirely above mortal concepts of morality. To him, an idea is inherently beautiful and valuable, regardless of whether it is used to forge a tool of grand healing or a weapon of devastating planar warfare. His divine court features prominent intellectual deities like Deneir, the master of literature, and Milil, the master of song, who serve as his hands to translate raw cosmic inspiration into written records and oral histories. Oghma's primary cosmic concern is the suppression of information; he fiercely opposes any entity, tyrant, or corrupt faction that attempts to rewrite history, burn archives, or enforce systemic ignorance upon the mortal world.
##### Worshippers
Wizards, scholars, lore bards, archaeological researchers, philosophers, librarians, and knowledge domain clerics. His grand temples are massive, multi-tiered archival strongholds, quiet scriptoriums, and sprawling universities where the acquisition of a rare, forgotten scroll is celebrated as a high holy achievement.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Bind the Thought: Record every historical event, scientific breakthrough, and philosophical concept you encounter; a piece of knowledge left unwritten is a tragic loss to the collective consciousness.
- Oppose the Censors: Stand unyielding against tyrants, cults, or political systems that try to hoard knowledge, falsify historical timelines, or burn libraries to control the masses.
- Keep the Neutral Mind: Treat information with objective clarity; do not let your personal biases, emotional attachments, or moral judgments cloud your analysis of ancient truths.
