##### Description/Background:
Bhaal is the cold-hearted and merciless Lord of Murder. Originally a power-hungry mortal adventurer, he ascended to godhood alongside Bane and Myrkul by striking a deal with the ancient scribe Jergal, forming the legendary cabal known as the Dead Three. While other death deities manage the passage of souls or focus on the quiet decay of old age, Bhaal explicitly governs over the act of violent, intentional killing. He believes that every drop of blood spilled in malice or ritual execution directly feeds his divine essence, making his followers view murder not just as a tool, but as a holy obligation. Bhaal was famously slain by the mortal Cyric during the Time of Troubles, but he masterfully anticipated his demise. Before falling, he walked the material plane to sire countless mortal children, the Bhaalspawn, structuring a grand, bloody prophecy that would inevitably trigger his resurrection through their inherited bloodlust and slaughter. He is depicted by his priesthood as a horrifying, multi-limbed entity or a shifting shadow carrying a jagged, weeping dagger.
##### Worshippers:
Assassins, sadistic serial killers, executioners, and members of clandestine murder cults. His shrines are strictly illegal, concealed within deep sewer networks, subterranean catacombs, or the hidden basements of innocent-looking businesses, always centred around a stone altar permanently stained with fresh blood.

##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Deal Death Once a Tenday: You must claim a life in the dark of night at least once every ten days; if circumstances restrict you, you must double your tally to appease the Lord.
- Let the Victim Know: Strive to ensure that your quarry looks into your eyes and fully comprehends who is striking them down in Bhaal's sacred name before the final blow lands.
- Feed the Flame: View every assassination and battlefield execution as a sacred act that fractures order and returns the strength of the multiverse back to your deity.

##### Favoured Weapon:
Dagger or Shortsword
