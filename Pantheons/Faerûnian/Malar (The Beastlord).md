##### Description/Background
Malar, known as the Beastlord or the Black-Blooded One, is the primal, unyielding personification of the savage hunt, bloodlust, and the cruel indifference of nature. He does not represent the peaceful balance of the wilderness, but rather its most violent, predatory aspects. Malar is a god of pure instinct, ruling over the raw thrill of the chase, the visceral terror of the prey, and the absolute dominance of the apex predator. He is often depicted as a massive, horrifyingly athletic humanoid beast, a shifting nightmare of matted fur, razor-sharp fangs, and ebony talons fueled by an insatiable hunger for the kill.

Malar’s theology is fundamentally anti-civilization. He views cities, laws, agriculture, and high technology as cowardly constructs that weaken the mortal spirit and shelter the unfit from the natural order. His most infamous legacy is his patronage of lycanthropes; he considers coruscating curses like werewolfism and weretigerism to be divine elevations that strip away weak mortal consciousness to reveal the perfect hunter within. He demands that his followers actively reject mercy, hunt down sentient creatures for sport, and participate in brutal seasonal rituals like the "High Hunt," where an intelligent being is given a head start before being systematically tracked down and torn to pieces under the moonlight.
##### Worshippers
Evil druids, bloodthirsty rangers, feral barbarians, opportunistic hunters, gnolls, and cursed lycanthrope packs. His shrines are deliberately hidden from civilized eyes, found only in the deepest, most claustrophobic thickets, blood-splattered caves, or rings of jagged bone where followers leave the raw hearts and skulls of their most challenging prey.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Feed the Primal Hunger: Survival is a continuous battle fought with tooth and nail; hunt constantly, indulge your predatory instincts, and never show mercy to the weak or the artificial.
- Tear Down the Soft Walls: View cities, fortresses, and legal boundaries as fragile illusions; actively terrorize civilized frontiers to remind mortals that the wild ultimately reclaims everything.
- Honor the Thrill of the Chase: Do not rely on underhanded poisons or distant magical trickery to kill your targets; look your prey in the eyes, hunt them with your raw physical prowess, and taste the victory firsthand.
##### Favoured Weapon
