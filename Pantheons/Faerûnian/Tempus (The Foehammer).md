##### Description/Background
Tempus, known across the battlefields of Toril as the Lord of Battles or the Foehammer, is the absolute divine embodiment of warfare, physical combat, and the raw, unyielding spirit of the soldier. He is not a god of tactical, intellectual strategy like Red Knight, nor is he a bloodthirsty, mindless slaughterer like Garagos; Tempus represents the chaotic, visceral reality of the battlefield itself, the clanging of steel, the roar of the shield wall, and the fierce, impartial wind of fortune that decides who lives and who dies in the mud. He is often depicted as a towering, muscular warrior clad from head to toe in battered, blood-stained plate armour, riding his twin legendary horses: Veiros the white mare and Deiros the black stallion.

Tempus operates under a strict, unyielding code of battlefield honour and total neutrality. He does not choose sides in mortal conflicts based on political agendas or moral alignments; he will grant his divine favour to an evil warmonger just as easily as a righteous defender, provided they fight with absolute bravery, face their enemies face-to-face, and refuse to engage in cowardly massacres. He fiercely despises the slaughter of innocents, the torture of prisoners, and the use of underhanded, dishonourable poisons to win a war. To Tempus, battle is the ultimate test of the mortal spirit, a sacred crucible where true character is revealed through raw courage, discipline, and physical prowess.
##### Worshippers
Soldiers, mercenaries, battle master fighters, war domain clerics, military commanders, gladiators, and honour-bound barbarians. His temples are fortified, functional stone keeps, open-air training yards, and armouries where weapons are continuously sharpened, and the air smells of weapon oil, sweat, and burning hearth fires.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Fight with Absolute Honour: Face your enemies directly and courageously; never resort to cowardly poisons, do not butcher civilian populations, and respect a defeated foe who surrenders with dignity.
- Fear No Combat: Meet the chaos of battle with an unyielding heart and a sharp blade; view every tactical conflict as a sacred crucible to prove your physical and spiritual strength.
- Prepare the Steel: Keep your armour meticulously maintained and your weapons perfectly sharpened at all times; a soldier who is unprepared for the sudden outbreak of war is an insult to the Foehammer.
##### Favoured Weapon
