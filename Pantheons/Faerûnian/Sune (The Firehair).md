Greater Deity
##### Description/Background: 
Sune, often called Lady Firehair, is the radiant goddess of beauty, love, passion, and artistic expression. She is a benevolent, highly emotional deity who believes that true beauty is the most precious thing in the multiverse, capable of inspiring mortals to greatness and shielding them from corruption. Sune's definition of beauty transcends superficial aesthetics, encompassing inner goodness, romance, and joyful intimacy, though she is fiercely protective of physical splendour as well. She is famously temperamental and vain, prone to deep bouts of sorrow if she witnesses hideous acts, yet she possesses an incredibly warm, nurturing heart. Sune actively encourages her followers to indulge in romance, create exquisite art, and live every moment to its fullest emotional potential. She rules from her divine realm of Brightwater, standing as a vibrant champion of light and pleasure against the dark, depressing, and life-extinguishing forces of the pantheon.
##### Worshippers: 
Artists, actors, lovers, poets, bards, and paladins of devotion who swear to protect the beautiful things of the world. Her temples, known as Houses of Firehair, are grand, luxurious estates filled with breathtaking statues, pristine reflection pools, lush gardens, and warm, lavender-scented baths where mortals gather to socialise and appreciate fine arts.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Nurture the Beautiful: Seek out and protect beauty in all its forms, whether it is a masterpiece painting, a blooming garden, a pristine landscape, or a gentle soul.
- Follow Your Heart: Embrace passion, love, and genuine affection without shame or hesitation; let joy and emotional expression guide your actions rather than cold, rigid laws.
- Banish the Hideous: Oppose cruelty, ugliness of spirit, and senseless destruction, understanding that malice is a blight that seeks to dim the natural brilliance of the world.
##### Favoured Weapon: 
Whip or Dagger (often stylized with elegant golden filigree)
