##### Description/Background
Tymora, known as Lady Luck or Our Smiling Lady, is the divine embodiment of good fortune, risk-taking, and the rewarding spark of pure skill within the Forgotten Realms. Her origins are deeply intertwined with tragedy and cosmic split. Originally, she and her bitter rival, Beshaba (the Goddess of Misfortune), existed as a single primordial deity of luck named Tyche. During an ancient planar incursion, Tyche was subtly infected by a deceptive curse from Moander, the god of rot. To save her soul, Selûne struck Tyche with a beam of pure lunar light, cleanly splitting the corrupted deity down the middle. From this division, Beshaba emerged carrying the bitter curse of bad luck, while Tymora inherited the radiant, vibrant essence of pure, benevolent fortune.

Tymora is a joyous, restless, and deeply charismatic goddess who champions those who dare to take massive leaps of faith. She does not favour the passive or the cowardly who sit safely at home; her divine favour is earned through bold planning, daring bravado, and trusting in one's own skill when staring down impossible odds. She views fate not as a rigid, pre-written script, but as an ever-shifting game of dice waiting for an ambitious hand to roll. She is a natural ally to bold explorers and the absolute patron saint of independent adventurers, actively laughing in the face of absolute despair and encouraging her followers to meet every twist of luck with an unshakeable smile.
##### Worshippers
Adventurers, gamblers, rogue swashbucklers, daring mercenaries, merchants making high-stakes trade investments, and trickery domain clerics. Her shrines are everywhere, found in bustling city casinos, frontier taverns, and hidden dungeon entrances, where travellers flick a silver coin into a small stone fountain or touch a lucky token before embarking on a dangerous quest.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Boldly Claim the Risk: Fortune favours the brave and the proactive; do not let fear keep you stagnant, dare to take the leap, test your limits, and trust your personal skills to carry you through.
- Keep a Smiling Heart: Meet bad luck, sudden setbacks, and catastrophic rolls with grace and good humour; the tides of fortune always shift, and a true believer smiles through the darkest hour.
- Oppose the Cruel Doom: Stand as a shield against the oppressive, fear-mongering machinations of Beshaba; actively dismantle cults that use superstition, hexes, or artificial fear to control the common folk.
