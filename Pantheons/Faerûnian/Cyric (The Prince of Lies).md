##### Description/Background
Cyric is the absolute embodiment of self-serving malice, megalomania, and divine betrayal within the Forgotten Realms. Originally a cynical, opportunistic mortal thief and assassin, Cyric’s destiny shifted drastically during the Time of Troubles. Alongside a band of fellow mortals, he tracked down and murdered the mortal avatar of Bhaal, the God of Murder, using the soul-drinking sword _Godsbane_ (which was secretly the god Mask in disguise). When the Overgod Ao restored the celestial balance at the conclusion of the crisis, Cyric was elevated directly to godhood, inheriting the combined portfolios of Bane, Myrkul, and Bhaal. Overnight, he became the supreme master of Strife, Death, and Murder.

His ascension only magnified his unchecked paranoia and madness. Driven by a desperate need to be recognized as the supreme being of the cosmos, Cyric ordered the creation of a magical tome titled the _Cyrinishad_. This book was enchanted with a reality-warping curse: anyone who read it, mortal or immortal, became instantly, fanatically convinced that Cyric was the one true god of the multiverse. In a poetic twist of fate, Cyric was tricked into reading his own book, plunging him into a permanent state of delusional madness. Though he eventually lost the portfolios of death and tyranny back to resurrected rivals, Cyric remains an incredibly volatile, dangerous engine of chaos, famously murdering Mystra, the Goddess of Magic, and triggering the century-long Spell plague that reshaped the entire multiverse.
##### Worshippers
Assassins, serial killers, corrupt politicians, false prophets, trickery clerics, rogue illusionists, and nihilistic cultists. His churches are not grand public structures but hidden, blood-stained basements and secret societies embedded deep within unsuspecting cities, where cells plot the structural downfall of local governments through misinformation and strategic assassinations.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Spread the Holy Lie: Truth is a weakness to be exploited; use deception, illusions, and carefully crafted rumours to turn allies against one another and collapse organizations from within.
- Slay the Worthy: True power is taken, never given; murder those who hold positions of authority, righteousness, or high social standing to prove that their protective gods are ultimately powerless.
- Acknowledge Only the Prince: Believe blindly in Cyric’s supreme authority and your own absolute superiority; treat all other faiths, laws, and moral codes with utter contempt.
##### Favoured Weapon
Longsword or Dagger (styled to look like a black, soul-drinking blade)
