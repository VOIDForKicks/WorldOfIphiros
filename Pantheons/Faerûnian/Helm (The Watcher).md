Greater Deity
##### Description/Background:
Helm, known as the Watcher or the Vigilant One, is the ultimate patron of guardians, protectors, and sentinels across Faerûn. He is an exceptionally disciplined, unyielding deity who prioritizes duty and alertness above all else. Helm does not judge the morality of an assignment; he believes that the act of protection itself is a sacred, absolute necessity to prevent the multiverse from dissolving into chaos. This absolute commitment to his role was proven during the Time of Troubles, when he was the only god allowed to retain his full divine powers to guard the celestial stairways, a duty that tragically forced him to slay Mystra when she attempted to force her way back to the heavens. While this event caused a temporary dip in his popularity among common folk, Helm’s dedication to shielding the innocent, watching over borders, and standing as a bulwark against evil has cemented his status as a foundational pillar of order in the pantheon. He is traditionally depicted as an imposing warrior clad in flawless, gleaming full plate armour that obscures any trace of a mortal form underneath.
##### Worshippers:
City guards, royal bodyguards, military sentries, paladins of devotion, and community defenders who stand watch through the coldest nights. His temples are built like sturdy, practical watchtowers or fortified keeps situated along contested borders, designed primarily to overlook surrounding landscapes and repel invasions.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Never Slumber on Watch: Maintain absolute vigilance at all times; a moment of distraction or complacency is an invitation to tragedy. 
- Protect the Defenceless: Stand as an unmoving wall between those under your care and the threats that seek to harm them, sacrificing your own safety if the duty demands it. 
- Uphold Your Charge: Fulfill your sworn oaths of protection with unwavering reliability, honoring the contract of guardianship regardless of personal feelings.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Bastard Sword or Longsword
