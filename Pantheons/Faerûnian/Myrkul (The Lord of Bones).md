##### Description/Background
Myrkul, also known as the Reaper or the Lord of Bones, is the chilling, ancient personification of the inevitability of death and the slow, creeping terror of physical decay. Originally a powerful, power-hungry mortal necromancer, Myrkul struck a legendary bargain with his adventuring companions, Bane and Bhaal. Together, they journeyed to the domain of Jergal, the original god of the end of all things. Weary of his eternal duties, Jergal willingly abdicated his cosmic power, allowing the three mortals to divide his massive portfolio. Myrkul claimed absolute dominion over the dead, the underworld, and the ultimate expiration of all living things, ruling from his grim, ash-covered Bone Castle in the Fugue Plane.

Myrkul is an intensely patient, calculating, and cold deity who delights in reminding mortals of their fragile mortality. Unlike Bhaal, who glories in the violent act of murder, Myrkul focuses on the quiet, lingering dread that precedes the grave, wrinkles, failing health, memory loss, and fading light. During the Time of Troubles, his unchecked ambition drove him to conspire with Bane to steal the Tablets of Fate from the Overgod Ao, an act of divine hubris that resulted in his mortal avatar being spectacularly destroyed over the skies of Waterdeep by the mortal mage Midnight. Though his portfolio was subsequently stolen by Cyric and later claimed by Kelemvor, Myrkul’s lingering spiritual corruption refused to fade. He survived for centuries as a disembodied vestige before ultimately anchoring his consciousness back into reality, returning as a lesser god to reclaim his worship among those who fear the cold hand of old age.
##### Worshippers
Necromancers, cultists obsessed with achieving lichdom, cynical undertakers, aging tyrants desperate to cheat their impending demise, death domain clerics, and long-lived races who fear the creeping onset of physical frailty. His shrines are typically stale, unventilated subterranean catacombs, abandoned mausoleums, or stagnant swamps where the odour of rot is treated as a sweet, pleasing incense.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Respect the Inevitable End: Death is the absolute, unyielding destination of every path; remind the arrogant, the young, and the powerful that their accomplishments will eventually crumble into dust.
- Savour the Creeping Decay: Do not rush to end a life prematurely with messy, passionate violence; allow old age, exhaustive disease, and psychological dread to systematically break your targets down first.
- Command the Fallen: Treat the physical remains of the dead as useful instruments of divine will; raise skeletal legions and animate zombies to show the living the absolute futility of resisting Myrkul's ultimate domain.
