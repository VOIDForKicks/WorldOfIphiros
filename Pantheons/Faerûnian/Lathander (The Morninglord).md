##### Description/Background
Lathander is the radiant, eternally optimistic personification of beginnings, hope, and the creative potential of growth within the Forgotten Realms. As the Lord of the Dawn, he presides over the transition from darkness to light, viewing each morning as a clean slate for the multiverse to reinvent itself. He is an immensely popular, energetic deity who champions self-improvement, artistic innovation, and the restless drive to build a better future. To Lathander, progress, passion, and the preservation of life are the truest expressions of mortal spirit, making him an absolute foe to the stagnant corruptions of undeath and decay.

However, his boundless enthusiasm has occasionally bordered on dangerous hubris. Centuries ago, in an event known as the Dawn Cataclysm, Lathander's well-intentioned but reckless ambition led him to try and reshape the entire planar pantheon entirely in his own image. The resulting cosmic backlash triggered a devastating war among the gods, collapsing several divine realms and destroying several lesser deities before his campaign was halted. Though the disaster taught him a degree of patience, Lathander remains an incredibly proactive, forward-looking force, always pushing his followers to look toward the next horizon with unshakeable hope and fiery determination.
##### Worshippers
Light domain clerics, oath of devotion paladins, bards, young idealists, baseline farmers hoping for a prosperous spring, expectant parents, and pioneers establishing new frontier colonies. His temples are masterworks of architecture, constructed out of polished white stone with expansive glass roofs and east-facing windows designed to catch the absolute first rays of the morning sun.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- **Sow Seeds of Hope:** Actively banish despair wherever you find it; treat every failure as a temporary setback and remind the broken that the dawn will always break the longest night.
- **Nurture New Beginnings:** Support young artists, protect newborn life, and fund innovative civic or magical projects that seek to build sustainable, prosperous futures for the realm.
- **Purge the Undead Stagnation:** Hunt down and incinerate vampires, liches, and necromantic blights with radiant fury; these entities are abominations that steal the natural cycle of life and renewal.
