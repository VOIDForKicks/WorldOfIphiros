##### Description/Background: 
Silvanus, known affectionately as the Forest Father or the Old Oak, is the ancient and powerful patron deity of nature and the untamed wilderness. He is one of the oldest and most widely revered entities within the Forgotten Realms pantheon, embodying both the gentle fertility of spring growth and the unyielding, destructive fury of a forest fire or a winter storm. Silvanus does not view nature through a sentimental lens; he represents the wild world in its entirety, completely detached from civilized concerns. His ultimate concern is maintaining the sacred cosmic balance between civilization and the wild, ensuring that expanding cities, logging operations, and agriculture do not permanently choke out the primeval ecosystems of Faerûn. He is often envisioned by his followers as a towering, patriarchal figure with a body composed of living wood, hair made of seasonal leaves, and eyes that burn with a deep, earthy wisdom.
##### Worshippers: 
Druids, rangers, wood elves, hunters, hermits, and frontiersmen who live in harmony with the wild. His places of worship are rarely constructed from stone or mortar; instead, his holy sites are sacred, hidden forest groves, rings of ancient standing stones, or the bases of massive, centuries-old trees where the boundary between mortal lives and pure wilderness is blurred.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets: 
- Preserve the Balance: Stand firmly against any entity that seeks to entirely tame, pave over, or exploit the wild lands, but also prevent nature from overwhelming mortal communities past the point of survival. 
- Kill Only for Need: Respect the flora and fauna of the world; take life only to survive, protect, or nourish yourself, and bitterly oppose those who hunt or clear-cut forests for pure greed or sport. 
- Trust the Natural Cycle: Accept the inevitability of decay, growth, life, and death; understand that natural disasters are not acts of malice, but a necessary cleansing to prepare the soil for new growth.
##### Favoured Weapon:
