##### Description/Background: 
Mystra is the goddess of magic and the literal personification of the Weave, the cosmic conduit that allows mortals to safely interact with raw, volatile magical energy. She is one of the most vital deities in the entire Forgotten Realms pantheon, as her health directly correlates to the stability of magic across the cosmos. The mantle of Mystra has been held by different individuals over cosmic history; the original deity Mystryl sacrificed herself to save the universe from the hubris of Netheril, her successor was slain by Helm during the Time of Troubles, and the mortal mage Midnight ascended to claim the title next. Despite these periodic, reality-shattering periods of silence or Spell plagues caused by her absences, Mystra remains a nurturing yet protective guardian of the arcane. She advocates for the responsible, creative expression of magic, actively opposing those who seek to use the Weave for ultimate, world-ending destruction or corruptive defilement.
##### Worshippers: 
Wizards, sorcerers, bards, sages, magic-users of all disciplines, and researchers of planar lore. Her temples are architectural masterpieces built as grand academies or sprawling laboratories, frequently humming with gentle, harmless magical phenomena and featuring massive glass observatories open to the night sky.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Cultivate the Weave: Treat magic as a beautiful, living art form; always seek to create new spells, uncover lost lore, and broaden the world's understanding of the arcane. 
- Practice Magical Responsibility: Do not hoard knowledge out of pettiness, but never unleash magical forces carelessly or destructively without considering the long-term impact on the fabric of reality. 
- Defend Arcane Freedom: Stand firmly against tyrannical forces or anti-magic zealots who seek to completely suppress or violently censor the natural study and usage of magic.
