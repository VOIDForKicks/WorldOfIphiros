##### Description/Background
Tyr, known as the Maimed God, the Even-Handed, and the Just, is the absolute bedrock of cosmic law, universal justice, and legal order within the Forgotten Realms. Originally an immigrant deity from an outer planar system, Tyr established himself as the premier moral arbiter of Faerûn, founding the Triad alongside Torm and Ilmater to create a united front against chaos, tyranny, and unchecked cruelty. Tyr’s physical appearance is a profound, living testament to his own absolute devotion to his tenets: he is completely blind, having lost his eyes to a tragic misunderstanding during a cosmic trial, and his right hand is missing, cleanly severed by the monstrous extraplanar entity Kezef the Chaos Hound during a courageous act of containment.

Tyr does not view the law as an unyielding weapon to oppress, but as a protective, rational shield meant to guarantee absolute equality, systemic fairness, and security for all civilized mortals. He expects his judges, paladins, and clerics to strip away all personal emotions, family allegiances, and political bribes when delivering a verdict. During the build-up to the cataclysmic Spellplague, Tyr suffered a profound spiritual crisis; manipulated by a web of illusions spun by Cyric, he erroneously suspected his ally Helm of treachery, resulting in a tragic duel that left Helm dead. Devastated by his own failure of objective judgment, Tyr abdicated his divine throne to Torm before perishing during a massive demonic invasion of the upper planes. However, his essential role in the cosmic balance made his absence unsustainable; he was ultimately resurrected, fully reclaiming his mantle as the premier god of noble, blind justice.
##### Worshippers
Order domain clerics, Oath of Devotion and Crown paladins, supreme court judges, urban watch captains, legal barristers, and honest politicians. His temples are grand, sterile, and imposing architectural structures, designed with perfectly symmetrical pillars, high vaulted chambers, and marble statues of blindfolded adjudicators where formal contracts are legally signed and witnessed.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Deliver Blind Judgment: Examine every legal dispute, crime, and moral conflict with absolute, clinical objectivity; never let wealth, high noble birth, or personal friendships tilt the scales of justice.
- Avenge the Lawless Deed: Hunt down vigilantes, corrupt politicians, and chaotic monsters who actively break contracts, exploit loopholes, or use brute force to bypass the codes of civilized society.
- Bear the Scars of Duty: Accept the physical tolls, emotional sacrifices, and difficult burdens that come with enforcing the law; like the Even-Handed, be prepared to give your own hand to preserve the order of the realm.
