##### Description/Background
Umberlee, known throughout the ports of Faerûn as the "Bitch Queen," is the malicious, temperamental, and profoundly greedy goddess of the deep sea. She is the terrifying personification of the ocean’s indifference to mortal life, representing the sudden, crushing power of whirlpools, hurricanes, and the suffocating terror of drowning. Unlike gods who might demand piety for the sake of salvation, Umberlee is worshiped almost exclusively out of raw, desperate fear. She is vain, fickle, and notoriously prone to breaking agreements on a whim, taking a sadistic delight in watching sailors struggle for survival against her violent storms or in the jaws of the apex sea predators she commands.

As one of the "Deities of Fury" serving under the Storm Lord Talos, Umberlee is a destructive force that cannot be reasoned with, only placated. Despite her universal reputation for malice, she is begrudgingly respected in every coastal city in the realms. Even the most devout followers of good-aligned deities will pause to toss gold, jewels, or precious cargo into the surf before setting sail, praying that their "tribute" is sufficient to buy a safe passage through her domain. She is the architect of the weresharks, her most loyal creation, and she views any attempt to tame the sea, bridge her waves, or chart her currents without paying the proper blood-price as a personal affront to her sovereignty.
##### Worshippers
Coastal dwellers who fear the rising tide, terrified sailors, opportunistic pirates, sentient aquatic predators (like krakens and sahuagin), and weresharks. Her "shrines" are typically found in the dankest corners of dockside taverns, underwater grottoes, or sea caves where the salt spray constantly coats the walls, and the air is thick with the smell of wet rot and brine.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Spread the Fear of the Deep: Ensure that every coastal settlement and shipping merchant remembers that the ocean belongs to Umberlee; remind them that safety is a fragile illusion that can be shattered by a single storm.
- Demand the Price of Passage: Never allow a service, blessing, or moment of intercession to pass without exacting a tribute, gold, blood, or cargo must always be surrendered to the Queen of the Depths.
- Invoke the Chaos of the Wave: Do not fear the destruction of the old to make way for the new; champion the chaotic, untameable power of the storm and ensure that those who challenge the sea are left to drown.
##### Favoured Weapon
Trident (known as "Drowning Death")
