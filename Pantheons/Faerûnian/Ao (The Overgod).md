##### Description/Background
Ao is the singular, absolute Overgod of the Realm space crystal sphere. Operating entirely outside the typical framework of mortal worship, prayer, and faith, Ao does not manage individual portfolios like war, love, or nature. Instead, he oversees the deities themselves, establishing the core natural laws of the cosmos and maintaining the strict equilibrium between the forces of law, chaos, good, and evil. He is an enigmatic, distant force who rarely intervenes directly in world events, stepping into the material or divine realms only when the cosmic order faces total systemic annihilation.

His most definitive and catastrophic intervention occurred during the Time of Troubles. When the ambitious gods Bane and Myrkul stole the Tablets of Fate, cosmic artefacts detailing the balance of the multiverse and the precise assignments of the gods, Ao stripped the entire pantheon of their divine immortality. He cast them down into the mortal world as fragile, vulnerable avatars, forcing them to experience the chaotic consequences of their neglect. He decreed that a deity's power would henceforth be bound directly to the devotion and size of their mortal following, transforming the gods from entitled cosmic tyrants into responsible guardians of their portfolios. Once order was restored by a mortal champion, Ao shattered the old tablets, re-elevating the surviving gods and cementing his position as the ultimate, unyielding authority above all creation.
##### Worshippers
Ao has no active mortal worshippers, cults, or organized churches, as he consciously ignores all mortal prayers and grants no spells to traditional clerics. However, rare, isolated philosophers, planar astronomers, high-level arcanists, and extreme order domain thinkers acknowledge his existence, cataloguing his rare historical interventions on stone tablets within deep, silent mountain observatories.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Uphold the Great Equilibrium: Act as an impartial steward of the world; prevent any single alignment, faction, or extraplanar force from establishing complete, unchecked hegemony over the realm.
- Enforce Divine Boundaries: Actively hunt down, seal, or banish rogue avatars, rogue demigods, and mortal spellcasters who attempt to usurp or disrupt the established cosmic hierarchy.
- Accept Your Mortal Charge: Understand that power is a profound responsibility given to protect and guide; fulfill your chosen duties to your community and party with absolute, unshakeable discipline.
