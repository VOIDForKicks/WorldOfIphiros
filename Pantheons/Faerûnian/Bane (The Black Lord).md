##### Description/Background: 
Bane is the absolute embodiment of pure, calculating tyranny, malicious conquest, and iron-fisted oppression. Alongside Myrkul and Bhaal, he was originally a mortal adventurer who ascended to godhood by striking a deal with Jergal, forming the infamous trio known as the Dead Three. Unlike chaotic warmongers who destroy for the sake of violence, Bane seeks total, structured submission. He desires to break the will of the multiverse and bend it beneath his absolute authority. He is an icy, terrifyingly disciplined mastermind who rules through fear, strategic intimidation, and military dominance. Though he has been killed in cosmic conflicts before, most notably during the Time of Troubles, his malevolent spirit inevitably claws its way back to the material plane, working through fanatical cults and military regimes to re-establish his iron grip over Faerûn.
##### Worshippers: 
Dictators, ruthless generals, corrupt magistrates, oppressive conquerors, and disciplined mercenaries who view compassion as a sickness. His temples are built like imposing, black stone fortresses, designed with brutalist architecture to make anyone who enters feel small, helpless, and completely subjugated.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets: 
- Rule through Strength: Might makes right. Those who have the power to rule carry the absolute duty to crush the weak and enforce order. 
- Allow No Dissent: Smite open rebellion and hidden subversion with equal, unyielding severity; absolute obedience to the chain of command is non-negotiable. 
- Spread Hatred and Strife: Break the alliances of your enemies by sowing distrust, hatred, and factional war, ensuring they are too broken to stand against your eventual conquest.
