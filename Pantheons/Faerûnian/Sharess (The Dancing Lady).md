##### Description/Background
Sharess is the ultimate patron of physical ecstasy, uninhibited joy, and deep sensory indulgence within the Forgotten Realms. She is a fascinating, complex deity whose existence has shifted dramatically across history. Originally a native Mulhorandi goddess of cats named Bast, she grew bored of her pantheon’s rigid nature and travelled across Faerûn, absorbing various foreign portfolios of love, pleasure, and performance. Her name changed to Sharess, but her journey into pure hedonism eventually led her into a dark, toxic trap. During her pursuit of ultimate sensory experiences, she wandered into the Abyss and was seduced, corrupted, and nearly consumed by the demon queen Graz'zt, transforming her into a tragic, hollow creature of dark lust and dependency.

Her salvation came during the Time of Troubles. Sune, the Goddess of Beauty, launched a daring divine intervention, tearing Sharess away from the Prince of Pleasure's demonic grasp and completely purging the fiendish taint from her spirit. Reborn and restored, Sharess is now a fiercely independent, benevolent entity who champions the pursuit of pleasure, provided it is entirely consensual, joyful, and free from the cruel control of others. She is often depicted as an incredibly alluring, shape-shifting woman with feline traits, such as amber cat-eyes, sleek ears, or a tail, who moves with flawless, hypnotic grace. She treats life as a beautiful, continuous festival, urging mortals to enjoy the warmth of a touch, the sweetness of a vintage wine, and the thrill of a late-night dance before their mortal time fades.
##### Worshippers
Courtesans, tavern keepers, bards of the College of Glamour, hedonistic nobles, festival planners, perfumers, and high-society socialites. Her holy sanctuaries are not traditional churches but opulent, cushion-filled lounges, perfumed bathhouses, and lively music halls where open-minded guests gather to share fine foods, trade secrets, and explore physical romance without societal judgment.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Savour Every Sensation: Treat your physical senses as holy instruments; indulge in beautiful music, delicious feasts, and intimate connections, encouraging others to break free from their rigid inhibitions.
- Protect Mutual Joy: Ensure that all forms of pleasure, passion, and love remain entirely consensual and liberating; actively dismantle rings of trafficking, forced bondage, or emotional coercion.
- Live in the Moment: Do not waste your life obsessing over distant historical regrets or anxious futures; celebrate the absolute present with laughter, dance, and unyielding optimism.
#### Favoured Weapon
