##### Description/Background: 
Shar is the ancient, bitter goddess of darkness, night, and loss. Alongside her twin sister Selûne (the goddess of light and the moon), Shar is one of the two primordial deities who manifested from the raw chaos that existed before Toril was fully formed. While her sister represents creation and light, Shar embodies the comforting, cold void that existed before existence itself, a void she desperately wishes to return the multiverse to. She feeds on mortal grief, heartbreak, and bitter resentment, offering her followers the gift of total forgetfulness to ease their psychological trauma, effectively hollowing out their souls to make them loyal agents of her will. Shar is the architect of the Shadow Weave, a dark mirror to Mystra’s magical Weave designed to subvert and control arcane energy from the shadows. Cynical, patient, and deeply vindictive, she wages an eternal, cosmic war against her twin sister and all forms of cosmic light, plotting to pull down the stars and drape the material plane in permanent night.
##### Worshippers: 
The broken-hearted, vengeful outcasts, assassins, shadow magic sorcerers, and nihilistic cultists who seek to forget past traumas. Her temples are subterranean sanctuaries completely devoid of natural light, often heavily guarded by her elite monastic order, the Dark Moon, and featuring deep, reflective pools of black liquid used for scrying.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets: 
- Embrace the Dark: Reveal your true self only under the cover of night; use darkness as both a physical shield and a weapon to unnerve those who rely on the false comfort of light. 
- Nurture the Loss: Do not heal from your grief, but channel your heartbreak, bitterness, and sorrow into absolute, focused devotion; let your pain drive you to tear down the achievements of the joyful. 
- Keep the Secrets Unbroken: Guard the mysteries of the night with your life; reveal nothing to outsiders, and use deception, misdirection, and sabotage to keep your enemies blind to your true goals.
##### Favoured Weapon: 
Dagger or Dagger (wielded as a manifestation of a concentrated shadow)
