Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background: 
Auril is a fickle, vain, and cold-hearted deity who embodies the winter season's unyielding cruelty. Known also as the Frostmaiden or Icedawn, she demands worship not out of love, but through sheer terror. Mortals placate her with sacrifices to survive freezing blizzards and devastating winters. She is traditionally counted among the Gods of Fury alongside Umberlee, Malar, and Talos. Auril delights in freezing things at the height of their absolute beauty to preserve them for eternity in flawless, unmoving ice. She lacks human empathy, viewing warmth, compassion, and passion as weaknesses that must be extinguished by the biting wind. Her power and isolation reached an iconic peak when she withdrew to the frozen northern edge of the world in Icewind Dale, weaving a localized cosmic curse to prevent the sun from rising and casting the region into an unending, twilight winter.
##### Worshippers: 
Arctic survivalists, desperate commoners trying to survive the winter, evil druids, frost giants, and cold-insulated humanoids like yetis or gnolls. Her shrines are stark, minimalist structures built entirely from blocks of glacial ice or carved directly into the sides of wind-beaten frozen peaks.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Cruelty of Winter: Let your heart grow as cold and unyielding as glacial ice. Show no mercy or pity to the weak, for surviving the frost requires absolute ruthlessness. 
- Preserve the Cold: Extinguish unnecessary warmth, comfort, and fire wherever it fosters complacency. The natural state of the world at its most beautiful is frozen and still. 
- Command through Fear: Teach mortals to respect the freezing dark. Let them know that their survival through the harsh winter months is a direct privilege granted only by your divine forbearance.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Battleaxe or Morningstar (wielded like a jagged shard of ice)
