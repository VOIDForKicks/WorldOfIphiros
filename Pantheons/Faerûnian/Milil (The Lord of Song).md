##### Description/Background
Milil, known as the Guardian of Singers and the Lord of Song, is the divine personification of musical inspiration, lyrical elegance, and performance art within the Forgotten Realms. Operating alongside Deneir as a loyal hand to Oghma, Milil does not merely represent the technical act of playing an instrument; he embodies the raw charisma, emotional resonance, and creative spark that turns basic chords into a timeless masterpiece. He is depicted as a stunningly handsome, youthful man radiating an aura of absolute confidence, wit, and vibrant energy. To Milil, a beautifully composed song or a perfectly delivered speech is the highest form of spiritual alignment.

Milil is a deeply benevolent, forward-looking deity who believes that music and performance are essential tools for elevating the mortal spirit, building communities, and preserving history through oral tradition. He encourages continuous experimentation, praise, and public performance, advising his followers to never hide their talents out of fear or false humility. However, his unshakeable desire for aesthetic perfection and adoration can sometimes manifest as vanity or a total disregard for practical survival. He expects his clergy to confront the darkest corners of the world with an uplifting melody, believing that true artistic beauty can disarm monsters, shatter curses, and outlast the cruellest empires.
##### Worshippers
Bards of all colleges, virtuoso instrumentalists, stage actors, court diplomats, light domain clerics, and traveling troubadours. His holy spaces are architectural marvels of acoustics, grand open-air amphitheatres, wood-panelled concert halls, and lively tavern stages where the air resonates with vocal harmonies and the continuous plucking of lutes.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Perform with Pure Passion: Treat every song, speech, or battle cry as a sacred offering; never perform half-heartedly or let your personal anxieties dampen the beauty of your craft.
- Inspire the Weary Mind: Use your voice, your poetry, and your charisma to uplift companions facing profound despair; let your music be a beacon of light in the deepest dungeons.
- Cultivate Artistic Talent: Actively mentor young musicians, preserve fading historical ballads, and fiercely protect theatres, instruments, and artists from targeted destruction or suppression.
##### Favoured Weapon
