##### Description/Background
Torm, known as the True, the Loyal Fury, and the Hand of Righteousness, is the absolute divine epitome of chivalry, duty, and unyielding loyalty within the Forgotten Realms. Originally a mortal hero who served as a champion of law and order, Torm ascended to divinity by proving that his personal honour and commitment to his oaths were completely unbreakable. Unlike deities who demand blind obedience to abstract law, Torm emphasizes the moral responsibility of power. He expects his followers to stand as protective shields for the weak, to execute the laws of just rulers with absolute fairness, and to maintain the highest possible standards of personal integrity.

Torm’s defining moment of cosmic glory occurred during the Time of Troubles over the skies of Tantras. When the malignant god Bane unleashed a devastating campaign of destruction across the continent, Torm chose to make the ultimate sacrifice to protect his mortal faithful. Knowing it would consume his own life essence, Torm absorbed the collective souls of his entire worshipper base in Tantras to fuel a monumental avatar battle, successfully striking down and destroying the Lord of Tyranny. Impressed by this flawless demonstration of divine duty, the Overgod Ao completely resurrected Torm shortly after the crisis, restoring his status. Following the chaotic events of the Spellplague and the subsequent death of Tyr, Torm ascended even further, taking his place as the supreme leader of the House of the Triad and a Greater Deity of justice.
##### Worshippers
Holy paladins (especially Oath of Devotion and Oath of the Crown), lawful fighters, royal bodyguards, city magistrates, boundary wardens, and order domain clerics. His temples are highly functional, immaculate stone fortresses, military academies, and polished marble courts where the daily routine includes rigorous martial training, legal study, and public service.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Fulfil the Oath with Honour: Your spoken word is an absolute, sacred bond; execute your promises, contracts, and duties with flawless precision, regardless of the personal cost.
- Stand as the Shield: Actively protect the vulnerable, the innocent, and the structures of just societies from extraplanar terrors, lawless bandits, and tyrannical conquerors.
- Maintain Perfect Integrity: Never look away from internal corruption or permit dishonourable behaviour within your own ranks; a champion of Torm must remain an unblemished example of righteousness.
##### Favoured Weapon
Greatsword or Longsword (styled to feature an engraving of a clenched gauntlet on the pommel)
