##### Mythology: 
Forgotten Realms (D&D Lore)

Storms, Destruction, Lightning, Earthquakes, and Rebellion
##### D&D Domains: 
Tempest
##### Alignment: 
Chaotic Evil
##### Holy Symbol: 
3 bolts in a W pattern, or three jagged lightning bolts radiating from a central point arranged in a 'W' shape
##### Godly Level:
Greater Deity
##### Description/Background: 
Talos, known as the Stormlord or the Destroyer, is the raging, short-tempered personification of nature's sheer, unbridled violence. He stands as the undisputed leader of the Gods of Fury, commanding an aggressive, destructive portfolio that covers devastating hurricanes, shattering earthquakes, fires, and volcanic eruptions. Talos is entirely motivated by a desire to tear down order and display his supreme dominance over the material plane, demanding worship through raw terror and appeasement. He harbours a deep, ancient hatred for civilized progress, taking immense pleasure in reducing sprawling cities, grand monuments, and carefully structured kingdoms to smoking ruins in mere moments. When he manifests an avatar, he appears as a broad-shouldered, wild-eyed warrior clad in jagged, black-iron scale armour, sporting a dark eyepatch over an empty socket that crackles with raw, chaotic lightning.
##### Worshippers: 
Pirates, chaotic marauders, brigands, mad arsonists, apocalyptic cultists, and isolated coast-dwellers trying desperately to appease his anger. His shrines are never built inside city walls; instead, they are rough, roofless structures made of scorched stone or jagged obsidian, intentionally erected on storm-beaten cliffs, high mountain peaks, or active volcanic rifts where lightning strikes frequently.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Unleash the Storm: Do not hoard your power or stay your hand; let your magic and physical might crash down upon your foes with the sudden, overwhelming violence of a tempest.
- Tear Down the False: Smash through rigid laws, fragile hierarchies, and the permanent stone structures of civilization, reminding mortals that everything they build can be broken in an instant.
- Rule by Terror: Teach the weak to fear the sky and the shaking earth; ensure they understand that their survival through a storm is a direct consequence of their offerings and submission.
#### Favoured Weapon: 
Spear or Trident (wielded like a lightning bolt)
