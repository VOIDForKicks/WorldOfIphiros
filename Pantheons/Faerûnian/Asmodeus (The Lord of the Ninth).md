##### Description/Background: 
Asmodeus is the undisputed master of the Nine Hells of Baator and the supreme ruler of all devils. Within the Forgotten Realms and broader D&D canon, his status shifted dramatically over the centuries from an archdevil of unmatched political cunning to a true, fully recognized Greater Deity. Brilliant, patient, and utterly ruthless, Asmodeus operates on a cosmic timeline, manipulating mortals and gods alike with airtight legal pacts, Faustian bargains, and masterfully woven deceptions. He presents himself as a necessary evil, maintaining that his brutal hierarchy and the eternal Blood War against the chaotic demons of the Abyss are the only things preventing the utter destruction of the multiverse. He is a master diplomat and orator, possessing an unparalleled charm that masks a terrifyingly cruel, calculating nature. His true form is rumoured to be a colossal, wounded serpent resting at the bottom of the deepest rift in Nessus, though he almost always projects the avatar of a flawless, impeccably dressed tiefling or humanoid lord carrying the Ruby Rod of Asmodeus.
##### Worshippers: 
Corrupt politicians, authoritarian rulers, dark cultists, power-hungry wizards, and ruthless mercenaries. His temples are never built openly in righteous lands; instead, his faith operates through secret societies, hidden subterranean shrines beneath seats of government, or grand, decadent estates where influential elites trade away their souls for mortal authority.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets: 
- Uphold the Contract: Never break the literal letter of a written agreement or oath, but use every loophole, technicality, and ambiguity to maximize your own leverage. 
- Enforce Hierarchy: Respect authority and enforce total obedience from those below you; chaos is a disease, and submission to power is the natural state of the multiverse. 
- Exploit Weakness: Identify the secret desires, vices, and flaws of your enemies, using them as leverage to slowly bend their will to your own purposes.
