##### Description/Background: 
The Raven Queen is a mysterious, enigmatic entity who rules from her fortress, the Memories of the Shadowfell. Originally a powerful mortal sorceress queen from the Feywild, she attempted a dangerous ritual to ascend to godhood and save her people, but the spell was sabotaged, tearing her soul apart and casting her deep into the plane of shadow. There, she reconstituted her essence into a unique divine force that completely bypassed the traditional system of mortal worship. She does not judge souls like Kelemvor, nor does she crave murder like Bhaal; instead, she is obsessed with the transitionary moment of death itself and the collection of intense mortal memories. She watches over the threads of fate, ensuring that the natural boundary between life and death is strictly maintained. The Raven Queen detests intelligent undead, particularly liches, who seek to cheat fate and lock away their memories eternally. She operates through her loyal, supernatural agents, the Shadar-kai, who harvest emotional relics and memories from across the Material Plane to fuel her dark, sorrowful realm.
##### Worshippers: 
Shadar-kai, hexblade warlocks, grave domain clerics, doomguide paladins, and those who seek comfort in the stoic finality of fate. Her shrines are rarely constructed in bright, bustling cities, manifesting instead as lonely black stone altars placed in misty swamplands, ancient crypts, or deep within the naturally shifting glooms of the Shadowfell.

##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets: 
- Accept the Thread of Fate: Do not delay the natural arrival of death for yourself or others; the thread must snap when the cosmic weave dictates. 
- Purge the Undying: Hunt down and destroy liches, vampires, and other abominations that use magic to artificially extend their lifespans or hoard their memories beyond the grave. 
- Gather the Lost Memories: Treat powerful emotions, final words, and tragic histories as sacred text; collect them and offer them up to the Queen to preserve the legacy of the fallen.

##### Favoured Weapon: 
Scythe or Sickle
