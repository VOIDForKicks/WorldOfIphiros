##### Description/Background
Selûne (pronounced _seh-LOON-ay_), the Our Lady of Silver, is one of the oldest and most foundational primordial deities of the Forgotten Realms. Born alongside her twin sister, Shar (the Goddess of Dark), from the raw primordial essence left behind after Lord Ao created the cosmos, Selûne represents the silver light of life, growth, and navigational guidance. Together, the sisters breathed life into the physical world, but a bitter, eternal schism erupted when Selûne moved to create a sun to warm the fragile, freezing mortal races. Infuriated by the encroachment of light into her pristine canvas of total darkness, Shar attacked. This cosmic war of the twins fundamentally shaped the planes, culminating in Selûne ripping out a portion of her own divine heart to hurl at her sister, an act of pure self-sacrifice that ignited the sun and created Mystryl, the first Goddess of Magic.

Selûne’s power waxes and wanes alongside the physical phases of the moon. She is a deeply maternal, compassionate deity who shines her silver light as a beacon of hope for lost travellers, weary sailors, and outcasts hiding in the night. She is the fierce protector of good-aligned lycanthropes who seek to manage their bestial curses with grace, standing in absolute opposition to the bloodthirsty savagery of Malar. Her ancient feud with Shar continues to rage across the material plane; while her sister seeks to plunge the multiverse into a state of numb, nihilistic amnesia, Selûne commands her followers to meet the dark with unyielding light, optimism, and an appreciation for the natural cycles of life.
##### Worshippers
Navigators, sailors, twilight domain sentinels, good-aligned lycanthropes (like weretigers or werebears), astronomers, diviners, and bards who play under the night sky. Her temples are typically open-air stone circles built on high mountain peaks, coastal cliffs, or tall white towers featuring expansive mirrors designed to reflect and focus the pure rays of the full moon into silver-glowing fonts.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Guide the Lost Through Dark: Act as a living beacon for those lost in physical darkness or emotional despair; help travellers find their footing and navigate the uncertain pathways of life.
- Embrace the Shifting Cycle: Accept that life, like the moon, features natural phases of light, shadow, and renewal; do not fear temporary setbacks or darkness, for the silver light always returns.
- Confront the Void: Stand as an unyielding wall against the numbing shadow of Shar and the predatory bloodlust of Malar; hunt down malevolent shadowy beasts and protect the vulnerable from nocturnal terrors.
##### Favoured Weapon
Heavy Mace (styled to resemble a stylized crescent moon or a sceptre topped with a glowing silver orb)
