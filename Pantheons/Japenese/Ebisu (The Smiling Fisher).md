##### Godly Level
Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background
Ebisu is a beloved deity of the common folk, particularly those who labour on the open sea. Often depicted as a cheerful, smiling figure clad in traditional hunting or fishing garb, he represents the rewards of honest effort and a sunny disposition. Legend tells of his birth as a child born without bones, who was cast adrift at sea, only to overcome his misfortune through perseverance and joy. Because of this, he is the patron of the resilient and the unlucky who find their way through grit and a steady heart. He does not offer shortcuts to riches; he offers the catch of the day to those who wake before the dawn and treat the waters with respect.
##### Worshippers
Fishermen, sailors, small-scale merchants, those who value humility, and individuals overcoming hardship through simple, steady work.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Find Joy in Labor: Treat your daily work as a sacred act; find contentment in the rhythm of your tasks and the satisfaction of a job completed well. 
- Cultivate Humility: Success is earned, not owed; remain grounded and kind to others, regardless of how much fortune you have found.
- Respect the Sea: Honour the natural world that provides for your survival; never take more than you need, and treat the environment with care.
