##### Mythology
Japanese
##### Domain (Mythological)
Guidance, Crossroads, and Paths
##### D&D Domains
Travel, Protection, Truth, Knowledge
##### Alignment
Lawful Neutral
##### Holy Symbol
A spear or a bridge
##### Godly Level
Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background
The deity who guards the crossroads of the world and provides guidance to those who seek a new path. He is recognized by his fierce expression and distinctive physical traits.
##### Worshippers
Explorers, pilgrims, and those embarking on major life changes.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Guide the Lost: Show the way to those who are blind to their path and protect the vulnerable along dangerous routes. 
- Respect the Choice: Understand that every fork in the road shapes destiny; honor the weight of choices made at the crossroads.
- Stand with Courage: Face the unknown with unwavering resolve when navigating new horizons or defending a threshold.
##### Favoured Weapon
Spear
