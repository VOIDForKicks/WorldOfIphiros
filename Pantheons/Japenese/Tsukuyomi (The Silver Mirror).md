##### Description/Background
Tsukuyomi is the god of the moon, born from the purification of Izanagi after his return from the underworld. As the twin of the sun goddess Amaterasu, he rules the night with a calm, unwavering precision. While Amaterasu represents the vibrant life and activity of the day, Tsukuyomi governs the quiet, reflective hours. He is a deity of intense discipline, cycles, and the measured flow of time. Legend tells of his separation from Amaterasu after a brutal act of judgment toward the goddess of food, highlighting his uncompromising devotion to purity and his sometimes cold, detached sense of justice. He is the guardian of the secrets revealed only in darkness and the silent watcher of the changing tides.
##### Worshippers
Chronologists, astronomers, navigators, mages who draw power from the night, and those who value cold logic and precise order above all else.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Respect the Cycle: All things have their time; understand that there is a season for action and a necessary season for quiet, dark introspection.
- Maintain the Order: Chaos is the enemy of the universe’s rhythm; ensure that laws, traditions, and the natural order are upheld with unwavering commitment.
- Observe and Reflect: The truth is often hidden in the stillness of the night; practice observation and patience, and let your judgment be as clear and cold as the moonlight.
