A wooden mallet (the _uchide no kozuchi_) resting atop two rice bales.
##### Godly Level
Minor Deity
##### Description/Background
Daikokuten is one of the Seven Lucky Gods, a benevolent figure who presides over the harvest and the accumulation of wealth. He is often depicted as a smiling, sturdy man wearing a flat cap, carrying a sack of treasures over his shoulder and his signature mallet in his hand. The mallet represents the power to manifest abundance, it is said that a single tap can bring forth precious gems, food, or coin. Daikokuten is not a god of greedy accumulation, but of the prosperity that comes from honest, hard work and careful stewardship of resources. He represents the comfort of a full larder and the security of a thriving community.
##### Worshippers
Merchants, farmers, bankers, guild leaders, and anyone seeking to improve their lot in life through diligence and sound investment.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Cultivate Prosperity: Foster growth in your community by supporting trade and ensuring that the harvest, literal or figurative, is shared fairly. 
- Work with Integrity: Wealth gained through trickery or theft is brittle; true fortune is built on reliable labour and honest dealings.
- Practice Prudence: Manage your resources wisely; a stockpile kept for a lean year is the greatest act of foresight one can perform.
