Thunder, Lightning, Storms, and Protection

Tempest, Protection, Chaos, Air

Chaotic Neutral
##### Holy Symbol
A circle of drums, or a single drum adorned with a _tomoe_ (a comma-like swirl symbol).
##### Godly Level
Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background
Raijin is the formidable and fearsome god of thunder and lightning, often depicted as a muscular, oni-like figure with a wild expression, surrounded by a ring of taiko drums that he strikes to create the rolling sound of thunder. He is a raw, elemental force, violent and unpredictable, much like the storms he commands. While his appearance and his destructive power can inspire terror, he is also a guardian who protects the sanctity of shrines and, as legend tells, has been known to turn his fury against invaders to protect the lands of Japan. He represents the duality of nature: the destructive potential of the storm and the necessary, life-giving rain that follows.
##### Worshippers
Warriors seeking raw power, sailors, farmers praying for rain, and those who respect the untamable energy of nature.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Respect the Power: Acknowledge that the natural world possesses forces far greater than mortal control; treat the storm with reverence. 
- Be Decisive: Like a lightning strike, act with clarity, speed, and overwhelming conviction when the moment calls for it.
- Protect the Sacred: Just as Raijin guards the temple gates, defend your community and your home from threats that would seek to destroy or corrupt them.
##### Favoured Weapon
Warhammer (representing the drum-beating hammers)
