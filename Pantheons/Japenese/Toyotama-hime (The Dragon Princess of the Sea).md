##### Description/Background
Toyotama-hime, also known as Luxuriant-Jewel-Princess, is a beautiful sea goddess and the daughter of the ocean deity Ryujin. She dwells within a magnificent undersea palace and is tied deeply to the mysteries, bounties, and shifting environments of the ocean waters. Famously, she wedded a mortal hunter named Hoori and brought him great luck, but later returned forever to the sea after he broke a sacred promise not to look upon her true form during childbirth. She represents the profound grace of the ocean, the bridging of different worlds, and the absolute sanctity of keeping one's word.
##### Worshippers
Coastal communities, pearl divers, expectant mothers seeking safe delivery, fishermen looking for good fortune, and creatures of mixed heritage.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Honour Your Oaths: A broken vow erodes trust like waves against sand; let your word be as unbreakable as the deepest stone.
- Bridge the Worlds: Seek to foster understanding and harmony between separate cultures, environments, and peoples.
- Nurture the Vulnerable: Shield new life, fragile beginnings, and the helpless from the harsh storms of the world.
