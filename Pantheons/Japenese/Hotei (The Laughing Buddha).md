##### D&D Domains
Joy, Life, Peace, Community
##### Alignment
Neutral Good
##### Holy Symbol
A large, overflowing linen sack (representing the treasures of his generosity) or a rotund, smiling face.
##### Godly Level
Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background
Hotei is one of the Seven Lucky Gods, recognizable by his wide, infectious grin and his generous, round belly. He is a wandering monk who carries his belongings in a cloth sack, but he never seems to empty it; it is said to contain endless gifts for the needy, the hungry, and the heartbroken. Hotei is a deity of profound contentment, he teaches that true happiness is not found in the grand conquest of kingdoms, but in the simple, daily appreciation of existence. He is a patron of the carefree and the compassionate, known for laughing at the absurdities of life and encouraging his followers to shed the heavy burdens of greed and status.
##### Worshippers
Monks, philanthropists, tavern owners, travellers, children, and those who seek peace in a chaotic world.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Spread Contentment: Find joy in what you have, and help others find it, too; a cheerful spirit is the greatest gift you can offer a weary soul.
- Give Freely: Do not cling to material wealth; true abundance comes from sharing what you possess with those who are in need.
- Laugh at Adversity: Do not let misfortune harden your heart; maintain your sense of humour, for it is the best shield against bitterness.
##### Favoured Weapon
Quarterstaff (used for support during his wanderings)
