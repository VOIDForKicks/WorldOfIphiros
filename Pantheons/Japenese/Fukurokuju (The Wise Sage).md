Minor Deity
##### Description/Background
Fukurokuju is one of the Seven Lucky Gods, recognizable by his exceptionally high forehead, which symbolizes his vast, accumulated wisdom. He is an ancient, meditative figure who is said to possess the ability to read the souls of men and understand the intricate patterns of the universe. Unlike gods of passion or war, Fukurokuju represents the virtues of a long, well-lived life: patience, deep learning, and the calm clarity that comes with age. He is often depicted in the company of a crane or a turtle, emphasizing his connection to the timeless, enduring nature of truth. He teaches that true wealth is not coin, but the breadth of one’s knowledge and the peace found in a steady, thoughtful spirit.
##### Worshippers
Scholars, sages, librarians, long-lived races (like elves or dwarves), teachers, and those who seek to unravel the profound mysteries of existence.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Seek Enlightenment: Never stop learning; the pursuit of truth is a lifelong journey that requires constant study and quiet contemplation. 
- Practice Patience: True wisdom cannot be rushed; allow time for understanding to ripen before acting upon your convictions.
- Value Enduring Truths: Focus on what lasts, the knowledge shared, the lives touched, and the insights gained, rather than the fleeting distractions of the present moment.
