##### Description/Background
Izanagi is the primordial progenitor of the Japanese pantheon. Alongside his wife, Izanami, he stirred the chaos of the beginning of the world with his jewelled spear, forming the first islands of the earth. His story is one of profound tragedy and cosmic duty; after the death of Izanami in the underworld, his failed attempt to retrieve her led to the separation of life and death. Following his return, he performed the first rite of purification, giving birth to the highest gods of the heavens, including the sun, moon, and storm deities, from the washings of his own body. He represents the act of bringing order from chaos, the heavy burden of creation, and the necessity of ritual purity to maintain the world’s balance.
##### Worshippers
Leaders, founders of cities, high priests, those dedicated to rites of purification, and anyone who feels the weight of stewardship over life and land.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Steward the World: Creation is a sacred, ongoing act; protect the natural order and strive to improve the world you inhabit. 
- Uphold Purity: Maintain the clarity of your spirit and your purpose; cleanse yourself of corruption and decay so that you may act with a steady hand.
- Seek Order in Chaos: When the world falls into turmoil, be the one who brings stability, logic, and structure to the confusion.
