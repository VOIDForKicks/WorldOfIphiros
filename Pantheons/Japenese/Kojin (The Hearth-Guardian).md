Neutral Good
##### Holy Symbol 
Three stones arranged in a triangle (the traditional hearth fire) or a blackened cooking pot.
##### Godly Level 
Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background 
Kojin is the protective deity of the kitchen and the hearth. He is a fiery and sometimes volatile spirit who demands cleanliness and respect in the space where food, the source of life, is prepared. He guards the home against misfortune and ensures that the fire never goes out, provided the inhabitants honour him. Though he is a household god, he is a force to be reckoned with; neglect or disrespect toward the hearth can invite his wrath, but proper care invites his blessings of warmth, plenty, and safety.
##### Worshippers 
Cooks, homesteaders, families, and those who treat the home as the most sacred of sanctuaries.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Tend the Flame: Keep the fires of your home burning and your cooking spaces clean, for the hearth is the centre of life and health.
- Protect the Domestic: Defend the sanctity of the home and the family; these are the foundations upon which a stable society is built.
- Offer Hospitality: Provide food and warmth to the hungry and the lost; a house that refuses to feed the stranger loses its blessing.
##### Favoured Weapon 
Dagger (used for kitchen tasks)
