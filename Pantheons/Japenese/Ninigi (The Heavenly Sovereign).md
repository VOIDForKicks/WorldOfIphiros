##### Description/Background 
Ninigi-no-Mikoto is the grandson of the sun goddess Amaterasu, sent from the heavens to pacify the earthly realm and establish the foundation of governance. He brought with him the wisdom to manage the harvest and the authority to unify the people. Ninigi represents the ideal of a just and divinely guided leader. He is not a tyrant, but a steward whose power is derived from his duty to his people and his connection to the celestial order.
##### Worshippers 
Monarchs, administrators, city planners, and those who strive to bring order and prosperity to troubled lands.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Lead with Duty: Power is a responsibility, not a prize; use your authority to uplift the land and the people under your care. 
- Foster Stability: Create laws and structures that provide safety and fairness for all, so that prosperity can take root.
- Be a Reflection: A leader should reflect the light of the divine in their actions; maintain personal integrity to act as a beacon for others.
