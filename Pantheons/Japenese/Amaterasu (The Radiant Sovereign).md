##### Description/Background
Amaterasu Omikami is the supreme sun goddess of the Shinto pantheon and the mythic ancestor of the Japanese Imperial line. Born from the left eye of the primordial creator god Izanagi during his ritual purification, she was granted rule over Takamagahara (the High Celestial Plain). She represents the vital, life-giving warmth of the sun, order, purity, and the foundational structure of civilization.

Her most famous myth centres on her tumultuous relationship with her brother, the volatile storm god Susanoo. After his destructive, chaotic rampages defiled her sacred rice fields and palace, a deeply grieved and offended Amaterasu retreated into the Heavenly Rock Cave (_Amano-Iwayato_), sealing the entrance with a massive boulder. Her self-exile plunged both heaven and earth into absolute, terrifying darkness, allowing evil spirits to run rampant. To lure her out, the other deities staged a raucous, joyful festival outside the cave entrance. Curious about the laughter, Amaterasu peeked out and saw her own breath-taking reflection in a sacred bronze mirror hung from a tree. Fascinated, she stepped forward, allowing the gods to pull her out and seal the cave behind her, successfully restoring light and warmth to the cosmos.
##### Worshippers
Emperors, high-ranking nobility, samurai of devotion, light domain clerics, rice farmers, and protectors of the realm who stand against fiends and undead. Her temples are grand wooden structures featuring towering, bright red _torii_ gates, designed with immaculate simplicity and built on high, east-facing plains where the first morning rays illuminate the central, highly polished bronze mirror.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Restore the Light: Stand as an unyielding beacon against corruption, darkness, and despair; actively purge the world of monsters and malevolence that thrive in the shadows.
- Cultivate Harmony: Nurture the community, respect societal order, and protect the foundational elements of civilization, agriculture, and life-giving prosperity.
- Maintain Radiance: Keep your soul, honour, and weapons pure; avoid chaotic impulses and treat your station with the absolute dignity befitting a servant of the heaven-ruler.
