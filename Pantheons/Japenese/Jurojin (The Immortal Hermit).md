##### D&D Domains 
Knowledge, Time, Peace, Patience

Lawful Good
##### Holy Symbol 
A peach (symbol of immortality) or a walking staff with a scroll attached.
##### Godly Level 
Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background 
Jurojin is the god of longevity and the personification of a life lived in accordance with the Tao. He is typically depicted as an old man with a long white beard, carrying a staff and a scroll that contains the lifespan of every living creature. He is a quiet, contemplative deity who wanders the world observing the natural order. Jurojin does not seek to intervene in the grand battles of the cosmos; rather, he encourages the pursuit of health, a peaceful mind, and the wisdom to accept the flow of time.
##### Worshippers 
Healers, elderly sages, those recovering from severe illness, and people seeking to prolong their natural life through healthy living and moderation.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Seek Balance: A long life is wasted if not lived with purpose and moderation; nurture your health and cultivate a calm spirit.
- Observe the Flow: Understand that time is a river; do not fight against the inevitable, but learn to navigate its currents with grace.
- Share Your Wisdom: Knowledge gained through long experience should be passed on to the young, ensuring the continuity of wisdom.
