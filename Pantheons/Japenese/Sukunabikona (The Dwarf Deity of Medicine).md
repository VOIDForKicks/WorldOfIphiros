##### Description/Background
Sukunabikona is a diminutive yet incredibly vital deity of healing, magic, and agriculture. Often described as a tiny dwarf god who arrived on the shores riding a moth or a small boat made from a seed pod, he partnered with the great deity Okuninushi to help build, cultivate, and pacify the world. He is credited with discovering the healing arts, identifying the medicinal properties of various plants, and creating hot springs (_onsen_) to cure ailments and wash away fatigue. Despite his small stature, his immense wisdom and intelligence allowed him to tame wild lands and provide civilization with the tools to survive plague and hardship.
##### Worshippers
Healers, herbalists, doctors, brewer-monks, hot spring keepers, and small-statured individuals who rely on wit and knowledge over physical strength.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Heal the Ailing: No sickness is too small or too massive to fight; use every tool at your disposal to restore vigour to the suffering. 
- Nurture Small Beginnings: Great power and profound change often sprout from the smallest seeds; protect and cultivate what others overlook.
- Share the Bounty: Knowledge of medicine and agriculture belongs to all living beings; never hoard the secrets of health for personal greed.
