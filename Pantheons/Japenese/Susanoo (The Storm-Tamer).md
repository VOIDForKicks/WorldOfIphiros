##### Description/Background
Susanoo is the tempestuous and formidable storm god, brother to Amaterasu and Tsukuyomi. Born of Izanagi’s nostrils during his purification, Susanoo is a creature of raw, untamed emotion, by turns a destructive force and a glorious hero. He is best known for his exile from the heavens after his raucous behaviour, followed by his legendary redemption in the mortal realm, where he famously slew the eight-headed serpent Yamata-no-Orochi to rescue a princess. He represents the wild, unpredictable power of nature and the potential for redemption through great deeds. He is a god for those who live on the edge, whose lives are marked by passion, struggle, and the courage to face monsters head-on.
##### Worshippers
Adventurers, outcasts, sailors, warriors who seek glory, and anyone who feels the stirrings of a wild spirit that cannot be constrained by societal norms.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Face the Storm: Do not shrink from challenges or chaos; lean into the fury of the storm, for it is in the eye of the gale that you find your true strength.
- Redeem Yourself through Action: No past mistake is beyond correction if you are willing to face the "serpents" in your path; prove your worth through brave and selfless deeds.
- Embrace Your Passions: Do not hide from your intensity; feel deeply and act decisively, but ensure your power is channelled toward righteous ends.
