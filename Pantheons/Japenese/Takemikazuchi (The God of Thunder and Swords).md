##### Description/Background
Takemikazuuchi is a powerful martial deity born from the blood of the fire god Kagutsuchi. Renowned for his absolute strength, discipline, and prowess with the blade, he famously subverted the wild gods of the earth to pave the way for the heavenly descent. He is credited in myth as a divine ancestor of sumo wrestling after victorious hand-to-hand combat and is a primary patron of the sword. Unyielding and fiercely protective, he represents the strategic, righteous use of force, martial discipline, and the terrifying cutting power of lightning.
##### Worshippers
Paladins, samurai, swordmasters, gladiators, military commanders, and martial artists seeking to perfect their technique.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Seek Perfection: Treat your weapon as an extension of your soul; master your mind and body through relentless training.
- Strike Righteously: Force must only be unleashed with precision and purpose; never draw your blade in malice or cowardice.
- Uphold the Line: Stand firm against chaos and lawlessness, serving as an unbreakable shield for the weak and innocent.
