Lawful Neutral
##### Holy Symbol
A coiled, jewel-encrusted dragon rising from a whirlpool.
##### Godly Level
Greater Deity
##### Description/Background
Ryujin is the formidable Dragon God who dwells within his sprawling, magnificent palace under the sea, constructed from coral, pearls, and precious gems. He commands the currents of the ocean and the weather that churns the seas. Ryujin is a powerful and mercurial figure; while he possesses the bounty of the deep, including the sacred "tide jewels" that can control the ebb and flow of the ocean, he can also bring devastating storms and shipwrecks to those who offend him. He represents the dual nature of the sea: life-giving and abundant, yet dangerous and indifferent to the affairs of mortals.
##### Worshippers
Sailors, coastal inhabitants, merchants of sea trade, and anyone who seeks the favour of the deep waters.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Respect the Deep: The ocean is vast and powerful; acknowledge its sovereignty and never take its bounty for granted. 
- Flow with the Tides: Adaptability is essential; understand that there is a time to be still and a time to unleash overwhelming force.
- Protect the Treasures: Guard the resources and secrets of the deep as if they were your own; ensure that the balance between land and sea is maintained.
##### Favoured Weapon
Trident (representing his command over the tides)
