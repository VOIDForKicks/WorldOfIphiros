##### Description/Background 
Konohanasakuya-hime, the "Princess who makes the blossoms bloom," is the deity of the delicate and the ephemeral. She is the daughter of the mountain god and represents the beauty and fragility of cherry blossoms, as well as the formidable, underlying power of the volcanoes they grow upon. Her story is one of trial and endurance; she survived a trial by fire to prove her loyalty and purity. She represents the intersection of outward grace and inward, unshakable strength.
##### Worshippers 
Gardeners, those who admire ephemeral beauty, volcano-dwelling peoples, and those who face trials of character.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Cherish the Moment: Life is as fleeting as a cherry blossom; live beautifully and fully, for we do not know when the petals will fall.
- Inner Resilience: Never mistake grace for weakness; possess the strength to survive the fire and the resolve to remain pure in intent.
- Respect the Earth's Might: Acknowledge the dormant power in the ground beneath your feet; beauty often rests upon a foundation of elemental strength.
