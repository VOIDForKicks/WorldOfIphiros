##### Description/Background
Hachiman is the divine patron of warriors and the protector of Japan. While he is deeply associated with the discipline, honour, and martial skill of the samurai, he also embodies the role of a guardian who watches over the prosperity and safety of the land. According to legend, he is often identified with the Emperor Ojin, a historical figure who transcended mortality through his commitment to order and military brilliance.

Unlike gods who revel in the chaos of battle, Hachiman views war as a solemn necessity, a tool to be wielded with precision, restraint, and an unwavering commitment to justice. He teaches that the true mastery of the bow and blade is useless without a spirit tempered by duty, compassion, and the protection of the innocent. He is the calm, steady eye of the strategist, standing in stark contrast to more bloodthirsty gods of slaughter.
##### Worshippers
Samurai, honourable knights, generals, tacticians, protectors of the realm, and archers who value discipline above raw power.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Master the Martial Form: Treat your combat skills as an art; refine your discipline and technique daily to ensure you are a precise instrument of justice. 
- Protect the Defenceless: Your strength is not for personal glory, but for the safety of those who cannot defend themselves; stand as a bulwark against tyranny.
- Act with Honour: Let your word be absolute and your conduct irreproachable; in battle and in peace, maintain the dignity expected of a champion.
