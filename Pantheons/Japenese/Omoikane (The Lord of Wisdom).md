##### Description/Background
Omoikane is the divine god of wisdom and counsel. His name translates to "he who gathers many thoughts," and he is famously credited with orchestrating the plan to lure the sun goddess, Amaterasu, out of the celestial cave. Omoikane is the divine strategist; he does not fight with steel, but with foresight and cleverness. He represents the importance of gathering diverse perspectives to solve complex problems and the power of knowledge to overcome even the darkest of situations. He is quiet, thoughtful, and ever-observant, often acting as an advisor to other deities.
##### Worshippers
Strategists, scholars, inventors, diplomats, mages, and anyone who believes that a well-placed idea is more powerful than a physical blow.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Seek Diverse Counsel: No single mind holds all the answers; consult widely, listen patiently, and synthesize the wisdom of others. 
- Foresight is Victory: Always look ahead; anticipate the consequences of your actions and prepare for every contingency.
- Value Intellectual Growth: The world is a puzzle to be solved; never cease learning, questioning, and expanding the horizons of your understanding.
