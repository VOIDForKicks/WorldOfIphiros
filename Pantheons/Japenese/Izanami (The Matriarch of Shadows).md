##### Description/Background 
Izanami is the tragic counterpart to Izanagi, the divine mother who helped forge the world before succumbing to the fiery agony of giving birth to the god of fire. Her death drove her into the dark realm of Yomi, the underworld. When Izanagi attempted to rescue her, he was horrified to find her body decayed and consumed by rot. Betrayed and humiliated, she unleashed her wrath upon him, vowing to take a thousand lives every day as punishment. She now rules the underworld as a formidable, terrifying goddess of death and decay. She represents the inescapable reality of mortality and the corruption that eventually claims all physical things.
##### Worshippers 
Necromancers, those who embrace the darker cycles of nature, mortals haunted by loss, and those who seek to understand the grim finality of death.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Acknowledge the Rot: Do not fear the inevitable decay of all things; accept it as a fundamental part of the cycle of existence.
- Embrace the Inevitable: Death is the only truth that cannot be bargained with; act with the cold resolve of one who knows that all roads lead to the dark.
- Respect the Boundary: Death is not a beginning, but a transformation; do not disturb the final slumber of the departed without solemn, dark purpose.
##### Favoured Weapon
