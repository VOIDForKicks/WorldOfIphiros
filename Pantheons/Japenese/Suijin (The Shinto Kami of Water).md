##### Holy Symbol
A pristine water droplet enclosing a freshwater lotus leaf.
##### Godly Level
Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background
Suijin is the peaceful yet vital guardian of the world's fresh waters, governing rivers, lakes, streams, and underground springs. Unlike the volatile dragon kings of the deep oceans, Suijin represents the life-giving, sustaining, and purifying nature of water required for daily survival, agriculture, and sanitation. Often associated with water spirits like the kappa or depicted as a benevolent presence near clean wells, Suijin offers protection to those who rely on pure waterways while demanding that the sources of life remain unpolluted and respected.
##### Worshippers
Farmers needing irrigation, town well-keepers, fishermen of rivers and lakes, travellers crossing treacherous waters, and traditional healers.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Preserve the Source: Protect streams, rivers, and wells from physical corruption and magical definement; water is life's blood.
- Maintain the Flow: Adapt smoothly to obstacles and let go of stagnant grudges, matching the dynamic, ever-moving nature of a river.
- Offer Purification: Wash away corruption, disease, and spiritual filth wherever they take root to restore natural harmony.
