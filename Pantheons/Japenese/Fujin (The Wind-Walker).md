Chaotic Neutral
##### Holy Symbol
A large, swirling sack (from which the winds are released) or a fan.
##### Godly Level
Minor Deity
##### Description/Background
Fujin is the primordial god of the wind, often depicted as a fearsome figure with wild hair and a giant, billowing bag of wind slung across his shoulders. He is an ancient force of nature, unpredictable, swift, and untamable. His presence is felt in the gentle breeze that carries a ship across the sea and the violent gale that reshapes the landscape. Fujin is not concerned with the moral struggles of mortals; he is concerned with movement, change, and the power of the air that breathes life into the world. He is a god who favours those who embrace constant change and refuse to be pinned down by stagnation.
##### Worshippers
Sailors, travellers, explorers, messengers, and those who believe that freedom is best found in constant motion.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Embrace Change: The wind never stops, and neither should you; welcome new horizons and do not let yourself grow stagnant. 
- Respect the Storm: Understand that power is meant to be respected, not controlled; acknowledge the destructive potential of nature in all things.
- Keep Your Path Open: Value your independence above all else; let your spirit remain as free and unburdened as the gale.
##### Favoured Weapon
Quarterstaff (or a spear representing a lightning strike)
