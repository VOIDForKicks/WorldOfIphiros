Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background
Ame-no-Uzume is the radiant goddess of the dawn and the patron of all who perform. She is most famous for her pivotal role in coaxing the sun goddess, Amaterasu, out of a dark cave by performing a raucous, joyous, and ribald dance that caused the other gods to roar with laughter. Her performance was so infectious and intriguing that it broke the silence of the world's darkness and brought light back to the heavens. She is a goddess of uninhibited spirit, representing the transformative power of humour, creativity, and the courage to make a spectacle of oneself for the sake of the greater good.
##### Worshippers
Dancers, actors, musicians, jesters, social activists, and those who use art and humour to combat despair or tyranny.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Bring the Light: Dispel gloom and sorrow with joy, laughter, and artistic expression; wherever there is darkness, find a way to perform. 
- Be Unafraid of Ridicule: Have the courage to act boldly and creatively, even if others judge you, for your spirit can inspire those who have lost hope.
- Celebrate the Community: Use your talents to bind people together, encouraging harmony and collective happiness through song and dance.
