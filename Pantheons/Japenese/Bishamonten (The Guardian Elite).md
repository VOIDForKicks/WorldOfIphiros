##### Description/Background
Bishamonten (derived from the Buddhist deity Vaiśravaṇa) is the fearsome armored god of war, divine justice, and the punisher of evildoers. He occupies a fascinating dual role in Japanese folklore: he is revered both as the guardian of the north among the _Shitennō_ (the Four Heavenly Kings) and as a bringer of prosperity among the _Shichifukujin_ (the Seven Lucky Gods). In classical iconography, he is always depicted as a fierce, scowling warrior clad in full, heavy samurai armor, holding a long spear or halberd in one hand and a miniature treasure pagoda (_stupa_) in the other. This pagoda symbolizes the divine treasure house of knowledge and spiritual wealth, which he strictly guards and dispenses only to those who prove themselves righteous and disciplined.

Historically, Bishamonten was the ultimate patron of samurai clans and legendary warlords. The famous 16th-century daimyō Uesugi Kenshin worshipped him so devoutly that he believed himself to be the god’s living avatar on earth, carrying a battle banner emblazoned with the character _Bi_ (for Bishamonten) into every conflict. Unlike chaotic deities of slaughter, Bishamonten represents calculated martial strategy, absolute law, and the defense of sacred spaces. He is the iron wall that protects the state from demonic invasion and the honorable commander who ensures that victory falls to those who fight with pure intent.
##### Worshippers
Samurai, oath of the crown paladins, tactical battle master fighters, tactical abjuration wizards, judges, and war domain clerics. His temples function as heavily fortified monasteries built at strategic northern choke points or border walls, featuring fierce guardian statues (_Niō_) at the gates and racks of masterfully forged, ceremonial weapons left as offerings by soldiers before marching to war.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Fight with Absolute Discipline: War is a sacred duty, not an excuse for reckless bloodlust; master your mind, hone your strategy, and execute your strikes with precision and restraint.
- Shield the Sacred: Stand as an unbreakable vanguard between innocent communities and the chaotic forces of destruction, fiends, or tyrants who threaten structural order.
- Guard Your Treasures: Protect the spiritual wealth, ancient knowledge, and resources entrusted to your care; ensure they never fall into the hands of those who would exploit them for evil.
