##### Description/Background
Ugajin is a unique and mysterious deity of harvest, fertility, and good fortune, often depicted as a serpent with a human head. Deeply intertwined with the cycles of soil nourishment and water, Ugajin is a spirit who transforms the raw resources of nature into tangible wealth and food security for civilization. Often associated or merged with Benzaiten, the goddess of fortune, Ugajin represents the quiet, foundational magic of the earth that rewards hard work, proper stewardship, and respect for nature's bounty with prosperity and abundance.
##### Worshippers
Farmers, merchants, coin-smiths, estate owners, and those seeking financial security or a successful growing season.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Nurture the Soil: True wealth springs from a healthy land; tend to the earth and rivers with care so they may continue to provide.
- Share the Bounty: When prosperity is granted to you, distribute a portion to ensure the well-being of the wider community.
- Respect the Cycles: Understand that growth takes time and patience; align your ambitions with the natural rhythm of planting and harvest.
