A Snake, or a traditional _biwa_ lute cradled by a white serpent.
##### Godly Level
Lesser Deity (One of the Seven Lucky Gods)
##### Description/Background
Benzaiten (originally syncretized from the Hindu goddess Saraswati) is a highly revered deity of everything that flows, music, words, water, talent, and wisdom. As the only female member of the _Shichifukujin_ (the Seven Lucky Gods), she is classically depicted as a beautiful woman playing a _biwa_ (a traditional Japanese lute). Because of her deep association with flowing things, her sanctuaries and shrines are almost exclusively built on islands, near coastal waters, or beside rushing rivers.

Her connection to snakes and dragons is foundational to her folklore. In one famous myth, a terrifying, five-headed dragon terrorized the residents of the village of Koshigoe for centuries, demanding child sacrifices. Benzaiten caused the island of Enoshima to erupt from the ocean floor to serve as her descent point. Entranced by her divine beauty and wisdom, the dragon courted her. Benzaiten firmly rejected the beast, stating she would never marry an entity that caused such immense suffering. Humbled and desperate to earn her favour, the dragon repented, swore an oath to protect the land, and transformed into a hill overlooking her island sanctuary. She represents the triumph of wisdom, artistry, and peaceful negotiation over raw, chaotic violence.
##### Worshippers
Bards, wizards of the school of divination or illusion, poets, diplomats, swashbuckler rogues, sea elves, and knowledge domain clerics. Her shrines are serene wooden pavilions situated on small, picturesque river islands or coastal cliffsides, filled with the soothing sounds of moving water and offerings of beautifully written poems, fine ink, and stringed instruments.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Let Wisdom Flow: Share knowledge, stories, and art freely across borders; view ignorance and stagnation as the ultimate barriers to mortal enlightenment.
- Tame the Beast with Wit: Rely on eloquence, music, and clever diplomacy to disarm your foes before resorting to blood; show that a well-placed word is sharper than any blade.
- Guard the Sacred Streams: Protect rivers, oceans, and lakes from corruption, respecting the life-giving, cleansing properties of water and the aquatic creatures that dwell within.
##### Favoured Weapon
Quarterstaff (often stylized to resemble a ceremonial instrument) or Shortsword
