##### Description/Background
Inari is one of the most revered and complex deities in the Japanese pantheon, serving as the benevolent provider of rice, the literal lifeblood of the culture, and a patron of success in industry and trade. Often associated with the fox (kitsune), who act as messengers and guardians, Inari is depicted in many forms: as a young woman, an old man, or a mysterious androgynous figure. This fluidity mirrors the changing seasons and the adaptability required for a successful harvest. Inari represents the harmonious relationship between humanity and the land; when humans respect the earth and cultivate it with diligent care, Inari ensures that the granaries remain full and the community thrives.
##### Worshippers
Farmers, merchants, artisans, those who work with the earth, and families seeking growth, health, and security in their homes.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets

- Work with Diligence: Success is the fruit of sustained effort; approach your labour with focus, consistency, and respect for the natural cycles.
- Honour the Messenger: Treat all living creatures with respect, recognizing that wisdom and guidance can often come from unexpected sources or humble beings.
