##### Description/Background
Tenjin is the deified spirit of Sugawara no Michizane, a brilliant 9th-century scholar, poet, and politician who was unjustly exiled due to court intrigue. Following his death, a series of fierce lightning storms struck the capital, leading the people to appease his powerful spirit by building shrines and revering him as Tenjin, the god of the sky and weather. Over time, his wrathful aspects calmed, and he became beloved as the ultimate patron of education, intellect, and academic success. He represents the triumph of knowledge, discipline, and honest capability over political corruption.
##### Worshippers
Students, scholars, poets, scribes, magistrates, academics, and wizards seeking clarity of mind before intense study or examinations.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Pursue Truth through Knowledge: Dedicate yourself to continuous learning; ignorance is a shadow that must be dispelled by the light of intellect.
- Cultivate Eloquence: Words hold immense power; hone your speech, poetry, and prose to inspire others and resolve conflicts peacefully.
- Stand against Injustice: Endure hardship and false accusations with dignity, trusting that truth and merit will ultimately prevail over deceit.
