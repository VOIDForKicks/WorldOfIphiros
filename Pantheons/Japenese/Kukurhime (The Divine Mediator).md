##### Description/Background 
Kukurhime is the goddess of mediation and communication. She is famously known for appearing to Izanagi and Izanami to bridge their divine discord and help restore balance between the two. She is the divine arbitrator, the one who listens to both sides and finds the thread that ties disparate spirits together. She is not a goddess of war, but of diplomacy, ensuring that grievances are aired and that chaos does not reign where understanding could prevail.
##### Worshippers 
Diplomats, judges, negotiators, mediators, and those who seek to heal rifts within families or communities.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Seek Understanding: Before you strike or judge, listen; there is almost always a thread of truth in the words of an opponent. 
- Bind the Broken: When strife tears a community apart, be the one to bridge the gap and mend the relationships.
- Maintain Neutrality: Justice requires an unbiased mind; do not let personal feelings cloud your role as an arbiter.
##### Favoured Weapon 
Whip (representing the "binding" cord)
