##### Description/Background:
Nonric was born in the frosty peaks of Aclea, where it learned to harness the ice of the mountain to aid it in protecting the people of Iphiros. Despite its monstrous appearance and the terrifying moniker "The Arctic Abomination," Nonric is a deeply compassionate protector. It views the unforgiving winter not as a cruel killer, but as a preservation chamber and a defensive wall against the chaos of the outside world. This Yeti god regularly partners up with Pyra (The Reborn), where Pyra’s fire purges corruption and sparks new beginnings, Nonric’s mists and ice shield the vulnerable, freezing threats in place and preserving the innocent until Pyra's warmth can reach them.

##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Be the Bulwark Against the Storm: The weak and the innocent cannot survive the harsh cold of the world alone. Stand as an unyielding wall of ice between those you protect and the threats that seek to destroy them.
- Preserve, Do Not Destroy: Cold preserves what warmth might rot. Hold fast to ancient wisdom, protect history from being lost, and keep the spark of life frozen safely away from enemy hands until the time is right for it to thaw.
- Look Beyond the Beast: True monstrosity lies in actions, not appearances. Judge others by the warmth of their heart and the lawfulness of their deeds, just as you expect the world to look past the terrifying visage of the Arctic Abomination.
