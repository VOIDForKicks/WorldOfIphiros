##### Mythology: 
Shattered Realm
##### Domain (Mythological): 
Fate and Destiny
##### D&D Domains: 
Knowledge, Peace, Twilight, Fate
##### Alignment: 
True Neutral
##### Holy Symbol: 
A silver needle threaded with a glowing strand of golden thread, piercing a small cloth tapestry.
##### Godly Level: 
Minor Deity
##### Description/Background:
Adis, known as "The Seamstress," is one of the four fates who weaves the tapestry of existence across the Shattered Realm. While other powers dictate grand designs or final outcomes, Adis meticulously stitches the individual lives, choices, and bonds of mortals together. She views the universe as a vast, interconnected garment where every single thread matters, ensuring that frayed connections are mended and that the fabric of reality does not unravel.
##### Worshippers: 
Weavers, tailors, seamstresses, community mediators, peacekeepers, and those who seek to repair broken relationships or heal fractured alliances.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Mend what is broken: dedicate your efforts to healing rifts between communities, individuals, and families, ensuring that the fabric of society remains strong.
- Honour every single thread: treat every person you meet as vital to the grand design, recognizing that even the smallest life holds immense value.
- Stitch with patience and care: approach conflicts and challenges with deliberate, careful precision rather than hasty force.
- Preserve the pattern: resist forces that seek to shred or destroy the bonds holding people together, acting as a guardian of unity and peace.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Needle-like Rapier or Heavy Weaving Needle (acting as a dagger)
