##### Mythology: 
Shattered Realm
##### Domain (Mythological): 
Destiny and History
##### D&D Domains: 
Knowledge, Order, Fate
##### Alignment:
True Neutral
##### Holy Symbol: 
An open tome bound in iron, crossed with a glowing quill that trails a line of ink.
##### Godly Level: 
Minor Deity
##### Description/Background:
Canir, known as "The Author," is one of the four fates within the Shattered Realm. While others weave or measure the threads of existence, Canir records every action, choice, and consequence in the great chronicle of the cosmos. Impartial and observant, Canir ensures that history is accurately documented and that the narrative of reality flows according to the cosmic script, indifferent to glory or tragedy.
##### Worshippers: 
Historians, archivists, scribes, chroniclers, judges, and scholars who seek to understand the past and preserve truth.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Record the truth without bias: chronicle events faithfully as they happen, refusing to distort history for politics, comfort, or personal gain.
- Honour the narrative of reality: respect the flow of time and destiny, understanding that every chapter, whether joyful or sorrowful, serves a purpose in the grand chronicle.
- Seek knowledge above all: uncover hidden truths, preserve lost lore, and ensure that wisdom is passed down to future generations.
- Weigh your words carefully: speak and write with deliberate precision, knowing that records and pronouncements shape the future.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Heavy Steel-Bound Tome or Quill-tipped Dagger
