##### Mythology: 
Shattered Realm
##### Domain (Mythological): 
Form and Destiny
##### D&D Domains:
Forge, Knowledge, Order, Fate
##### Alignment: 
True Neutral
##### Holy Symbol:
A stone mason's chisel resting against a half-carved block of marble, glowing with a steady internal light.
##### Godly Level: 
Minor Deity
##### Description/Background:
Naxtos, known as "The Sculptor," is one of the four fates within the Shattered Realm. While the other fates weave, record, or paint the paths of existence, Naxtos carves the physical and structural reality of destiny into enduring form. Naxtos chips away the superfluous chaos of the universe, shaping raw potential into solid, concrete outcomes and giving tangible structure to the abstract designs of fate.
##### Worshippers: 
Sculptors, stone masons, architects, blacksmiths, structural engineers, and those who seek to build lasting legacies out of chaos.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Shape reality through discipline: Approach every challenge with patience, steady hands, and meticulous effort, cutting away impurities to reveal the true form beneath.
- Build enduring foundations: Create works, institutions, and bonds that stand the test of time, resisting the erosion of decay and disorder.
- Respect the raw material: Understand the limitations and strengths of what you are given before attempting to force it into a new shape.
- Stand firm against chaos: Be a pillar of stability and structure in an unpredictable world, holding fast when external forces threaten to crumble your community.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Heavy Mason's Hammer or Chisel-tipped Dagger
