##### Mythology: 
Shattered Realm
##### Domain (Mythological): 
Creation and Destiny
##### D&D Domains: 
Knowledge, Twilight, Peace, Fate
##### Alignment: 
True Neutral
##### Holy Symbol: 
An artist's palette made of dark stone, crossed with a glowing brush dripping with iridescent paint.
##### Godly Level: 
Minor Deity
##### Description/Background:
Laris, known as "The Painter," is one of the four fates of the Shattered Realm. While other fates measure, record, or stitch the paths of existence, Laris applies the broad strokes of color, emotion, and environment to the grand canvas of reality. Laris determines the shifting moods, landscapes of experience, and vibrant nuances that define mortal lives, ensuring that existence is a rich, dynamic masterpiece rather than a rigid script.
##### Worshippers: 
Painters, visual artists, illusionists, interior designers, dreamers, and those who seek to bring beauty and expression into a chaotic world.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Add colour to the world: bring beauty, art, and creative expression to communities that have grown dull, bleak, or despairing.
- Embrace perspective: recognize that every situation can be viewed in countless shades and lights, and look beyond the surface monochrome of conflict.
- Paint your own destiny: use the creative spark given by Laris to shape your environment and choose your own path upon the cosmic canvas.
- Preserve the aesthetic balance: protect works of art, natural wonders, and cultural heritage from destruction and decay.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Brush-tipped Quarterstaff or Artist's Mallet
