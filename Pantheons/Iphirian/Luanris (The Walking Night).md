Iphirian
##### Domain (Mythological): 
Moon
##### D&D Domains: 
Moon, Twilight, Knowledge, Trickery
##### Alignment: 
Neutral Good
##### Holy Symbol: 
A silver crescent moon cradling a human footprint or a wandering eye
##### Godly Level: 
Greater Deity
##### Description/Background:
Luanris is one of the two moons that orbit the world of Iphiros. Unlike distant, untouchable deities, Luanris physically traverses the night sky in an unpredictable, wandering orbit, watching over the silent hours of the world. In Iphirian myth, Luanris represents the transition between waking life and dreams, the comfort found in solitude, and the quiet guidance that darkness provides to those who are lost. While the sun brings harsh exposure and judgment, Luanris offers a cloak of silver shadow, hiding the weary, protecting night travellers, and illuminating hidden truths that sunlight burns away.
##### Worshippers: 
Night guards, nocturnal scouts, hermits, travelers who journey after dusk, astronomers, and those seeking solace in quiet introspection.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Be the light in the deep dark: just as Luanris cuts through the pitch-black night with a pale, steady glow, you must protect those who wander in dangerous or metaphorical darkness.
- Observe before you act: the moon watches silently before tides shift, so you must listen, observe, and understand a situation fully before letting your judgment fall.
- Embrace solitude and reflection: wisdom is found in quiet places, and you should dedicate time each night to stillness, contemplation, and communion with the silver wanderer.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Crescent Glaive or Quarterstaff (often tipped with silver or polished steel to catch the moonlight, resembling the curved shape of the wandering moon)
