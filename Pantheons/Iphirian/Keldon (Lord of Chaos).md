A fractured, bleeding eye encased in a jagged, asymmetrical star.
##### Godly Level: 
Outer Deity
##### Description/Background:
One of the two primordial beings created alongside the world of Iphiros. This being is entirely for chaos and destruction. At the dawn of time, Keldon sought to instantly unmake and shatter the newborn world. However, he was defeated by his counterpart, Ulan, who bound him in a cosmic cage and cast him deep into the dark spaces between the stars. Though imprisoned, Keldon is not entirely silenced. His malice bleeds through the celestial bars, allowing him to subtly influence the minds of mortals, whispering promises of destructive power to the mad, the desperate, and the vengeful
##### Worshippers: 
Nihilists, mad sorcerers, apocalypse cults, and those corrupted by the whispers from the night sky.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Unmake the Cage: The stars are a prison, and the world is a lie. Shatter the seals of order wherever they are built.
- Embrace the Storm: Let your whims guide your actions. Chaos is the true nature of reality; order is just a temporary illusion.
- Bleed the World: Leave nothing whole. Every act of destruction weakens the threads holding Keldon back.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Flail or Greataxe
