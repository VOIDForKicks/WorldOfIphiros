##### Mythology: 
Shattered Realm
##### Domain (Mythological): 
War, Conflict, and Bloodshed
##### D&D Domains: 
War or Tempest
##### Alignment: 
Chaotic Evil
##### Holy Symbol: 
A shattered shield crossed with a bloodied, notched broadsword, engulfed in pale, erratic flames.
##### Godly Level: 
Major Creature
##### Description/Background:
Lumir, known as "The Warlord," is one of Keldon's five knights of Chaos, born directly from the seed of War. Unlike structured military commanders who fight for territory or peace, Lumir drives mortals to engage in endless, senseless slaughter and endless conflict for its own sake. Serving as an instrument of the imprisoned outer deity Keldon, Lumir’s presence incites uncontrollable rage, turning brother against brother and plunging entire continents into brutal, eternal warfare.
##### Worshippers: 
Bloodthirsty mercenaries, ruthless raiders, warmongers, berserkers, and cultists who thrive on chaos and battle.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Foment endless conflict: Instigate war wherever peace takes root, ensuring that blood never stops flowing across the realms.
- Abandon mercy: Show no quarter to enemies or allies alike, proving that compassion is a weakness that invites ruin.
- Feed the chaos of battle: Let unrestrained violence guide your actions, tearing down the structures of civilization through the fury of total war.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Greatsword or Heavy Warhammer
