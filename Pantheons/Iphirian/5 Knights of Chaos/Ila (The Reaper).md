##### Mythology: 
Shattered Realm
##### Domain (Mythological): 
Death, Slaughter, and Mortality
##### D&D Domains: 
Death, Grave
##### Alignment: 
Neutral Evil
##### Holy Symbol: 
A gleaming steel scythe blade dripping with dark blood, bisecting an hourglass filled with black sand.
##### Godly Level: 
Major Creature
##### Description/Background:
Ila, known as "The Reaper," is one of Keldon's five knights of Chaos, born directly from the seed of Death. While natural death is a peaceful part of the cosmic cycle, Ila’s grim purpose is violent, untimely, and absolute slaughter, serving as an instrument to violently tear the souls from mortals and feed the entropy championed by the imprisoned outer deity.
##### Worshippers: 
Assassins, merciless executioners, death cultists, and those who view slaughter as the ultimate equalizer.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Harvest the living: Bring swift and unprovoked mortality to the flourishing, ensuring that life is cut short before it can build lasting legacies.
- Deny natural peace: Reject peaceful passing; death should be feared, sudden, and violent to feed the chaos of the cosmos.
- Serve the final silence: Eradicate hope and future by bringing total, unyielding ruin to all who stand in your path.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Two-Handed Scythe or Bone-Etched Sickle
