##### Mythology: 
Shattered Realm
##### Domain (Mythological): 
Conquest, Subjugation, and Brutal Domination
##### D&D Domains: 
War, Death, or Tempest
##### Alignment: 
Lawful Evil
##### Holy Symbol: 
An iron crown crushed under the weight of a spiked, blood-stained iron gauntlet.
##### Godly Level: 
Major Creature
##### Description/Background:
Davohr is one of Keldon's five knights of Chaos, born from the seed of Conquest. While his master Keldon seeks total unmaking and sheer entropy, Davohr twists destruction into ruthless subjugation, forcing nations to bow through violence, fear, and iron-fisted tyranny. He carves a bloody path across the Shattered Realm, turning civilizations against one another to shatter alliances from within.
##### Worshippers: 
Warlords, brutal tyrants, mercenary conquerors, and brutalized armies who believe might makes right.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Subjugate the weak: Force the fractured realms under a single, violent heel, proving that peace is only achieved through total dominance.
- Rule through terror: Crush dissent swiftly and mercilessly, letting fear be your primary tool for control.
- Break alliances: Turn friend against friend and kingdom against kingdom, ensuring that unity is forever shattered in Keldon's name.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Heavy War Pick or Spiked Greatsword
