##### Mythology: 
Shattered Realm
##### Domain (Mythological): 
Famine, Want, and Deprivation
##### D&D Domains: 
Death, Trickery, or Grave
##### Alignment: 
Neutral Evil
##### Holy Symbol: 
An emaciated, skeletal hand clutching an empty, cracked golden bowl.
##### Godly Level: 
Major Creature
##### Description/Background:
Ciren is one of Keldon's five knights of Chaos, born directly from the seed of Famine. As a herald of the imprisoned Outer Deity Keldon, Ciren embodies endless hunger, barren soil, and the slow, agonizing depletion of life and resource. Wherever Ciren’s influence spreads, fields wither, wells run dry, and a gnawing, insatiable greed takes root in the hearts of mortals, driving them to consume or destroy everything in their path.
##### Worshippers: 
Desperate scavengers, doomsday cultists, raiders, and those who believe that stripping the world bare is the only way to find truth.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Starve the land: Strip away abundance and leave nothing behind, proving that all mortal security is fleeting.
- Cultivate endless want: Sow greed and desperation among the populace, turning friend against friend over meagre resources.
- Embrace the void: Honour the emptiness within by rejecting luxury, comfort, and peace, serving as a hollow vessel for Keldon's destructive will.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Scythe or Worn, Rust-Etched Spear
