##### Mythology: 
Shattered Realm
##### Domain (Mythological): 
Pestilence, Disease, and Decay
##### D&D Domains: 
Death or Nature
##### Alignment: 
Chaotic Evil
##### Holy Symbol: 
A cracked, plague-doctor's ceramic mask oozing a trail of dark, pestilent green vapor.
##### Godly Level: 
Major Creature
##### Description/Background:
Melaris, known as "The Plague," is the final of Keldon's five knights of Chaos, born directly from the seed of Pestilence. While the other knights bring famine, conquest, slaughter, and war, Melaris rots the world from the inside out, spreading virulent diseases, rotting blights, and agonizing sicknesses that break down the physical bodies and communities of mortals, feeding the ultimate entropy of the imprisoned outer deity.
##### Worshippers: 
Pathogen cultists, plague doctors gone mad, hermits of the waste, and those who view physical decay as the truest equalizer.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Spread the rot: Carry disease and decay into clean, thriving cities, ensuring that health and vitality are poisoned at their roots.
- Break the flesh: Treat the physical form as a temporary, fragile vessel meant only to suffer and decompose in service to the grand corruption.
- Fester in secret: Let sickness creep through water supplies, crowds, and quiet corners before erupting into devastating outbreaks.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Corroded Dagger or Plague Censer (used as a flail)
