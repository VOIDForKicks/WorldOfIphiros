##### Mythology: 
Iphirian
##### Domain (Mythological): 
Sun
##### D&D Domains: 
Light or Nature, Dawn, Dusk
##### Alignment: 
Lawful Neutral
##### Holy Symbol: 
Lawful Neutral
##### Godly Level: 
Greater Deity
##### Description/Background:
Rava is the majestic embodiment of the sun that rises and falls every day, driving the celestial chariot across the sky to bring light, warmth, and life to Iphiros. Unrelenting, orderly, and absolute, Rava's light touches every corner of the world, exposing secrets, nourishing the harvest, and burning away the impurities of shadow and decay.
##### Worshippers: 
Farmers, agricultural workers, daytime guards, judges, rulers, and priests of the solar rite who mark the turning of the seasons and the passing of each day.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Bring warmth and life: use your gifts to nurture the innocent, protect the vulnerable, and help communities flourish just as the sun helps crops grow.
- Let nothing hide in the shadows: champion truth, justice, and clarity, ensuring that deceit and hidden malice are dragged out into the blazing light.
- Honour the cycle: respect the natural order of day and night, life and rest, understanding that endurance requires both blazing activity and quiet submission to the dark.
- Burn bright and unyielding: face hardship with unshakeable resolve, remaining as steady and reliable as the rising sun.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Radiant Morningstar or Longsword (glowing with a permanent, gentle solar heat)
