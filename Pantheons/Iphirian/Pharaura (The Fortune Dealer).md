##### Mythology: 
Iphirian
##### Domain (Mythological): 
Prophecy
##### D&D Domains: 
Knowledge, Arcana, or Fortune
##### Alignment: 
True Neutral
##### Holy Symbol: 
A pair of ornate, blindfolded eyes resting over an open deck of celestial tarot cards or a spinning astrolabe.
##### Godly Level: 
Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background:
Pharaura is the revered Goddess of prophecies and the chief messenger of the four fates. She walks the thin veil between what was, what is, and what is yet to come, whispering glimpses of tomorrow into the minds of the desperate and the wise. She believes that the tapestry of fate is already woven, but mortals possess the free will to choose how they walk toward their inevitable destinations.
##### Worshippers: 
Fortune-tellers, cartomancers, astrologers, mystics, statesmen seeking guidance on future conflicts, and individuals desperate to alter a grim destiny.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Honour the tapestry of fate: respect the inevitability of destiny, knowing that every thread has its purpose in the grand design.
- Speak truth to power: deliver the prophecies entrusted to you with absolute honesty, regardless of whether the listener wishes to hear them.
- Guide the lost traveller: use your foresight to help those wandering blindly make informed choices about their paths.
- Guard the secrets of tomorrow: not all truths are meant for every mind; protect dangerous prophecies from those who would exploit them for selfish gain.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Stellar Dagger or Heavy Iron-Bound Tome
