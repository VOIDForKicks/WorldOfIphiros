##### Description/Background:
Kairos was not born a god, he is the future, fully ascended, corruption-free form of Hadarai. Originally a mortal from a distant past, Hadarai was plucked from his own era by Anare (The All-Knowing) for reasons the present-day god keeps close to his chest, entirely unaware that Ouroboros would also secretly chosen the boy as an avatar. After the end of the Chrono Corsairs adventures, where the corruption is finally exposed and defeated, Hadarai ascends to godhood as Kairos.

Upon his ascension, Kairos is given a new name and a holy duty by Ulan to protect the timeline. However, Kairos carries a burden no other deity shares: memories of the past. He discovers that Keldon's corruption of Ouroboros has been tearing the fabric of time allowing the Chrono Corsairs, Xeno's minions and Xeno himself to step through time. To ensure his own history happens exactly as it needs to so he can save his friends, Kairos slips through these tears. He must operate through vague clues.
##### Worshippers: 
Because Kairos operates from the future and keeps his identity hidden, he has no formal churches or public worshippers in the present day. His only true "worshippers" are the party members themselves, who revere him as the "Mystery Merchant."
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Secure the Thread: The timeline is a fragile tapestry. Hunt down the anomalies and minions of chaos who seek to unravel history for their own ends.
- Provide the Key: Do not fight the battle _for_ mortals, but open the doors they need to find their own strength and fulfil their destiny.
- Embrace the Shadow: True heroism often requires working in silence, sacrificing your name and identity so that the light of the future can survive.
