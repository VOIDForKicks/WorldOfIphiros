True Neutral
##### Holy Symbol: 
A fading silver crescent moon partially eclipsed by a dark, starless circle.
##### Godly Level: 
Greater Deity
##### Description/Background:
Aione is the second moon that orbits Iphiros, characterized by its strange and strict celestial cycle of appearing in the night sky for only 2 nights every 6 days. Because of its infrequent and fleeting presence, Aione is viewed as a mysterious, elusive deity representing rare windows of opportunity, cyclical patience, and the profound power of scarcity. While its sibling moon Luanris is a constant companion to the night, Aione's rare arrivals are treated as momentous events laden with arcane significance.
##### Worshippers: 
Astrologers tracking complex planetary alignments, arcane researchers studying cyclical magic, reclusive mystics, and individuals who prize patience and timing above all else.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Honour the cycle of absence and presence: understand that power and wisdom do not need to be constantly displayed; sometimes waiting is the most potent action you can take.
- Seize the fleeting moment: just as Aione's light graces the sky for only two brief nights, you must act decisively when rare opportunities present themselves.
- Guard the hidden timing: keep secrets safe and reveal your intentions only when the moment is right, aligning your deeds with the rhythms of the cosmos.
- Respect the unseen: value what is hidden in the dark just as much as what is brought to light, knowing that absence holds its own form of presence.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Ruined Chakram or Crescent Dagger (designed to be thrown or used in swift, precise bursts, mirroring a brief celestial transit)
