Two interlocking, metallic rings pulled taut by a pair of heavy, ethereal chains that fade into mist.
##### Godly Level: 
Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background:
Ensuring the planes don't drift too far away, Damos uses his body as chains to keep the planes connected without letting them overlap and allowing travel between them. While his greater counterpart, Ouroboros, keeps the temporal loop of life and death moving forward in time, Damos anchors the spatial architecture of reality itself. Without his constant vigilance, the different realms of existence would float off into the cosmic void or crash into one another, causing apocalyptic planar bleeding. He serves as the ultimate planar anchor and toll keeper, maintaining the boundary lines so that extraplanar threats stay out, while leaving just enough connectivity for mortal travellers to cross the thresholds safely.
##### Worshippers: 
Planar travellers, horizon walkers, dimensional mages, cartographers of the multiverse, gatekeepers, and those who guard dimensional rifts or portals.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Anchors Aweigh: Hold the line against planar drift. Do not let external cosmic forces destabilize the balance of Iphiros, and act as a living anchor when your companions face chaotic upheaval.
- Guard the Threshold: Portals and rifts are privileges, not playthings. Ensure that extraplanar entities do not breach the boundaries illegally, and punish those who build unauthorized rifts that cause reality to bleed.
- Walk the Middle Path: Change is inevitable, but chaos is destructive. Keep your mind open to the shifting realities of the multiverse, but remain firmly rooted in your duties so you are never lost to the infinite void.
