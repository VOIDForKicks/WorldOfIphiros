A pristine pearl glowing with a soft internal light, resting in the centre of a nautical compass carved from whalebone.
##### Godly Level: 
Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background:
A servant of Mariana (The Leviathan), this merfolk goddess guides the creatures of the Iphirian seas along Mariana's path. While Mariana rules the depths with a crushing, iron will, Slieve acts as her elegant emissary and the coordinator of the ocean’s vast currents. She translates the Leviathan’s strict oceanic laws into navigable shipping lanes, seasonal migration routes, and royal decrees for the underwater courts. Slieve believes that the sea is a grand highway that requires absolute order to survive; those who follow the designated paths are granted safe passage, while those who stray into forbidden waters are swiftly abandoned to the merciless depths. She embodies the grace, protocol, and quiet majesty of the deep-sea nobility.
##### Worshippers: 
Navigators, mapmakers, merfolk royalty, deep-sea ambassadors, maritime pilgrims, and captains who meticulously follow legal shipping routes to avoid Mariana’s wrath.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Respect the Hierarchy: Order keeps the tides turning. Honour your superiors, maintain the protocols of the court, and respect the ancient divine boundaries established by the rulers of the deep.
- Walk the True Path: Deviation is the first step toward ruin and chaos. Guide the lost back to the designated road, protect established trade routes, and never let yourself or your charges wander into forbidden territory.
- Dignity in Duty: Carry yourself with the poise and nobility of the high seas. Whether commanding a fleet or guiding a lone traveller, execute your duties with unwavering composure, fairness, and strict adherence to the law.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Spear (resembling a royal sceptre with a streamlined, piercing tip) or Rapier
