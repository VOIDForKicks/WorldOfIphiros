Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background:
Azion serves as the righteous long arm of the law, commanding the primal instincts of nature to enforce justice across Iphiros. While his greater counterpart, Hytos, stands as the stationary, unyielding guardian of the grave and the fortress gates, Azion is the active force that hunts down lawbreakers and chaotic elements. He bridges the gap between civilization and the wild, believing that true justice is a natural law inherent to all living creatures. Azion often works alongside Hytos, utilizing packs of celestial beasts, loyal hounds, and apex predators to track down fugitives, enforce decrees, and scout ahead of the divine armies to ensure order prevails.
##### Worshippers: 
Bounty hunters, rangers, judges who travel to remote frontiers, law enforcement officers, beast masters, and paladins who proactively seek out injustice.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Hunt the Lawless: Justice cannot be passive. Track down those who flee from their crimes, cross boundaries illegally, or sow chaos in the quiet corners of the world, and bring them to answer for their deeds.
- Listen to the Instincts: True law is written in the hearts of all noble creatures. Trust the primal instincts that separate right from wrong, and use the strength of the beast to defend the helpless.
- Lead from the Front: As the vanguard, you must be the first to face danger. Never send others where you fear to tread; clear the path so that order and justice may safely follow behind you.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Longsword or Spear (often designed to look like a piercing fang or claw)
