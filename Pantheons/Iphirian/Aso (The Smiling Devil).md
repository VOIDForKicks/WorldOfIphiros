##### Holy Symbol: 
A porcelain Tiefling horn wrapped in a vibrant, blooming red rose, split down the middle by a sharp, roguish grin.
##### Godly Level: 
Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background:
Aso is a suave, free-spirited deity who bridges the gap between the wild emotional resilience of Yuki (The Trickster) and the bustling, structured communities of mortals. Despite her intimidating title, _The Smiling Devil_, Aso is not malicious; she is a passionate champion of mortal agency, romance, and the vibrant chaos of civilization. While Yuki spreads joy in the wilds and protects the youth, Aso focuses on the adult heart, fostering the messy, beautiful bonds of love and the unyielding hope that drives societies to build, celebrate, and thrive. She believes that civilization shouldn't just be about rigid laws and survival; it must have a soul fueled by passion, community, and the freedom to pursue one's desires.
##### Worshippers: 
Trickers, star-crossed lovers, matchmakers, bards, architects of bustling cultural hubs, community organizers, and tieflings or outcasts looking to build a welcoming home in civilization.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Ignite the Passion: Love and desire are the truest expressions of freedom. Protect lovers, foster deep connections within communities, and ensure that the spark of romance and kinship is never crushed by cold tyranny or dull routine.
- Defend the Sanctuary: Civilization is at its best when it is a safe haven for expression. Build up and defend spaces where people can gather, share art, and speak freely without fear of persecution or star-born madness.
- Keep Hope Alive: When the shadows lengthen and the future looks bleak, crack a smile and find a way forward. Hope is a fierce, unruly flame; never allow despair to make you complacent or quiet.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Rapier or Whip
