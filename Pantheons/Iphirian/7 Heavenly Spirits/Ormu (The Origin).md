##### Description/Background:
Ormu is the oldest living creature on Iphiros, the First Dragon. In the chaotic infancy of the world, before Keldon was imprisoned, Ormu used his primal strength and innate understanding of magic to shelter the lesser mortal races. Recognizing his immense protective spirit, Ulan chose him to be the very first mortal elevated to godhood. Imbued with cosmic power, Ormu became "The Origin". He used his newfound divine domains to help Ulan maintain the physical and magical safety of Iphiros, while Ulan regained her strength and life began safely taking root.
##### Worshippers:

- Kindle the Spark: Knowledge and magic are sacred gifts meant to elevate mortal life. Protect ancient wisdom, and never allow the flame of curiosity to die out.
- Mend the Fractures: When the defences of the world weaken or the bonds of society break, do not abandon them. Stand firm, stoke the fires of resolve, and reforge what was broken.
