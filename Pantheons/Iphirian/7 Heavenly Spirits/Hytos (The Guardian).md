##### Holy Symbol: 
A three-headed canine silhouette framed by an unbroken shield.
##### Godly Level: 
Greater Deity (7 Heavenly Spirits)
##### Description/Background:
Once a humanoid god, Hytos possessed a fierce, unyielding sense of justice, all while commanding the domains of Tempest, Death and Nature. Through-out the “First War” Hytos openly spoke ill of Keldon, condemning him for his want to destroy the world of Iphiros. Because of this Keldon cursed Hytos transforming him into a Cerberus-like creature hoping for him to become a feral beast. However due to Hytos’s unwavering sense of justice he he kept his sanity. Ulan noticed this and invited him into the 7 Heavenly Spirits giving him the title of “The Guardian” and appointing him new domains of Protection, Order and Grave
##### Worshippers: 
Paladins of Devotion or Crown, Grave domain clerics, city watchmen, fortress guards, royal protectors, and those who guard mausoleums and sacred burial grounds to keep them safe from desecration.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Be the Unbroken Shield: Stand between the innocent and the forces of chaos. Your body and soul are a fortress; let nothing pass that would cause harm.
- Honour the Final Rest: The grave is a sacred boundary of Order. Ensure the dead sleep peacefully, and ruthlessly destroy any undead or chaotic abominations that violate this rest.
- Defy the Corruptor: Never bow, never yield, and never speak softly of Keldon’s dark influence. Face the madness of the stars with an unyielding heart.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Maul or Flanged Mace (often wielded alongside a heavy shield)
