##### Description/Background:
Once a regular Hydra, this god ascended by gathering information from across Iphiros about its land and history, eventually leading to the creation of a ritual that would allow her to obtain godhood. After reaching her goal she earned the title of "Anare, The All Knowing". The ritual she preformed came at a cost, all the information she had obtained could no longer be kept to herself as even with her multiple heads she could not retain all the information so she created a library in the town now known as Goldbourne to store it all, keeping what she has dubbed as "Forbidden Knowledge" in a hidden vault. Recognizing her immense dedication to preservation, Ulan appointed her as one of the 7 Heavenly Spirits.
##### Worshippers: 
Librarians, scholars, cartographers, wizards, judges, and archivists. Her temples resemble vast, silent scriptoriums or universities. Monsters seeking enlightenment or a way to break their feral curses might also secretly revere her, seeing her ascension as proof that any creature can rise above its base nature through intellect.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Preserve the Record: Knowledge must be recorded and preserved, for a world that forgets its past is doomed to chaos.
- Contain the Fracture: When order breaks, act swiftly to cage the chaos before it spreads
- Guard the Vault: Some truths are too heavy for mortal minds; protect the secrets of the world from those who would use them to tear it apart.
