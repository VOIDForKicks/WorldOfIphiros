Greater Deity 
##### Description/Background:
Yuki is a magnificent, ancient Nine-tailed Kitsune god who embodies the playful, unpredictable side of Nature. While she loves to cause harmless mischief, pull pranks on stuffy mortals, and weave elaborate illusions, her heart is entirely pure. Above all else, she is dedicated to bringing smiles, laughter, and hope to the children and innocent people of Iphiros.

Ulan appointed Yuki as a Heavenly Spirit because absolute order without joy is just a cold prison. Yuki provides the world with emotional resilience. She understands that Keldon’s greatest weapon is despair and madness; by spreading joy, she keeps the hearts of mortals strong enough to resist the dark whispers bleeding from the stars.
##### Worshippers: 
Children, entertainers, actors, thieves with hearts of gold, pranksters, fey creatures, and travellers who look for joy and safety in the wild forests.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Kindle the Laughter: Despair is the gateway to Keldon's madness. Bring joy, smiles, and laughter to the innocent wherever you go, for a happy heart is a shield against chaos.
- Outwit the Oppressor: Do not fight brute force with brute force if you can help it. Use your mind, your illusions, and your wit to make fools out of tyrants and cultists.
- Protect the Innocent: Stand fiercely in defence of the young, the vulnerable, and the gentle souls of Iphiros. Let your mischief never cause true harm to those who do not deserve it.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Dagger or Shortbow (representing quick, precise, and nimble strikes)
