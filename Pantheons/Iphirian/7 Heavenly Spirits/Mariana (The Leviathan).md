##### Description/Background:
Mariana is a massive, primordial Kraken god that slumbers deep in the abyssal depths of Iphiros's oceans, ruling the volatile waters stretching between the Asmark Expanse and the Wailing Ocean. Unlike her feral, chaotic kin who destroy mindlessly, Mariana values absolute dominion and structure. She views the oceans as her sovereign territory and demands total submission from everything that sails across or swims within it.

When Ulan sought powerful anchors to maintain order on Iphiros, Mariana accepted the mantle of a Heavenly Spirit. She despises Keldon because chaos threatens her tightly regulated underwater empires. For Mariana, protecting the world isn't about kindness, it is about preserving her domain and ensuring no chaotic, star-spawned trash pollutes her seas.
##### Worshippers: 
Ruthless sea captains, deep-sea divers, tritons, sahuagin tribes who bend to her law, coastal tyrants, and sailors who offer heavy tribute to survive the Wailing Ocean.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Rule with an Iron Tentacle: Chaos breeds in weakness. Command absolute obedience from those beneath you, and let your authority be as crushing as the deep ocean pressure.
- Conquer the Storm: Do not fear the tempest; become it. Channel the violent fury of the waves to break the resolve of Keldon’s agents.
- Claim the Tribute: The oceans give life, but they demand payment. Ensure that all who benefit from the world’s resources pay their dues to the spirits who preserve it.
