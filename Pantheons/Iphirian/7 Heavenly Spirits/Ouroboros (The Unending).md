##### Description/Background:
Circling the world of Iphiros ensuring death and life walk hand in hand, Ouroboros watches peacefully ensuring the cycle never gets broken or overlaps. As a primordial entity who governs the mechanical flow of Time and weaves the complex threads of Fate, he does not view death as a tragedy or life as a triumph, but rather as the front and back of the same golden coin. To Ouroboros, every mortal's destiny is a predetermined thread, and time is the loom that moves it forward.

Ulan appointed Ouroboros to be one of the 7 Heavenly Spirits because she recognized a vital cosmic truth: absolute Order cannot mean total stagnation. For the creatures of Iphiros to truly grow and flourish, Time had to move forward dynamically, generations had to pass, and individual Fates needed a natural, unpredictable rhythm. By elevating Ouroboros, Ulan enshrined the eternal cycle as a fundamental law of reality, ensuring that even though individual lives must end according to their destiny, the grand artistry of creation would remain forever unending.
##### Worshippers: 
Hospice workers, midwives, elder druids, chroniclers, fatalistic philosophers, diviners, and common folk who pray for a peaceful life and an undisturbed death.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Trust the Turning Wheel: Do not cling desperately to life, nor rush eagerly toward death. Embrace each season of existence as it comes, for all things have their proper time.
- Preserve the Horizon: Actively hunt down those who violate the cosmic order, such as necromancers creating mindless undead or mages altering time for selfish gains. The cycle must remain unbroken.
- Weave Your Thread: Fate is a tapestry, but you hold the needle. Live your life fully and authentically, leaving a meaningful legacy behind for the generation that will rise from your ash.
