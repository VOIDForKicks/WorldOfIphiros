##### Mythology: 
Shattered Realm
##### Domain (Mythological):
Life, Light, Fire
##### D&D Domains:
Life, Light, Fire
##### Alignment:
Lawful neutral
##### Holy Symbol: 
A blazing phoenix silhouette emerging from a cracked, glowing egg.
##### Godly Level: 
Greater Deity (7 Heavenly Spirits)
##### Description/Background:
Born from the ashes of the “First War”, after residual comic energy from Ulan and Keldon’s battle fell on the battlefield. The phoenix god understands that without death, life cannot thrive. She lives by the rule “Death must always give to life”. To stop keldon breaking free she ensures the sparks of creation are always allowed to rise from the ashes of the fallen. Ulan saw the desire to push mortals to new heights in Pyra, giving her the title “The Reborn” and recruiting her into the 7 Heavenly Spirits.
##### Worshippers: 
Doctors, midwives, pyromancers who view fire as a tool of purification rather than destruction, and those who have survived near-death experiences or tragic losses and seek a fresh start.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Purify the Rot: Keldon’s corruption is a cancer on reality. Cleanse the land, the dead, and the corrupted with holy fire so that untainted life may grow in its place.
- Kindle the Dying Embers: Where hope, life, or goodness is fading, be the spark that reignites it. Never allow total despair to extinguish the light of Iphiros.
- Accept the Cycle: Do not fear endings, for all things must pass so that new things can begin. Fight to protect the world, but accept that rebirth requires the shedding of the old.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Scimitar (representing a curving flame or the sweeping ray of a sunbeam) or Glaive
