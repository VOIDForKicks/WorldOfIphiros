Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background:
A descendant of Ormu (The Origin), this Bronze dragon grew up near the town of Pavv, where it struck a deal to protect the village in exchange for gold. Over time, Panot’s fierce commitment to fair transactions, community defence, and the prosperity of the land elevated her to godhood. She believes that true safety is built on a foundation of mutual trust, honest labour, and reliable trade. While her ancestor Ormu focuses on the grand design of the cosmos and grand creation, Panot focuses on the earth beneath mortals' feet, ensuring the soil remains fertile and the markets remain fair. She views contracts not as chains, but as the bedrock of a peaceful, thriving society.
##### Worshippers: 
Merchants, farmers, caravan guards, tax collectors, magistrates, bronze dragons, and community leaders who value honour, commerce, and mutual protection.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Honour the Contract: Your word is your bond, and an honest deal is sacred. Never break a promise, cheat a customer, or exploit the desperate, for a society built on greed will surely collapse.
- Guard the Harvest: Protect both the literal crops of the field and the fruits of civilization's labour. Stand as a shield between peaceful communities and the raiders or monsters that wish to steal what they did not earn.
- Wealth Through Work: Gold is not a hoarding prize; it is a tool to foster growth, build strong defences, and feed the hungry. Cultivate the land and trade with fair scales so that all may prosper.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Halberd (resembling a stylized, sweeping dragon wing) or Light Crossbow.
