An unblinking, perfectly symmetrical eye carved into a flawless geometric diamond, surrounded by a ring of silver stars.
##### Godly Level: 
Outer Deity
##### Description/Background:
Born alongside Keldon at the dawn of Iphiros, Ulan is the primordial force of Order and Preservation. Unlike her chaotic counterpart, Ulan looked upon the fledgling creatures of Iphiros and felt a profound, maternal love. Realizing Keldon’s malice would never allow them to survive, she exhausted a massive portion of her divine power to subdue him, locking him away within a cage of stars so mortals could safely grow and flourish. Knowing she had to remain vigilant to keep Keldon bound, she appointed seven lesser deities, **The 7 Heavenly Spirits**, to watch over the mortal realm and bestow fragments of divine power upon chosen mortals to combat Keldon's lingering influence.
##### Worshippers: 
Kings, judges, protectors, community leaders, and the common folk who pray for safety under the night sky.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Nurture the Growth: Protect the innocent and allow life to flourish in peace and safety.
- Hold the Line: Stand firm against the chaotic whispers of the stars; be the shield that keeps the darkness at bay.
- Honour the Spirits: Walk the path of the 7 Heavenly Spirits, serving as their eyes and hands on Iphiros.
