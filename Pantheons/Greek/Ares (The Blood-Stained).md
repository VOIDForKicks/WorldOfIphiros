##### Description/Background:
Ares, the son of Zeus and Hera, is the Olympian god of war. Unlike his sister Athena, who commands the strategic, tactical, and just aspects of military conflict, Ares represents the raw, chaotic, and unbridled brutality of the battlefield. He is the personification of bloodlust, slaughter, and physical violence. Accompanied into battle by his sister Eris (Discord) and his sons Phobos (Panic) and Deimos (Terror), Ares thrives on the clamour of clashing steel and the scent of carnage. Despite his terrifying physical prowess and intimidating presence, Greek myth frequently depicts him as impulsive, whiny when injured, and easily outsmarted by cooler heads, most notably when he was trapped in a bronze jar by the Aloadae giants or wounded by the mortal hero Diomedes with Athena's guidance. He is famously known for his passionate, taboo love affair with Aphrodite, a union that blended raw desire with aggressive power, cementing his role as a god driven entirely by extreme, untamed mortal impulses.
##### Worshippers:
Berserkers, bloodthirsty gladiators, chaotic mercenaries, ruthless warlords, and soldiers who lose themselves to battle fury. His shrines are brutally simple structures built from uncarved stone, often situated near historic slaughter grounds, execution sites, or active military frontiers, decorated with the scavenged weapons and armour of slain enemies.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Feed the Fury: Yield completely to the adrenaline of combat; look down upon hesitation, mercy, or diplomatic restraint as signs of unforgivable weakness.
- Test Your Might: Seek out the strongest foes to prove your supremacy on the battlefield; a warrior's true worth is carved directly into the flesh of their adversaries.
- Shed the Weakness: Honour the harsh law of survival; understand that the weak are meant to be trampled by the strong, and only those with the courage to spill blood deserve to rule.
