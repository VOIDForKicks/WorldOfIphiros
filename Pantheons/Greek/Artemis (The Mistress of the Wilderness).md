Greater Deity (Olympian)
##### Description/Background:
Artemis, the twin sister of Apollo and daughter of Zeus and Leto, is the fiercely independent Olympian goddess of the hunt and untamed nature. Having requested eternal virginity from her father, she eschewed the politics of the royal courts to roam the deep, primeval forests of Greece alongside her loyal band of nymphs and hunting hounds. Artemis acts as both a protector of young wildlife and the ultimate apex predator of the woods, utilizing her flawless archery skills to bring down prey. While she can be a gentle guardian to women in labor and those living in harmony with nature, she possesses a terrifying, unforgiving wrath. She famously transformed the hunter Actaeon into a stag to be torn apart by his own hounds simply because he glimpsed her bathing, and she rained down fatal silver arrows upon the children of Niobe to avenge an insult to her mother.
##### Worshippers:
Rangers, scouts, druids, hunters, wood elves, and isolated protectorates who live far from the corruption of city walls. Her sanctuaries are rarely stone temples, manifesting instead as quiet, pristine forest clearings, hidden waterfalls, or mountain shrines adorned with shed deer antlers and silver tokens.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Protect the Wild Cradle: Defend the deep, untamed spaces of the world from aggressive civil expansion and the taint of corruption.
- Hunt with Honor: Take only what you need to survive; track your prey with absolute respect, and mercilessly punish those who poach or slaughter wildlife for vanity or cruelty.
- Guard Your Independence: Trust your own instincts above the laws of kings; never allow yourself to be shackled, tamed, or compromised by civilized politics.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Longbow or Shortbow
