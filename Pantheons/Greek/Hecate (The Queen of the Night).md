##### Description/Background
Hecate is the ancient Greek goddess of magic, witchcraft, and liminal spaces. Unlike many of her fellow Titans who were cast into Tartarus after the Titanomachy, Hecate supported Zeus and the Olympians, earning her immense respect and the right to retain her sweeping dominion over the earth, sea, and sky. She is uniquely associated with boundaries, thresholds, and three-way crossroads, places where the veil between worlds is thinnest.

Hecate played a pivotal role in the myth of Persephone's abduction. Using her signature twin torches, she helped Demeter search for her missing daughter through the pitch-black night, eventually becoming Persephone's companion and advisor in the Underworld. Often depicted in classical art as a triple-formed goddess looking in all directions simultaneously, she commands a spectral retinue of hellhounds and restless ghosts. She represents the mysterious, hidden currents of the weave, offering protection to travellers at night while simultaneously serving as the ultimate patron to those who practice secret, chthonic sorcery.
##### Worshippers
Witches, warlocks, necromancers, rogue arcane tricksters, diviners, and travellers seeking protection along dangerous highways at night. Her shrines are rarely formal temples; instead, they are small stone pillars, triple-faced statues, or simple altars placed directly at outdoor crossroads, inside deep caves, or at city gates where food offerings ("Hecate's Suppers") are left under the light of a new moon.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Walk the Threshold: Guard the boundaries between life, death, and magic; respect the liminal spaces and understand that transitions are where true power resides.
- Master the Unseen: Treat arcane knowledge, herb craft, and witchcraft with deep reverence; do not flaunt your magical gifts, but use them with calculated, silent precision.
- Light the Dark: Act as a guide for those lost in physical or metaphorical darkness, using your insight to reveal hidden truths, but never hesitate to unleash the terrors of the night upon the arrogant.
