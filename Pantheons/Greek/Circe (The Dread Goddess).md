##### Description/Background
Circe is the legendary sorceress-goddess of Greek myth, famed for her vast knowledge of illusions, transmutation, and toxic potions. Exiled to the lonely, dense island of Aeaea, she turned her secluded estate into a den of arcane experimentation. She famously used her magical brews to transform unsuspecting travelers into docile beasts, surrounding her villa with wolves and lions that retained human intelligence.

In Homer's _Odyssey_, she ensnared Odysseus's scouting party and swiftly turned them into swine. It was only when Odysseus confronted her, armed with the holy herb _moly_ gifted by Hermes to resist her enchantments, that she relented. Impressed by his immunity, she restored his crew, hosted them for a year, and used her prophetic insight to guide him safely past the Sirens and Scylla. Circe represents the untamed, intoxicating danger of magic separate from Olympian oversight, relying on active craftsmanship, herb gathering, and absolute individual agency.
##### Worshippers
Enchanters, transmuters, alchemists, hexblade warlocks, and isolated mages who weave magic through potions and nature. Her shrines are rarely public, hidden instead within dense island jungles, fragrant greenhouses, or elegant, trap-filled woodland manors where wild beasts act as guardians.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Master the Brew: Treat every herb, poison, and potion as an extension of your divine will; always let your chemistry outsmart brute strength.
- Reshape the Arrogant: Use your magic to strip away the disguises of hypocrites and fools, shifting their physical forms to reflect their true, beastly inner natures.
- Guard Your Domain: Fiercely protect your personal sanctuary and your independence from external authorities, welcoming travellers with a calculating mixture of hospitality and extreme caution.
##### Favoured Weapon
Quarterstaff or Dagger (often used to stir cauldrons or harvest mystical herbs)
