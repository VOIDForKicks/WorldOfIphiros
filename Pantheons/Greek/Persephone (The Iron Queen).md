##### Description/Background
Persephone occupies a unique dual space in Greek mythology as both the radiant maiden of spring growth (_Kore_) and the terrifying, cold Queen of the Underworld. The daughter of Zeus and Demeter, her life fractured when Hades abducted her to his subterranean kingdom. Her mother's grief caused a catastrophic global famine, forcing Zeus to broker a compromise. Because Persephone had consumed a handful of pomegranate seeds while in the dark realm, binding her legally to the land of the dead, she was fated to divide her year: spending two-thirds of her time in the mortal world above and one-third with her husband below. This cycle directly explains the seasons, blooming into spring upon her return and freezing into winter during her descent.

Classical myth reveals that Persephone is far more than a passive victim; she grew into an absolute, unyielding sovereign alongside Hades. Known to ancient mortals by terrifying euphemisms like "The Dread Goddess," her name was rarely spoken aloud out of fear. She actively governed the dead, passing cold judgments on legendary heroes and deciding who deserved mercy or eternal torment. Strikingly protective of her domain, she fiercely managed her court, famously transforming the nymph Minthe into a garden plant when she tried to seduce Hades. She perfectly embodies the cosmic paradox that life cannot exist without death, and that new growth only rises after seeds sprout in the dark.
##### Worshippers
Spore or land druids, grave domain clerics, oath of ancients paladins, farmers, and necromancers who view life and death as a seamless, beautiful cycle. Her temples reflect her split existence: above ground, they are blossoming greenhouses and open-air floral courtyards; beneath the earth, they are solemn, black-marble crypts where dead flowers are burned as incense.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Honour the Cycle: Do not look at life and death as bitter enemies; embrace both the warmth of renewal and the absolute finality of the grave as necessary halves of existence.
- Command Your Fate: Do not let yourself be defined by the tragedies or changes thrust upon you; adapt to your surroundings and turn a position of captivity into a seat of absolute power.
- Strike and Bloom: Be gentle, nurturing, and compassionate to those who seek peace, but unleash an iron-clad, unyielding wrath upon those who dare disrupt the natural balance or insult your domain.
##### Favoured Weapon
Sickle or Scythe (often wrapped in fresh ivy or dead roots)
