##### Description/Background:
Athena is the venerable Olympian goddess of wisdom and intellect. Born fully formed and clad in gleaming armour directly from the forehead of her father, Zeus, she stands as the divine counterweight to the chaotic, bloodthirsty violence of her brother Ares. Athena represents the tactical, defensive, and strategic side of military conflict, favouring discipline, justice, and cunning over mindless slaughter. She is a dedicated patron of civilization, philosophy, and practical arts, having famously gifted the first olive tree to Athens to secure her position as the city's protector. Renowned for her level-headedness, she acted as the divine advisor to Greece's greatest mythic heroes, including Odysseus, Perseus, and Heracles, guiding them with cool logic rather than emotional impulse. Her personal shield, the Aegis, bears the terrifying visage of the Gorgon Medusa, serving as a stark warning that her calm, reasonable nature can instantly give way to unyielding, iron-clad resolve when defending cosmic law and civilization.
##### Worshippers:
Tacticians, military generals, scholars, philosophers, paladins of justice, and master artisans. Her temples are architectural masterpieces located at the highest points of major cities, functioning as combination military nerve centres, legal courts, and grand libraries where knowledge is guarded and debated.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Victory Through Strategy: Never rush blindly into conflict; evaluate your enemy, plan your manoeuvres, and understand that a battle won without unnecessary bloodshed is the ultimate triumph.
- Uphold Civilization and Law: Protect the structural foundations of society, advocating for fair laws, education, rational discourse, and the growth of practical craftsmanship.
- Temper Might with Reason: Let your sword be guided by a clear mind and a righteous heart; never yield to base bloodlust or petty vengeance.
