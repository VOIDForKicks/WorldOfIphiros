##### Description/Background
Gaia (or Gaea) is the primordial personification of the Earth itself, emerging out of Chaos at the very dawn of creation. As one of the foundational building blocks of the universe, she gave birth to the sky (Uranus), the mountains (Ourea), and the sea (Pontus) without a partner. She then united with Uranus to birth the Titans, the Cyclopes, and the Hundred-Handed Ones (Hekatonkheires).

Gaia's mythology is defined by her protective instinct toward her offspring and her fierce resistance to tyranny. When Uranus imprisoned her monstrous children in the deep dark of Tartarus, she forged a legendary flint sickle and conspired with her youngest Titan son, Cronus, to overthrow and castrate his father. Centuries later, when Cronus proved to be an equally cruel tyrant by swallowing his own children, Gaia aided Rhea in hiding the infant Zeus, setting the stage for the Titanomachy. Yet, she is not explicitly a friend to the Olympians; when Zeus locked her Titan children away in turn, a furious Gaia birthed the terrifying storm-giant Typhon and led the Gigantes in a massive rebellion against Mount Olympus. She represents the absolute, uncaring, and ancient power of the planet, nurturing and giving life to all things, yet fully capable of shifting continents to swallow whole pantheons.
##### Worshippers
Archdruids, stone sorcerers, earth genasi, hermits, and elder races who feel a connection to the planet older than the gods themselves. Her sanctuaries are deep subterranean caverns, natural monolithic archways, tectonic rifts, or ancient valleys untouched by any mortal tool or architecture.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Rooted in the Soil: Honor the earth beneath your feet as the true origin and final destination of all living things; fiercely oppose anything that would fundamentally poison or corrupt the land.
- Oppress the Tyrant: Stand alongside the subjugated and the hidden; never allow a self-proclaimed king or god to permanently seal away or mistreat their kin.
- Endure Like the Mountain: Maintain a deep, unshakeable patience, knowing that civilizations, empires, and even gods are mere fleeting moments compared to the timeless lifespan of the earth.
