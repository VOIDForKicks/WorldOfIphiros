A golden bow entwined with blooming red roses, or a single silver-tipped arrow piercing a heart.
##### Godly Level
Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background
Eros is the primordial force of attraction and the embodiment of the spark that draws beings together. Often depicted as a winged youth or a mischievous child, he is the son of Aphrodite, though his influence predates the Olympian order itself. He is both the gentle affection that binds souls and the overwhelming, chaotic passion that ignores all logic and duty.

Eros is a fickle and impulsive power. He does not care for mortal politics, social standing, or divine laws; he cares only for the magnetic pull of the heart. His arrows, some tipped with gold to inspire uncontrollable desire, others with lead to induce disdain, have brought even the greatest gods and heroes to their knees. He is not malicious, but he is dangerous, for he treats the lives of mortals as a grand, unfolding romance. Those who honour him find their lives filled with beauty and intensity, while those who scorn his influence often find their hearts withered or their lives turned toward cold, empty ambition.
##### Worshippers
Lovers, poets, bards, diplomats, hopeless romantics, and those who believe that connection is the only thing worth fighting for.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Follow the Heart: Trust your instincts above cold logic; the heart recognizes truths that the mind often misses.
- Foster Connection: Seek to bridge divides and bring people together, for a world without affection is a world of shadows.
- Embrace the Unpredictable: Do not fear the chaos of love; allow it to reshape your path and open your eyes to the beauty of the unknown.
