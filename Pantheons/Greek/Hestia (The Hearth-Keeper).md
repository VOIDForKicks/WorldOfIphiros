##### Description/Background
Hestia is the serene, deeply revered goddess of the hearth fire, the home, and the right ordering of domestic and civic life. As the firstborn child of the Titans Cronus and Rhea, she holds the unique mythological distinction of being both the eldest of the original Olympians and, having been the last to be disgorged by her father, the youngest. Swearing a solemn oath to Zeus to remain eternally pure and independent, she completely removed herself from the chaotic rivalries, romantic scandals, and violent power struggles that consumed the rest of the pantheon.

Because the hearth fire was the absolute centre of survival in ancient Greece, providing vital warmth, food preparation, and sacrificial connections to the divine, Hestia was honoured above all others. Every meal began and ended with a small libation poured to her, and she automatically received the first portion of every public sacrifice. She is the literal embodiment of hospitality (**xenia**) and sanctuary. When Dionysus ascended to godhood, Hestia gracefully surrendered her golden throne on the ruling Olympian council to him to prevent a civil war among the deities, contenting herself to sit by the central hearth of Mount Olympus, quietly tending the eternal flame that sustained the cosmic court.
##### Worshippers
Healers, community leaders, pacifists, redemption paladins, common peasants, and life domain clerics who protect communal safe havens. Her sanctuaries are found inside every civic hall and family home; her formal temples are welcoming circular buildings where a public eternal flame is guarded day and night, offering absolute, iron-clad sanctuary to any refugee, traveller, or weary soul who steps across the threshold.
###### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Kindle the Hearth: Nurture and protect your community, ensuring that the weak are fed, the homeless are sheltered, and the flame of civility is never allowed to die.
- Offer Unconditional Sanctuary: Honour the laws of hospitality; grant safe harbour and protection even to your bitterest enemies if they seek peace beside your fire.
- Keep the Inner Peace: Guard your mind against petty rivalries, anger, and political ambition; understand that true strength lies in quiet stability and unwavering gentleness.
