##### Description/Background
Zagreus is a highly enigmatic figure deeply rooted in Orphic mystery traditions, where he is worshipped as the "firstborn Dionysus." According to these ancient mystic accounts, he was the son of Zeus and Persephone, conceived in secret before her full descent into the Underworld. Zeus favoured the infant child immensely, even placing him upon the throne of Olympus and intending him to inherit supreme cosmic rule. This favouritism incited a lethal jealousy in Hera, who hired the Titans to assassinate the young god. Distracting the child with toys and a mirror, the Titans ambushed Zagreus, tore him into pieces, and devoured his flesh.

However, Athena managed to rescue his still-beating heart and delivered it to Zeus. In an act of divine rebirth, Zeus used the heart to fashion a new body, or fed it to the mortal princess Semele, allowing Zagreus to be reborn into the world as the god Dionysus. Because of this horrific fragmentation and subsequent resurrection, Zagreus is viewed as a master of rebirth and a bridge between mortal suffering and divine immortality. He represents the hidden, cyclical secrets of life emerging from absolute destruction, and the wild, raw vitality of the bloodline.
##### Worshippers
Hedge knights, monster hunters, rogue swashbucklers, tieflings or genasi struggling with complex divine/fiendish heritages, and secret cultists of the Orphic mysteries who reject standard temple dogmas. His shrines are rarely found in cities; they are tucked away in subterranean hunting lodges, deep forest ruins, or hidden backrooms of fighting pits where blood offerings and dynamic toasts are made in his name.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Defy the Inevitable: Never accept a grim fate or captivity without a fight; break through any walls, labyrinths, or divine edicts that try to keep you trapped.
- Honour the Rebirth: Understand that great power and wisdom often come from being broken apart; help others pick up their pieces and rise stronger from their personal tragedies.
- Fight for Family: Protect the bonds of blood and chosen kin; hold a deep, unshakeable loyalty to those who stand by you, even when the entire world, or the gods themselves, demands their isolation.
