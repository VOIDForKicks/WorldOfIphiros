##### Holy Symbol
A set of scales balanced upon a sword, or a broken wheel.
##### Godly Level
Minor Deity
##### Description/Background
Nemesis is the divine force of righteous retribution, the cold arm of fate that punishes those who succumb to hubris or defy the cosmic order. She is not a goddess of mindless vengeance, but of necessary balance; when a mortal becomes too arrogant, hoards too much wealth, or upsets the natural equilibrium of the world, Nemesis is the one who steps in to restore the scales. She is often depicted with wings to signify her swiftness and a measuring rod to signify the precise nature of her judgment. She is the quiet, chilling reminder that no one, not even kings or heroes, is above the consequences of their actions.
##### Worshippers
Judges, bounty hunters, disgraced knights seeking atonement, victims of grave injustice, and those who believe that every debt must eventually be paid.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Balance the Scales: Ensure that every action has its equal reaction; do not let the arrogant go unpunished or the suffering go unheard.
- Observe the Hubristic: Watch for those whose pride blinds them to the laws of the universe, and be the instrument that brings them back to earth.
- Seek Measured Justice: Retribution must be proportional to the offense; do not allow anger to turn into blind cruelty, for justice requires a steady hand.
