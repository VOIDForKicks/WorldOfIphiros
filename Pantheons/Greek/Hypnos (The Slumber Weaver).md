##### Description/Background
Hypnos is the ancient Greek personification of sleep, born of the primordial personification of night, Nyx, and darkness, Erebus. He resides in a dim, silent cavern within the Underworld, located near the palace of Hades. The River Lethe (Forgetfulness) flows through his misty domain, where the air is thick with the scent of magical poppies that induce deep slumber. Hypnos is the twin brother of Thanatos (Peaceful Death) and the father of the Oneiroi, the spirits of dreams led by Morpheus.

Unlike the more violent chthonic deities, Hypnos is a calm, gentle spirit who brings rest, relief, and healing to weary mortals by temporarily unshackling them from their waking troubles. However, his power over sleep is absolute and can overpower even the mightiest immortals. In the _Iliad_, Queen Hera bribed Hypnos with the gift of a golden throne and marriage to Pasithea, one of the Graces, to lull Zeus to sleep. Hypnos succeeded, blinding the King of the Gods to the battlefield of Troy and altering the course of the war. He represents the silent, pervasive power of rest, a state where all mortals, heroes, and gods are stripped of their active will.
##### Worshippers
Insomniacs, dream-weavers, spies who strike under the cover of night, twilight domain clerics, and weary adventurers seeking absolute rest. His shrines are cosy, quiet chambers hidden away in subterranean structures, filled with soft furs, heavy incense, and obsidian bowls filled with sleepy water or crushed poppy seeds.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Guard the Dream Realm: Protect the minds of the innocent while they sleep, hunting down malevolent entities, night hags, or demons that twist natural slumber into horrific nightmares.
- Bring Peace to the Weary: Provide comfort, healing, and absolute rest to those broken by the trials of the waking world; view sleep as a sacred medicine that restores the soul.
- Master the Quiet Inevitability: Understand that all things must eventually rest; do not fear the dark or the quiet, but embrace them as the natural counterweights to active life.
##### Favoured Weapon
Quarterstaff or Blowgun (used to fire sleep-inducing darts)
