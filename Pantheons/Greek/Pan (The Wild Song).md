##### Godly Level
Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background
Pan is the boisterous, goat-legged god of the untamed wilderness. Unlike the refined Olympians who dwell in marble halls, Pan haunts the deep forests, mountain caves, and sun-drenched pastures. He is a paradoxical deity: a source of both pastoral peace and sudden, irrational terror. The word "panic" is derived from his name, originating from the stories of him letting out a shriek so loud and disorienting that it would send entire armies fleeing in confusion. He is a creature of pure instinct, mirthful, lusty, and fundamentally incompatible with the constraints of "civilized" society. To follow Pan is to leave the city walls behind and embrace the raw, unpolished truth of the natural world.
##### Worshippers
Rangers, druids, satyrs, shepherds, wilderness explorers, and those who feel stifled by urban life and seek the freedom of the wild.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Preserve the Wild: Protect the sanctity of untamed places; civilization should respect the boundaries of the forest, not conquer them.
- Embrace Your Instincts: Do not suppress your natural urges or hide from your emotions; live with the same raw honesty as the creatures of the woods.
- Celebrate Life’s Rhythm: Find joy in the song of the birds and the rustle of leaves; honor the spirit of play and the freedom of the open road.
