##### Description/Background
Hades is the stern, unyielding ruler of the Greek Underworld and the eldest son of the Titans Cronus and Rhea. Following the overthrow of their father, Hades and his brothers Zeus and Poseidon drew lots to divide the cosmos; Hades drew the subterranean depths, effectively separating him from the sunny peaks of Mount Olympus. Often misunderstood as an evil or malicious deity due to modern associations with death, Hades was viewed by the ancient Greeks as a dispassionate, completely fair administrator of cosmic balance. He is not a judge of souls himself, nor is he a torturer; rather, he is the cold, legalistic guardian who ensures that the laws of mortality are respected and that no soul ever escapes his dark domain.

He is also known as _Plouton_ (The Wealthy One) because he commands all the precious metals, minerals, and agricultural seeds buried within the earth. His most famous myth involves his abduction of Demeter's daughter, Persephone, to be his queen. Though their union began in deceit, classical mythology portrays Hades as a remarkably faithful and respectful husband compared to his Olympian brothers. He possesses the Helm of Terror (or Invisibility), a divine artefact forged by the Cyclopes that strikes absolute panic into the hearts of mortals and gods alike.
##### Worshippers
Necromancy or grave domain clerics, miners, geologists, ancestral guardians, and judges who value strict, impartial execution of the law. His temples are never built in bustling markets; instead, they are subterranean complexes, dark marble mausoleums, or open pits where black animal sacrifices are offered while looking down toward the earth.

##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Preserve the Finality: Treat the boundary between life and death as an iron-clad law; actively hunt down intelligent undead, liches, or fools trying to cheat their destined time.
- Maintain Impartial Justice: Cast aside personal bias, pity, and emotion when rendering a verdict; treat the beggar and the high emperor with the exact same legal scrutiny.
- Guard the Earth's Bounty: Respect the subterranean riches of the world, protecting ancient troves and deep caverns from greed-driven pillagers who disrupt the natural balance.
