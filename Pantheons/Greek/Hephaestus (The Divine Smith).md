##### Description/Background
Hephaestus is the master artisan of the gods, the celestial blacksmith who fashions the weapons, armour, and palaces of Olympus. Cast from Mount Olympus in his infancy due to his perceived imperfection, he survived the fall and turned his tragedy into a life of unrivalled creation. He resides in the heart of volcanoes, where the sound of his hammer rings out as the heartbeat of the earth itself.

Despite being mistreated by his kin, he remains the most industrious of the gods, valuing the tangible results of labour and the ingenuity required to shape raw materials into greatness. He is a god of patience, trial-and-error, and the quiet dignity of a job done well. He does not seek glory, but he demands respect for the process of creation. Those who rush their work or produce shoddy goods earn his silent, heavy judgment.
##### Worshippers
Blacksmiths, engineers, architects, inventors, miners, and warriors who rely on finely crafted equipment.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Respect the Craft: Every object created with care has a soul; never accept half-measures or settle for subpar results.
- Mastery Through Labor: Knowledge is earned at the forge; refine your skills constantly through practice and patience.
- Shape the World: Use your ingenuity to improve the lives of others, building structures and tools that endure long after you are gone.
