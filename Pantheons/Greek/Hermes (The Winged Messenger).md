##### Description/Background
Hermes is the clever, quick-witted messenger of the gods and the son of Zeus and the Pleiad Maia. Operating as the ultimate divine bridge between Mount Olympus, the mortal realm, and the dark depths of the Underworld, he possesses unparalleled swiftness. He is traditionally depicted as a handsome, athletic youth wearing winged sandals (_talaria_), a winged petasos hat, and carrying the _caduceus_, a sacred staff that can induce sleep, wake the slumbering, or guide souls.

Hermes was a trickster from the very day he was born; as an infant, he slipped out of his cradle, stole the sacred cattle of his brother Apollo, and cunningly crafted the first lyre from a tortoise shell to soothe Apollo's resulting rage. Because he roams freely across all boundaries, he is revered as the _Psychopomp_, the gentle guide who escorts the shades of the dead down to Charon's ferry. He is the ultimate patron of travellers, merchants, and gamblers who rely on luck and wit, but he is equally praised by thieves and spies who look to his slippery, elusive nature for divine protection. Quick with a joke and even quicker with a plan, he serves as the invaluable problem-solver of the Olympian pantheon.
##### Worshippers
Rogues, bards, horizon walker rangers, traveling merchants, diplomats, messengers, and trickery domain clerics. His shrines are rarely massive temple complexes; instead, they are small stone pillars or cairns called _herms_ placed along trade routes, at major city crossroads, and outside tavern doors to bring travellers luck and safe passage.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Keep Moving Forward: Never allow yourself or others to become stagnant; celebrate travel, exploration, and the fluid exchange of ideas and cultures across borders.
- Outwit, Don't Outmuscle: Rely on your silver tongue, clever schemes, and sharp reflexes to bypass obstacles; violence is a clumsy substitute for a perfectly executed trick.
- Protect the Traveller: Act as a guardian to those walking dangerous roads, offering aid to wandering souls, merchants, and refugees fleeing tyranny.
