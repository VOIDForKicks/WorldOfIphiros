##### Description/Background
Zeus is the supreme ruler of Mount Olympus, the King of the Gods, and the absolute authority over the sky, weather, law, and cosmic justice. The youngest child of the Titans Cronus and Rhea, he escaped being swallowed by his father thanks to a deception orchestrated by his mother. Raised in secret on the island of Crete, he returned as an adult to poison Cronus, forcing him to disgorge his swallowed siblings. Zeus then led his brothers and sisters, alongside the freed Cyclopes and Hundred-Handed Ones, in a cataclysmic ten-year war known as the Titanomachy to overthrow the old regime. When dividing the universe with his brothers Poseidon and Hades, Zeus drew the sky, claiming ultimate kingship over the cosmos.

His absolute authority is enforced by the Master Bolt, an apocalyptic weapon forged for him by the Cyclopes that can shatter mountains and strike down rogue immortals. Zeus is also the divine guardian of **xenia** (the sacred law of hospitality) and state governance, punishing oath-breakers, tyrants, and anyone who threatens the fundamental order of the world. However, his mythology is equally defined by a chaotic romantic nature; he frequently transformed himself into various animals and golden showers to bypass guardians and seduce mortal women and nymphs. This insatiable nature resulted in a vast lineage of demigod heroes (like Heracles and Perseus) and divine heirs, routinely stoking the terrifying, world-altering wrath of his queen, Hera.
##### Worshippers
Emperors, warlords, high judges, paladins of devotion or the crown, tempest domain clerics, and blue or bronze dragonborn who value absolute authority and structural law. His temples are the most extravagant monuments in the world, colossal, open-roof marble pantheons built on the absolute highest peaks of mountain ranges or at the literal centre of grand imperial capitals.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Command the High Court: Establish and enforce absolute law, order, and societal structure; chaos and rebellion must be struck down swiftly with overwhelming force.
- Uphold Absolute Justice: Act as an unyielding judge, protecting the laws of hospitality and contracts; false rulers and oath-breakers deserve no mercy.
- Display Regal Might: Never show weakness, hesitation, or fear to those beneath your station; carry yourself with the absolute confidence and terrifying presence of a storm king.
