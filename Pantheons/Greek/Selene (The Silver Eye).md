Lesser Deity
##### Description/Background
Selene is the personification of the moon, a radiant and ancient deity who drives her shining chariot across the velvet expanse of the night sky. She is the watcher of the dreaming world, a gentle presence that provides light to the lost and inspiration to the restless. Unlike the scorching intensity of her brother, Helios (the Sun), Selene’s light is reflective, calm, and transformative. She governs the subtle shifts of the tides and the shifting phases of the psyche. She is often associated with the melancholic beauty of the night, holding a deep, singular love for the mortal Endymion, a shepherd whom she granted eternal, ageless sleep so she could visit him nightly in his dreamscape.
##### Worshippers
Navigators, mystics, dream-interpreters, those who work by moonlight, and individuals seeking clarity amidst confusion.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Guide the Lost: Be a light to those wandering in physical or metaphorical darkness; offer guidance to the weary. 
- Honor the Cycle: Respect the natural ebb and flow of life; understand that there is a season for action and a season for quiet reflection.
- Seek Inner Truth: The moon reveals what the sun obscures; trust your intuition and pay close attention to your dreams, for they carry hidden wisdom.
