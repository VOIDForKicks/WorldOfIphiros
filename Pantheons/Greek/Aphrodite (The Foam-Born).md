##### Description/Background:
Aphrodite is the ancient Greek goddess of love, desire, and physical beauty. According to Hesiod's _Theogony_, she was born from the white foam generated by the severed genitals of the primordial sky god Uranus, drifting ashore on the island of Cyprus. Renowned as the most beautiful being on Mount Olympus, her presence inherently kindles irresistible infatuation and romantic passion among mortals and immortals alike. While she can be a fiercely benevolent patron of true lovers and artists, Aphrodite possesses a notoriously fickle, vain, and vindictive streak. She rarely hesitates to inflict devastating, tragic curses or ruinous obsession upon anyone who insults her beauty, neglects her altars, or rejects the power of love, such as the ill-fated Narcissus or the tragic Queen Pasiphaë. She is intricately linked to the catalyst of the Trojan War, having bribed Paris with the love of Helen of Troy in exchange for the golden apple of discord.
##### Worshippers:
Artists, bards, actors, lovers, courtesans, and individuals seeking to find or protect romance. Her temples are architectural marvels of fine marble situated on coastal cliffs or inside fragrant, blooming rose gardens, filled with smooth reflection pools and dynamic statues celebrating the idealized mortal form.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Celebrate Desire: Honour love, physical beauty, and heartfelt romance as the ultimate, joyful expressions of the mortal soul.
- Reject Cruel Asceticism: Oppose those who seek to entirely suppress passion, emotional intimacy, or aesthetic pleasure out of bitterness or rigid, unfeeling dogma.
- Protect the Inspired: Defend creators, poets, and artists who capture the radiant essence of beauty, and punish the arrogant who believe themselves above the sway of love.
