True Neutral
##### Holy Symbol:
A single gold coin (an obol) or a skeletal hand gripping an oar.
##### Godly Level:
Minor Deity / Chthonic Spirit (Daemon)
##### Description/Background:
Charon is the grim, steadfast ferryman of the Greek Underworld, born of the primordial deities Erebus (Darkness) and Nyx (Night). His sacred duty is to steer his skiff across the dark, swirling waters of the rivers Acheron and Styx, transporting the shades of the recently deceased into the realm of Hades. However, passage is never free; Charon strictly demands a toll of a single obol coin, traditionally placed in the mouth of the deceased during burial rituals. Those who died penniless, or whose bodies were left unburied without proper rites, are coldly refused entry, forced to wander the desolate, misty shores of the river as restless ghosts for a hundred years. Unyielding, silent, and immune to mortal pleading, Charon fiercely guards the boundary of the dead. He only yields his oar to those who present a golden bough or to rare, legendary heroes possessing overwhelming divine mandate or tragic leverage, such as Orpheus, Heracles, or Psyche.
##### Worshippers:
Undertakers, grave diggers, sailors who navigate treacherous subterranean waters, and grave domain clerics who oversee funerary rites. His shrines are sombre, dimly lit subterranean vaults or lonely stone docks built beside stagnant marshes, black lakes, or deep cavern rivers where coins are dropped into the depths as offerings.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Enforce the Toll: Ensure that the dead receive their proper rites and are buried with their symbolic passage tokens, maintaining the sacred economic balance of the afterlife.
- Guard the Crossing: Prevent the living from illegally crossing into the realms of the dead, and ruthlessly hunt down spirits that attempt to flee back across the water into the material world.
- Maintain Stoic Neutrality: Do not show favour, pity, or malice to the souls in your charge; treat the peasant, the king, the hero, and the villain with the exact same quiet, cold indifference.
##### Favoured Weapon:
Quarterstaff (styled as a heavy wooden ferryman's oar)
