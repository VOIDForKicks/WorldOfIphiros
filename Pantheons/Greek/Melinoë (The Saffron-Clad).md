##### Description/Background
Melinoë is a mysterious, ancient chthonic nymph-goddess invoking the terror of nightmares and mental madness. According to the Orphic Hymns, her lineage is born from a striking act of divine deception: Zeus disguised himself as Hades, the ruler of the Underworld, to seduce Persephone at the mouth of the river Cocytus. Because of this dual, conflicting parentage, Melinoë was born with a body split down the middle, one half cast in radiant, divine Olympian light, and the other half shrouded in the deep, pitch-black dark of the chthonic realms.

Melinoë wanders the mortal realm during the dead of night accompanied by a silent entourage of restless ghosts. She possesses the terrifying ability to drive mortals to sudden madness by manifesting as bizarre, shifting apparitions, morphing through shapes that embody her victim’s deepest psychological fears. Her presence is a stark reminder of the inescapable anxieties hidden within the dark corners of the human mind, bridging the pristine realm of the living with the unsettling, unresolved trauma of the dead.
##### Worshippers
Illusionists, whisper bards, aberrant mind sorcerers, rogue phantoms, and death domain clerics who seek to control or banish psychological terror. Her shrines are kept away from populated cities, hidden in mist-veiled cemetery groves, abandoned catacombs, or deep, echo-filled caverns where worshippers leave silver coins and offer libations of dark wine to ward off her nightly terrors.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Master the Nightmare: Learn to weaponize the fear and hidden anxieties of your enemies; understand that a mind broken by terror is defeated before the sword is even drawn.
- Guide the Restless: Offer rites and solace to earth-bound phantoms and ghosts who wander the world in madness, easing their confusion or directing their vengeance purposefully.
- Honor the Dual Nature: Balance the light you project to the world with a deep, unblinking understanding of the darkness festering within; never deny the shadows of the soul.
