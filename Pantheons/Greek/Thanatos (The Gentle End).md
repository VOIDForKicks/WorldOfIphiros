##### Mythology
Greek
##### Domain (Mythological)
Death, Mortality, Peaceful Passing, and the Finality of Life
##### D&D Domains
Death, Grave, Peace, Patience
##### Alignment
Lawful Neutral
##### Holy Symbol
An inverted torch, symbolizing the extinguishing of life's flame.
##### Godly Level
Intermediate Deity
##### Description/Background
Thanatos is the personification of non-violent death, the winged psychopomp who arrives when a mortal's thread is finally cut by the Fates. Unlike the chaotic terror of a gruesome demise, Thanatos represents the natural, inevitable transition from existence to the afterlife. He is often depicted as a sombre, beautiful youth, carrying a sword to sever the silver cord of life. Though feared by mortals, he is not malicious; he is an impartial servant of the cosmic order. He performs his duty with a quiet, solemn professionalism, ensuring that the dead are guided safely to their final resting place and that the transition is as painless as the laws of the universe allow.
##### Worshippers
Morticians, hospice healers, judges of the dead, weary travellers who embrace the inevitable, and those who seek to ensure that all souls find peace after their final breath.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Respect the Finality: Death is a natural transition, not a defeat; honour the moment of passing with solemn dignity. 
- Oppose the Unnatural: Root out the undead and those who seek to cheat the grave, for denying the end is a violation of the cosmic order.
- Provide Peaceful Passage: Alleviate the suffering of the dying whenever possible, and ensure the departed are remembered with grace.
##### Favoured Weapon
Sickle or Scythe
