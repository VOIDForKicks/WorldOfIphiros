##### Description/Background
Helios is the primordial personification of the Sun in Greek mythology. Every morning, crowned with the glittering aureole of the sun, he rises from the eastern ocean (Oceanus) in a magnificent chariot forged of gold and fire, driven by four fierce, blazing steeds. He races across the sky to bring light to mortals and gods alike, descending in the evening into the west, where he floats back to his eastern palace in a giant golden cup.

Because his daily journey takes him above the entire world, Helios is renowned as the "All-Seeing" witness to all earthly deeds and secrets. It was Helios who witnessed Hades abducting Persephone and revealed the truth to a grieving Demeter. Though he generally stood apart from Olympian politics, his wrath could be devastating. In the _Odyssey_, when Odysseus's starving crew slaughtered his sacred, immortal cattle on the island of Thrinacia, a furious Helios threatened Zeus that he would abandon the sky and shine the sun exclusively in the Underworld instead. Terrified of a world thrown into cosmic imbalance, Zeus swiftly struck Odysseus's ship down with a thunderbolt to appease the solar Titan.
##### Worshippers
Paladins of devotion, light domain clerics, watch guards, judges, and truth-seekers who swear solemn oaths. His temples are open-air circular stone rings built on the highest mountain peaks or major east-facing coastal cliffs, designed so his rays illuminate the central altar at the exact moment of dawn.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Banish the Shadows: Act as an unwavering light in the darkness; bring hidden betrayals, lies, and crimes out into the open for all to see.
- Uphold Your Oath: Treat a sworn promise as a cosmic law; never break your word, and fiercely punish oath-breakers who swear false testimonies in the light of day.
- Maintain the Cosmic Order: Rise to your duties every single day without fail or complaint; let your discipline be as constant and unyielding as the path of the sun.
