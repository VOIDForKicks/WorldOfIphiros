##### Description/Background
Hera is the majestic and formidable Queen of the Gods, ruling Mount Olympus alongside her husband and brother, Zeus. The daughter of the Titans Cronus and Rhea, she serves as the divine patroness of marriage, monogamy, and the sanctity of the family unit. In classical art, she is traditionally depicted as a regal, matronly figure crowned with a royal diadem and holding a pomegranate, a symbol of fertility and marital union.

Despite her role as the guardian of the home, Hera’s mythology is dominated by her fierce, unyielding vengeance. Tormented by Zeus’s endless marital infidelities, she routinely directed her divine fury not at her king, but at his mortal lovers and their illegitimate offspring. She famously sent a pair of serpents to strangle the infant Heracles in his cradle, drove the princess Semele to her fiery death, and cursed the nymph Echo to only repeat the words of others. As a political powerhouse, her favoured city was Argos, and she played a critical, unwavering role in steering the destruction of Troy during the Trojan War to avenge the insult of Paris's judgment. She represents the iron-clad authority of law, family lineage, and the terrifying rage of a betrayed sovereign.
##### Worshippers
Queens, noble rulers, matriarchs, paladins of the crown, legal judges, and clerics of order who oversee political alliances and wedding vows. Her temples are grand, strictly organized marble cathedrals located in the aristocratic quarters of major imperial capitals, often guarded by highly disciplined knights and filled with tame, free-roaming peacocks.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Protect the Sacred Vow: Uphold the sanctity of marriage, legal contracts, and sworn oaths; relentlessly hunt down those who commit treachery, betrayal, or oath-breaking.
- Enforce Rightful Rule: Support legitimate authority, societal hierarchies, and structural order; chaotic rebellion and the fracturing of family lineages are heresies against the crown.
- Avenge the Slit: Never let an insult to your dignity, your house, or your family line pass without a calculated, absolute response; let your enemies learn that royalty does not tolerate disrespect.
