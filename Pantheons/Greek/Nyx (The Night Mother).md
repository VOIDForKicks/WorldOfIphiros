##### Description/Background
Nyx is the primordial personification of the Night, an ancient and unimaginably powerful entity who emerged at the very dawn of creation from Chaos. Residing in a shadowy palace in the deepest depths of Tartarus, she is a foundational force of the cosmos. Nyx stands as one of the most revered and feared figures in Greek mythology; even Zeus, the king of the gods, holds a deep, trembling respect for her. In Homer's _Iliad_, when Hypnos fled to Nyx for protection after angering Zeus, Zeus held back his wrath, fearing to do anything that would displease the ancient goddess of the night.

Nyx is the mother of an immense lineage of powerful cosmic forces. Partnered with Erebus (Darkness), she gave birth to Aether (Light) and Hemera (Day). On her own, she birthed a sweeping retinue of chthonic abstractions that govern the mortal condition, including Thanatos (Death), Hypnos (Sleep), the Oneiroi (Dreams), Nemesis (Retribution), the Moirai (The Fates), and Eris (Strife). She represents the vast, beautiful, and terrifying unknown of the dark, an absolute power that existed before the gods of Mount Olympus drew breath and will remain long after they fade.
##### Worshippers
Gloom stalker rangers, twilight domain clerics, shadow sorcerers, astronomers, assassins who hunt in absolute darkness, and philosophers who study the origin of the planes. Her sanctuaries are deep, unlit cavern vaults, astronomical observatories open to the midnight sky, or ancient monoliths standing alone in uncharted wilderness where the stars shine brightest.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Revere the Dark: View the night not as a place of evil, but as a sacred veil of rest, mystery, and primordial beauty that balance the harsh light of day.
- Protect the Secrets: Guard ancient mysteries, forbidden knowledge, and hidden truths from those who would exploit them; some things are meant to remain cloaked in shadow.
- Acknowledge the Inevitable: Respect the cosmic order and the ancient forces of fate, retribution, and time, knowing that all mortal and divine empires eventually return to the dark.
##### Favoured Weapon
Dagger or Sickle (often styled as a crescent moon)
