##### Description/Background:
Apollo, the twin brother of Artemis and son of Zeus and Leto, is one of the most complex and revered Olympian deities. He is the ultimate embodiment of Hellenic ideals, commanding a vast array of domains that bridge civilized sophistication with terrifying cosmic power. As the master of the lyre, he brings harmony and order to the divine courts, yet his silver bow can rain down devastating arrows of pestilence upon societies that breach cosmic laws. Apollo is famously linked to the Oracle of Delphi, established after he famously slew the monstrous serpent Python; through his high priestesses, he channels absolute, unvarnished truth and prophetic foresight to kings and heroes alike. Though traditionally depicted as an idealized, radiant, and ageless youth who champions enlightenment, medicine, and mathematics, he is also fiercely prideful. He famously flayed the satyr Marsyas alive for daring to challenge his musical supremacy, serving as a chilling reminder that even the most brilliant light carries a scorching, merciless heat.
##### Worshippers:
Bards, poets, musicians, medical healers, paladins of ancient oaths, celestial warlocks, and elite archers. His grand temples are built from flawless white marble, positioned on high hills or adjacent to natural hot springs and sacred, steam-venting chasms where seers gather to read the threads of fate.

##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Bring Truth to Light: Unmask deception, expose hidden conspiracies, and speak with unvarnished honesty, using your voice and insight to banish the shadows of ignorance.
- Cultivate Harmony: Treat performance, music, and art as sacred geometry for the soul; strive to bring aesthetic balance, healing, and artistic enlightenment to the communities you visit.
- Strike with Precision: Do not unleash chaotic or mindless violence; let your combat strikes be as deliberate, swift, and clean as a masterfully placed arrow from a silver bow.
