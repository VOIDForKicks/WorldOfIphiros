##### Description/Background
Poseidon is the tempestuous ruler of the seas and oceans, and one of the three foundational brothers, alongside Zeus and Hades, who overthrew the Titan Cronus. When dividing the cosmos by drawing lots, Poseidon claimed the vast aquatic realms, commanding everything from the deepest marine trenches to the crashing coastal waves. Known to ancient mortals as the "Earth-Shaker," his moods dictate the safety of anyone who dares to step off dry land; with a single strike of his divine trident, he can trigger catastrophic earthquakes, shatter mountains, tear open new springs, or whip the oceans into apocalyptic hurricanes.

In classical mythology, Poseidon is portrayed as exceptionally prideful, fickle, and quick to take offense. He famously fought Athena for patronage over the city of Athens, offering mortals the first horse or a saltwater spring, but lost to her gift of the olive tree, a slight he resented for centuries. His wrath is perhaps most famously detailed in the _Odyssey_, where he relentlessly torments the hero Odysseus for blinding his cyclops son, Polyphemus, strands him at sea for a decade, and destroys his ships. Despite his volatile nature, he is also credited as the creator of horses, having shaped them out of breaking sea foam, representing the wild, untameable grace of his domain.
##### Worshippers
Sailors, pirates, coastal merchants, fishermen, tritons, and tempest domain clerics who treat the sea with deep, fearful respect. His temples are massive, open-air stone fortresses built directly on dangerous cliff edges or half-submerged in coral reefs, where travellers drown grand treasures, fine wine, or white horses in the surf to secure safe passage before setting sail.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Command the Storm: Never shy away from your internal passion or power; let your wrath be as absolute and destructive as a tidal wave when provoked.
- Respect the Deep: Keep a healthy, fearful reverence for the unknown depths of the world, and ensure that those who treat the oceans with arrogance are swiftly humbled.
- Claim Your Sovereignty: Defend your territory, your crew, and your honor with absolute ferocity; never allow a rival to slight your name without drowning their ambitions.
