##### Description/Background
Epona (whose name derives from the Gaulish word for "Great Mare") is a highly prominent Celtic protector goddess whose worship extended far beyond tribal lands, famously becoming the only foreign deity to be granted her own official feast day and shrine network within imperial Rome. She is classically depicted as a serene woman sitting side-saddle atop a majestic white mare, or standing proudly surrounded by spirited wild colts. She holds a _cornucopia_ (horn of plenty) or baskets overflowing with ears of corn and grain, representing her dual role as a goddess of livestock abundance and a provider for working beasts.

Epona is the ultimate guardian of the bond between horse and rider. In the ancient world, where a community’s survival, trade, and military prowess depended entirely on equine health, Epona was a vital economic pillar. She protects stable hands, cavalrymen, and travellers traversing dangerous frontier roads. Beyond her earthly agricultural domains, she possesses a distinct psychopomp role in Celtic folklore: she guides the souls of fallen warriors and travellers across the spiritual rivers into the afterlife, her swift white horses ensuring they reach the summerlands safely without being intercepted by malevolent entities.
##### Worshippers
Cavalry officers, rangers, paladins of devotion, animal trainers, teamsters, messengers, stablemasters, and nature domain clerics. Her shrines are a common sight across continental trade routes, located inside bustling commercial livery stables, cavalry forts, or mountain passes where travellers hang iron horseshoes and leave offerings of sweet oats, apples, and fresh straw.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Honour the Working Beast: Treat your mounts, beasts of burden, and companion animals with absolute dignity and care; those who abuse or overwork their creatures face your immediate wrath.
- Safeguard the Open Road: Provide protection, shelter, and medical care to travellers, pilgrims, and messengers navigating dangerous wild territory.
- Ride with Swift Honour: Face your battles and your journeys with unshakeable confidence, acting as a swift, vanguard shield for those who cannot move quickly enough to defend themselves.
