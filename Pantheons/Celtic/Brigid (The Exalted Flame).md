##### Description/Background
Brigid (meaning "The Exalted One") is one of the most powerful, beloved, and enduring goddesses in Celtic mythology. A daughter of the great Dagda, she is a classical master of three core, distinct disciplines that the ancient Celts saw as completely interconnected: the fire of the forge (craftsmanship and metalworking), the fire of the hearth (healing, fertility, and childbirth), and the fire of the mind (poetry, inspiration, and divine law). Because her domains encompass almost every element of early human culture, she was widely revered across disparate tribes, eventually seamlessly transitioning into historical Christian folklore as a foundational patron saint.

Her mythology emphasizes balance, innovation, and unity. She is credited with inventing **keening**, a sacred, poetic form of mourning ritual, which she sang while weeping for her fallen son, Ruadán, during the war between the Tuatha Dé Danann and the Fomorians. Her presence in a campaign world bridges the rough, elemental wild with advanced, refined civilization. She represents the sparks of divine inspiration that turn base materials into breath taking armour, raw words into legendary lore, and deep sickness into profound restoration.
##### Worshippers
Blacksmiths, armorers, lore bards, hedge doctors, forge domain clerics, transformation wizards, and artisanal guilds. Her temples function simultaneously as community hospitals and state-of-the-art metalworking foundries, where a dedicated sisterhood of clerics keeps an eternal flame burning day and night, ensuring that no malicious entity or dark magic can snuff out the local community's productive warmth.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Stoke the Creative Spark: Never allow a day to pass without making something beautiful or functional; view your crafts, poems, and spells as an extension of the goddess's inner fire.
- Mend What is Broken: Dedicate yourself to repairing the world, whether that means forging a broken blade, knitting a shattered bone, or soothing an aching soul.
- Guard the Hearth Fire: Protect the vulnerable, foster the growth of the young, and guarantee that the light of community and hospitality is never snuffed out by winter's darkness or tyrannical fear.
