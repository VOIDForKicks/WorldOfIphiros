##### Description/Background
Teutates (whose name directly derives from the Celtic word _teutā_, meaning "tribe" or "people") is one of the most foundational and heavily revered deities of continental Gaulish myth. Forming a prominent triad with Esus and Taranis, Teutates was not an abstract god of natural elements, but the literal divine personification of the community's survival, law, and sovereignty. He is the steadfast guardian of the collective, the deity responsible for establishing the legal oaths that kept clans united and providing the martial strength needed to defend tribal borders against outside threats.

While he acted as a fierce protector, classical Roman accounts details that Teutates demanded an exacting and absolute price for the continued preservation of his people. His sacrificial rituals were unique and unyielding: chosen victims were plunged headfirst into massive, ceremonial cauldrons filled with water or ale and held beneath the surface until they drowned. This ritual drowning was viewed not as a senseless act of cruelty, but as a solemn, cleansing transaction, returning life to the sacred vessels of the tribe to guarantee collective prosperity, victorious warfare, and immunity from plague.
##### Worshippers
Oath of the crown paladins, order domain clerics, clan chieftains, town watch captains, community judges, and local militia commanders. His shrines are centrally located assembly halls, fortified village squares, or great feasting houses where communities gather to settle legal disputes, swear binding oaths, and pour libations into large central vats.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Sacrifice for the Collective: Place the safety, health, and survival of your community or party entirely above your personal ambitions or comfort.
- Uphold the Tribal Law: Respect the established customs, treaties, and legal boundaries of your people; punish those who threaten internal unity or break their sworn words.
- Stand as the Sovereign Wall: Confront outside invaders, raiders, and chaotic entities with unyielding force; you are the shield that ensures your people live to see another dawn.
