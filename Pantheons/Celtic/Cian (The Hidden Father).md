##### Description/Background
Cian (meaning "The Long-Lasting" or "Ancient One") is a pivotal yet tragic hero-god within the Irish Celtic pantheon, best known as the father of Lugh Broad-Shield (the pan-Celtic god of kingship and mastery). A prominent noble of the Tuatha Dé Danann and a son of Dian Cecht, the god of healing, Cian possessed exceptional talent in magic, alchemical medicine, and shapeshifting. His defining myth centres on his bitter rivalry with Balor of the Evil Eye, the monstrous king of the Fomorians. Balor stole Cian's prized possession: a magical cow of absolute abundance, the _Glas Gaibhnenn_.

Seeking retribution, Cian infiltrated Balor’s floating iron fortress on Tory Island by utilizing advanced illusions to disguise himself as a woman. There, he successfully wooed Balor's daughter, Ériu/Ethniu, who had been locked away in a solitary tower due to a prophecy stating Balor would be slain by his own grandson. Their secret union brought forth Lugh. Tragically, Cian met a grim end when he was hunted down by the sons of Tuireann. Despite using his shapeshifting powers to transform into a pig to hide within a herd, his enemies recognized him, transformed themselves into hounds, and brutally stoned him to death. His passing ignited a legendary quest for blood-fine (_éric_) that reshaped the magical treasures of the Irish world, cementing Cian as the ultimate symbol of enduring lineages hidden beneath tragedy.
##### Worshippers
Arcane tricksters, moon druids, path of the beast barbarians, spymasters, multi-classed wizard/rogues, and families protecting hidden sorcerous heritages. His shrines are tucked away inside hollowed-out hills, secret rooms within medical academies, or forest clearings where illusions cast by senior clerics keep the entire site completely invisible to uninitiated eyes.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Master the Art of the Shifting Form: Never rely solely on a single face or raw strength; use illusions, disguises, and clever transformations to bypass obstacles and outmanoeuvre your foes.
- Preserve the Lineage: Protect the children, the students, and the legacies entrusted to your care; ensure that the spark of hope survives even if you must fall to protect it.
- Avenge the Slain: Never let the murder of an innocent or a kin-member go unanswered; pursue justice diligently, tracking the wrongdoers across any distance or plane.
