##### Description/Background
Rhiannon is a brilliant, strong-willed figure of profound elegance and unyielding resilience hailing from the Welsh branch of Celtic mythology (_The Mabinogion_). She originally appeared to Pwyll, Prince of Dyfed, as an ethereal queen riding a magnificent, unhurried white horse. Though Pwyll and his fastest horsemen pursued her with everything they had, her horse moved at a steady, effortless pace that remained magically out of reach until Pwyll finally thought to ask her politely to stop. Opting to marry a mortal rather than her intended Otherworld suitor, Rhiannon brought supernatural prosperity to Dyfed, accompanied by the legendary Birds of Rhiannon (_Adar Rhiannon_), whose otherworldly songs were so powerful they could wake the dead or lull the living into a blissful sleep.

Her story took a deeply tragic turn when she was falsely accused of murdering and consuming her infant son, Pryderi, who had vanished from his nursery under mysterious circumstances. Rather than lashing out in vengeance or despair, Rhiannon maintained her innocence while enduring a cruel, humiliating penance: for seven years, she was forced to sit by the horse-mounting block of the palace gates, tell her tragic tale to visiting strangers, and physically carry them up to the castle on her back like a beast of burden. Her supernatural grace, patience, and absolute lack of bitterness outlasted her accusers. When her son was discovered alive and safely returned years later, her status as the rightful, resilient Queen of Sovereignty was permanently cemented.
##### Worshippers
Animal trainers, messengers, equestrians, circle of dreams druids, bards, and paladins of devotion who prize internal fortitude. Her shrines are typically pristine white stables, crossroad mounting blocks, or hillside pastures where her followers leave offerings of fresh apples, woven horsehair ribbons, and intricate wooden bird flutes.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Endure with Regal Dignity: When faced with false accusations, deep betrayal, or tragic circumstances, let your unyielding patience and inner strength outshine the malice of your enemies.
- Honour the Boundless Steed: Treat horses, pegasi, and beasts of burden as sacred partners rather than tools; protect them from cruel exploitation and match their natural loyalty.
- Soothe the Burdened Mind: Use your voice, your music, or your magic to bring comforting rest to the weary and hope to those facing immense psychological hardship.
