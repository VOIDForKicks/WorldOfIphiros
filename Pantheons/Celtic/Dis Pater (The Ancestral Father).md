##### Description/Background:
While "Dis Pater" originated as a Roman god of the underworld, Julius Caesar famously used the name in his _Gallic Wars_ to describe a primary deity worshipped by the Celts. According to Caesar, the Gauls claimed to be descended directly from Dis Pater, viewing him not as a monster, but as an ancestral father of their entire race. Because he is a god of the deep underground, the Celts associated him with the transition from darkness to light; this is why the Celtic calendar counts time by nights rather than by days.

In Gaulish archeology, he is frequently identified with Sucellus, the "Good Striker." He is depicted as a bearded, mature figure holding a large, long-handled mallet. His mallet has dual, paradoxical powers: a strike from one side can take a life or plunge a soul into the underworld, while a strike from the other side can heal, resurrect, or unearth hidden wealth from the soil. He is a solemn but fair custodian of the deceased, ensuring souls reach their final resting place in the Shadowfell or the Outer Planes.
##### Worshippers: 
Miners, ancestral traditionalists, undertakers, Grave clerics, and those who honours their lineages. His shrines are usually dark, underground structures, such as deep caves, crypts, or the lowest levels of dwarven or human settlements.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Respect the Dead: Ensure all souls receive proper burial rites and are safely guided to the afterlife. Fiercely oppose mindless undead animation.
- Remember the Ancestors: Guard the history, lineages, and ancient traditions of the mortal races.
- Balance the Scales: Understand that death is a necessary boundary, but remember that out of darkness and the deep earth comes new life and wealth.
