##### Description/Background
Nuada, known universally as _Nuada Airgetlám_ ("Nuada of the Silver Hand"), is the foundational high king and premier chieftain of the Tuatha Dé Danann. A paragon of chivalry, noble leadership, and martial prowess, he led his divine host during their migration to Ireland. During the First Battle of Mag Tuired against the native Fir Bolg, Nuada engaged the champion Sreng in a cataclysmic duel, where a sweeping blow from Sreng's blade severed Nuada's right arm. Though the Danann won the war, ancient cosmic laws dictated that an physically imperfect or marred ruler could not hold the throne of sovereignty. Nuada was forced to abdicate his crown, yielding leadership to the tragic, half-Fomorian tyrant Bres.

During his exile, the physician-god Dian Cecht worked alongside the legendary smith Credne to forge a fully functional, articulated prosthetic arm crafted from pure, enchanted silver. Later, Dian Cecht's brilliant son, Miach, magically regrew Nuada's original flesh and blood over the silver framework, completely restoring his physical integrity. Reclaiming his rightful throne, Nuada banished the corrupt Bres, ushering in an era of defensive preparation against the Fomorian threat. Recognizing the tactical brilliance of the newly arrived hero Lugh, Nuada selflessly abdicated his military command to the young multi-talented god before the Second Battle of Mag Tuired. Though Nuada was ultimately slain and decapitated by the death-gaze of the giant Balor of the Evil Eye, his legacy stands as the ultimate symbol of noble self-sacrifice, kingly duty, and justice.
##### Worshippers
Paladins of devotion or the crown, war and order domain clerics, high-born military commanders, honourable duellists, constitutional judges, and monarchs striving to rule with fairness. His temples are fortified, white-stone keep-sanctuaries adorned with immaculate silver statues, where military veterans can find prosthetics, rehabilitation, and spiritual solace after enduring the scars of war.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Govern with Selfless Justice: True authority is earned through service, sacrifice, and absolute fairness; place the survival, prosperity, and moral health of your people entirely above your personal ambition.
- Maintain Your Honor Amid Scars: Physical trauma, disability, and heavy defeats do not diminish your spiritual worth or your capability to lead; adapt, rebuild what was broken, and return to the front line stronger.
- Recognize the Gifts of Others: A great leader knows when to step aside for the greater good; do not let ego prevent you from elevating exceptional talent, wisdom, or capability when it serves the realm.
