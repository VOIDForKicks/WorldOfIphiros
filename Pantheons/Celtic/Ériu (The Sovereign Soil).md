##### Description/Background
Ériu, alongside her divine sisters Banba and Fódla, is a prominent goddess of sovereignty belonging to the Tuatha Dé Danann. She is the literal personification of the land, so deeply tied to its identity that her name evolved into the modern word for the country itself: Ireland (_Éire_). In Celtic tradition, kingship was not merely a political appointment; it was a mystical marriage between the ruler and the land goddess. If the king was just, pure, and honourable, Ériu would bless the realm with bountiful harvests, peaceful weather, and safety from invaders. If the king was corrupt or physically marred, the land would rot and wither in protest.

When the invading Milesians (the mythic ancestors of modern humans) arrived to claim the island, they encountered the three sisters. While Banba and Fódla used magic to repel them, Ériu recognized the shifting tides of fate. She met the Milesian poet and judge Amergin on the hill of Uisneach, the spiritual centre of the world. She welcomed the newcomers, predicting that the island would belong to them forever, provided they treated her memory with absolute respect. In gratitude, Amergin swore a sacred oath that the land would eternally bear her name. Though the Tuatha Dé Danann eventually retreated into the _Sidhe_ (the subterranean fairy mounds), Ériu remains the unyielding soul of the soil.
##### Worshippers
High-ranking monarchs, oath of the crown paladins, order domain clerics, regional governors, folk hero fighters, and local druidic circles who guard the boundaries of ancient kingdoms. Her shrines are grand stone seats or thrones carved directly into bedrock on the highest hills overlooking vast valleys, where rulers are traditionally crowned and offerings of local grain, wine, and soil are buried.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Protect the Spirit of the Realm: Your duty is to the land and its people, not just the crown; stand firmly against tyrants, blights, or extraplanar corruptions that bleed the soil dry.
- Honour the Sacred Compact: Ensure that laws are just and leaders remain worthy of their power; act as a check against royal corruption and greed.
- Defend Your Borders: Guard the home front with unyielding resolve; stand as an immovable wall against foreign invaders or chaotic monsters trying to conquer your community.
