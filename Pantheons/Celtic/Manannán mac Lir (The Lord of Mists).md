##### Description/Background
Manannán mac Lir ("Son of the Sea") is the preeminent Celtic god of the ocean, magic, and storms. Unlike the Tuatha Dé Danann, who rule the physical hills of Ireland, Manannán is an older, elemental entity who presides over Emain Ablach (the Isle of Apple Trees), the mystical Celtic Otherworld of eternal youth and abundance. He is the master of illusion and psychological warfare; his magnificent cloak can change colour to match the sea or the sky, allowing him to seamlessly blend into the horizon or conjure blinding sea mists (_Feth Fiadha_) that turn invaders around or drive them to madness.

Manannán possesses an unparalleled arsenal of legendary artefacts. He rides across the roaring waves in a self-propelling chariot pulled by his majestic horse, Enbarr of the Flowing Mane, who can run over water as easily as dry land. He is the original owner of _Fragarach_ ("The Answerer"), a sword that no armor can stop and which forces anyone with its blade at their throat to speak the absolute truth. As a psychopomp, he guides the souls of the dead across the turbulent western seas into the tranquil realms of the afterlife, protecting them from the horrors of the deep.
##### Worshippers
Sailors, swashbuckler rogues, naval privateers, tempest domain clerics, circle of the land (coast) druids, and seafaring wizards. His shrines are coastal sea caves, lighthouses, or isolated rocky islands where his followers burn aromatic driftwood and leave offerings of fine silver coins, exotic spices, and sweet mead cast directly into the breaking surf.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Rule the Shifting Tide: Adapt seamlessly to every circumstance; be as fluid as the calm sea and as devastatingly violent as the incoming storm when provoked.
- Master the Art of Illusion: Never show your true strength or strategy upfront; use deception, mists, and magical misdirection to confound your enemies before they can strike.
- Guard the Voyagers: Protect those who brave the perils of the ocean or the transitions of life, ensuring safe passage for travellers and guiding lost souls safely home.
##### Favoured Weapon
Scimitar or Rapier (styled to mimic waves or flowing ocean currents)
