##### Description/Background: 
The Morrígan (often translated as "The Phantom Queen" or "Great Queen") is one of the most enigmatic and terrifying deities of the Tuatha Dé Danann. She is a goddess of fate, war, and sovereignty who frequently appears as a single entity or as a tripartite goddess, a trinity alongside her sisters Badb and Macha (and sometimes Nemain). The Morrígan does not typically engage in direct, physical combat with a sword or shield; instead, she dominates the battlefield through psychological warfare, sorcery, and prophetic incantations. She is a master shapeshifter, famously taking the form of a hooded crow or raven to hover directly over bloody conflicts, instilling a paralyzing, blood-chilling panic in those destined to fall while filling her chosen warriors with an unstoppable battle-frenzy. She also represents the literal soil and sovereignty of the land, testing kings and heroes, most notably Cú Chulainn, to see if they possess the spiritual grit to rule or survive. Her appearance at a riverbank washing the blood-stained armour of a warrior (in the guise of the Washer at the Ford) is an absolute, unavoidable omen of impending death.
##### Worshippers:
Warlocks, divination wizards, frontline commanders, political conspirators, and rangers or druids associated with scavenging beasts. Her shrines are usually hidden away in dark, deeply unsettling places, shadowy groves choked with ravens, abandoned battlefields, or misty river forks where the veil between life and death is razor-thin.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Accept the Omens: Read the signs of fate and do not coddle mortals with false hope; the thread of destiny is unyielding, and death comes for all who are marked. 
- Seize Your Destiny: Fight with absolute, unbridled intensity when fate calls you to action; true sovereignty belongs to those with the willpower to take it. 
- Reap the Spoils: Let the natural order take its course on the fallen; honour the crows and scavengers that cleanse the land after the storm of war.
