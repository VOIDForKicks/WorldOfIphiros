##### Description/Background
Dian Cecht is the brilliant, yet intensely volatile and envious god of healing and medicine within the Tuatha Dé Danann. He is best known for his miraculous medical feats during the First Battle of Mag Tuired. When Nuada, the high king of the Danann, had his arm severed by a Fomorian champion, an injury that legally disqualified him from kingship due to physical imperfection, Dian Cecht worked alongside the smith-god Credne to forge a fully functional, articulating silver arm for the king, earning him the title Nuada Airgetlám (Nuada of the Silver Hand). Furthermore, Dian Cecht blessed the legendary Sláine Well, a mystical spring that instantly healed any wounded Danann warrior thrown into its waters, effectively granting his faction functional immortality on the battlefield.

However, Dian Cecht’s mythology is deeply darkened by an unforgiving, murderous pride. His own son, Miach, proved to be an even more gifted surgeon. Miach, dissatisfied with the silver arm, managed to magically regrow Nuada’s original flesh-and-blood arm over the course of nine days. Consumed by a toxic, professional jealousy that his son had surpassed his medical capabilities, Dian Cecht attacked Miach. He struck his son's head four progressive times with his sword; though Miach's magic healed the first three slashes, the fourth strike cleaved his brain, killing him. From Miach’s grave, 365 healing herbs sprouted, corresponding to his joints and sinews. When Miach's sister, Airmed, arranged the herbs systematically onto her cloak to catalogue their distinct properties, a still-furious Dian Cecht scattered the cloak, ensuring mortals would never fully master the absolute secrets of universal medicine.
##### Worshippers
Alchemists, battlefield surgeons, plague doctors, life domain clerics who demand steep prices for their miracles, and elite wizard-apothecaries. His temples are sterile, strictly run sanitariums and medical academies adorned with silver trim, where uninitiated visitors are forbidden from viewing the inner laboratory vaults, and medical knowledge is hoarded behind lock and key.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Protect the Trade Secrets: Magical and physical medicine is an elite, hard-earned craft; never distribute your highest cures freely to the unworthy or untrained.
- Keep the Body Whole: View physical degradation, scars, and lingering curses as personal insults to your craft; utilize every tool, prosthetic, or spell at your disposal to repair the biological machine.
- Tolerate No Rivals: Strive to be the absolute master of your craft within your circle; do not allow underlings or peers to outshine your achievements without asserting your dominance.
