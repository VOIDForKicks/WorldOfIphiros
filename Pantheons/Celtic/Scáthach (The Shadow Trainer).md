##### Description/Background
Scáthach, whose name translates directly to "The Shadowy One," is a legendary warrior-queen, prophetess, and martial master in the Ulster Cycle of Celtic lore. Operating from Dunscaith Castle (the "Fortress of Shadows") on the mist-shrouded Isle of Skye, she stands apart from the typical courtly politics of the gods. She is the ultimate gatekeeper of combat perfection, running a brutal, supernatural training ground for elite heroes. Her training regimen is famously lethal, designed to push mortals past their physical limits into the realm of superhuman capability.

Her most definitive mythological role is that of a mentor to Ireland's greatest hero, Cú Chulainn. Under her strict tutelage, he mastered incredible feats of acrobatics, tactical positioning, and occult combat manoeuvres. Most notably, Scáthach bestowed upon him her ultimate, terrifying weapon: the _Gáe Bulg_ (the Spear of Mortal Pain). This barbed spear, crafted from the bones of a sea monster, could only be thrown from the foot; once it entered a victim's body, it opened up thirty distinct barbs throughout their flesh, making extraction impossible without cutting the body open. Gifted with second sight, Scáthach does not look at the world with sentimentality; she views combat as an exacting, spiritual science and foresees the violent fates of those she trains.
##### Worshippers
Battle master fighters, kensei or open hand monks, twilight domain clerics, gloom stalker rangers, and weapon smiths who specialize in exotic arms. Her shrines are stark, minimalist training halls, high-altitude mountain passes, or subterranean rings where her followers duel strictly at dawn or dusk, offering broken enemy blades and drops of blood spilled during sparring.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Strive for Flawless Form: Do not rely on luck, brute strength, or messy passion; treat your body and weapon as precision instruments, mastering every stance, strike, and counter.
- Be the Shield in the Shadows: Embrace the darkness and the mist not as a tool for cowardly murder, but as an tactical advantage to neutralize threats before they realize you are there.
- Train the Next Vanguard: Share your martial wisdom with those who possess the raw discipline to survive your tutelage; forge the next generation into unbreakable shields for the realm.
