##### Description/Background:
Belenus (also known as Bel or Belinus) is one of the most ancient, widely worshipped, and powerful deities in the Celtic pantheon. He is a radiant solar god representing the rejuvenating warmth of the sun, the purifying nature of fire, and the restorative powers of light.

His name literally translates to "The Shining One" or "The Bright One." He is traditionally depicted driving a brilliant chariot pulled by majestic horses across the sky, bringing dawn and driving away darkness. His worship is deeply tied to the festival of Beltane ("Bel's Fire"), where large bonfires are lit to bless livestock, purify the community, and welcome the return of summer.

As a god of healing, Belenus is associated with thermal springs and sacred wells. His light doesn't just burn; it cleanses sickness, purges poisons, and knits wounds. He is a benevolent and warm deity, fiercely opposed to plague, undeath, and eternal darkness.
##### Worshippers: 
Paladins, doctors, local community leaders, farmers, and sun-worshipping druids. His temples are grand, open-roofed stone structures designed to catch the first rays of the morning sun, often built near hot springs or high hilltops.

##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Be a Beacon: Warm those who are cold, guide those who are lost in the dark, and bring joy where there is despair.
- Purge Corruption: Destroy the undead, eradicate disease, and extinguish unholy, corrupting fires.
- Honour the Hearth: Protect the community and the home, keeping the defensive fires burning against monsters of the night.
