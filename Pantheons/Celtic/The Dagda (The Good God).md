##### Description/Background:
The Dagda is one of the chief deities and a paternal leader of the Tuatha Dé Danann. His title translates directly to "The Good God," which refers not to his moral standing, but to his absolute competence in all things, he is a master of magic, warfare, wisdom, and craftsmanship alike. He is traditionally depicted as a rustic, large-framed figure wearing a simple tunic, embodying raw vitality and unpretentious power. The Dagda possesses three legendary treasures that define his divine duties. The first is his sacred Cauldron of Plenty, an artefact from which no company ever leaves unsatisfied, symbolizing his dominion over hospitality, harvest, and endless abundance. The second is his massive, dual-ended club (the Lorg Mór), which can slay nine men with a single blow from one end, but instantly restore them to life with the other. His third treasure is the Uaithne, a magical oak harp used to command the natural order of the seasons and play the chords of weeping, laughter, and sleep.
##### Worshippers: 
Farmers, druids, chefs, community protectors, and bards who value hospitality and leadership. Shrines to The Dagda are usually found in lively communal spaces, feast halls, or large grain storehouses, often featuring an ever-burning hearth and a communal stone basin representing his cauldron.

##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets: 
- Provide for the Community: Ensure that no one under your watch goes hungry or without shelter; true strength is measured by the ability to sustain others. 
- Master Many Trades: Never limit your skills or rely solely on brute force; strive to understand magic, labor, and the arts to better serve your allies. 
- Protect the Natural Cycle: Defend the seasonal harvest and the proper balance between life and death, using force only when the prosperity of the innocent is threatened.
