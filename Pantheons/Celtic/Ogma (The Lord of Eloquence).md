##### Description/Background: 
Ogma (also known as Ogmios in Gaulish contexts) is a premier deity of the Tuatha Dé Danann who uniquely bridges the gap between absolute intellect and physical might. He is famously credited with inventing Ogham, the earliest historic script of the Irish language, which he created as a tool for the learned and wise to record secrets and poetry. In continental Gaulish representations, he is depicted as an older, weathered man resembling Hercules, holding a club and a bow, but drawing crowds to himself by fine chains made of gold and amber that attach from his tongue to the ears of his smiling followers, a powerful allegory for the captivating power of eloquent speech. Far from being a frail scholar, Ogma serves as the physical champion of the gods, balancing his immense mental faculties with legendary feats of arms on the battlefield, notably wrestling and reclaiming the sword of the Fomorian king Tethra.
##### Worshippers: 
Bards, wizards, runic scholars, diplomats, and martial athletes who value mental clarity as much as physical fitness. His shrines are often established within grand archives, academic forums, or training grounds where public debates and physical trials are held simultaneously.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets: 
- Sharpen Both Mind and Body: Do not allow your intellect to wither into weakness, nor your strength to dull into ignorance; true perfection lies in the balance of both. 
- Speak with Purpose: Use your words as precision tools to unite, inspire, and persuade; let your speech carry the weight of unshakeable truth. 
- Defend the Written Word: Protect ancient texts, languages, and records from destruction or magical censorship.
