##### Description/Background:
In Irish mythology, Donn is the lord of the dead and the ruler of the afterlife. Unlike psychopomps who warmly guide souls, Donn is a somber, isolated figure. Originally one of the mortal Sons of Míl Espáine, he insulted the goddess of Ireland (Ériu) and was cursed to drown in a fierce storm off the southwest coast. He was buried on a rocky, wave-battered island known as _Tech Duinn_ (The House of Donn). Upon his death, he became divine, and his island became the gathering place where all mortal souls must travel before passing into the true Otherworld.

Donn is closely associated with shipwrecks, sea storms, and the deep ocean darkness. He watches over the dead with detached finality, blowing his great hunting horn to call wandering souls out of the material realm and into his dark house. While he is generally seen as cold and unyielding rather than overtly malevolent, he fiercely guards his domain and does not tolerate souls escaping his grasp.
##### Worshippers:
Lonely sailors, severe undertakers, grave diggers, and doom-driven rangers. Shrines to Donn are rarely found in cities; instead, they are built on desolate sea cliffs, foggy coastlines, or remote rocky islands facing the open ocean.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
 Answer the Call: Understand that all things must eventually die; do not flee from your mortality or prevent others from meeting their destined end.
 Gather the Lost: Prevent souls from remaining stranded as ghosts or phantoms, ensuring they heed the call to the afterlife.
 Weather the Storm: Stand firm and stoic against the chaotic, destructive forces of the world, just as the House of Donn withstands the raging sea.
