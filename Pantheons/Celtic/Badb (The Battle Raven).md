##### Description/Background
Badb (pronounced _Bive_, meaning "Crow" or "Battle Raven"), also known as Badb Catha, is a terrifying war goddess in Irish Celtic mythology. She is most famously recognized as one of the three sisters who form the formidable divine triad known as the Morrígan (the Great Queen), alongside Macha and Anand/Morrigan. While her sisters focus on sovereignty and the strategic plotting of battles, Badb is the raw, visceral manifestation of the battlefield itself. She frequently manifests above swirling clashes as a giant, screeching hooded crow, flapping her wings to stir up mass panic, madness, and blinding confusion among enemy soldiers.

Badb is deeply tied to prophecy and fate, acting as a grim harbinger of violent deaths. Before a major conflict, she often appears to legendary heroes in the guise of the "Washer at the Ford", a pale, spectral woman kneeling at a riverbank, weeping as she washes the blood-soaked armour and clothing of those destined to fall in the upcoming fight. In the foundational mythological battle of _Mag Tuired_, she used her terrifying voice and supernatural illusions to drive the invading Fomorians into a state of paralyzing terror, securing victory for the Tuatha Dé Danann. She does not actively seek to protect mortals; rather, she revels in the chaotic, necessary shedding of blood that reshapes the lineages of the world.
##### Worshippers
Barbarians (especially Path of the Zealot or Wild Magic), war domain clerics, college of spirits bards, echoes, diviners, and chaotic monster hunters. Her shrines are not orderly structures; they are sacrificial stone dolmens hidden deep within mist-shrouded valleys or historical execution fields, left unattended except for the flocks of wild ravens that nest nearby to consume offerings of raw meat and dark ale.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Sow Panic Among Foes: Break the resolve of your enemies before you even strike; use psychological warfare, terrifying war cries, and illusions to let fear win the battle for you.
- Heed the Signs: Pay absolute attention to omens, dreams, and the flights of corvids; never ignore the warnings of fate, for the spirits speak clearly to those who listen.
- Embrace the Chaos of the Fray: Do not look for clean, orderly lines in a fight; throw yourself into the shifting tide of combat, knowing that life and death are determined in the absolute frenzy of bloodshed.
##### Favoured Weapon
