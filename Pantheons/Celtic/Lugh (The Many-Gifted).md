##### Description/Background
Lugh, frequently called _Lugh Lámfhada_ ("Lugh of the Long Arm") or _Samildánach_ ("The Many-Gifted"), is the ultimate hero-king of Celtic lore. He famously won his place among the Tuatha Dé Danann by marching up to the gates of Tara and declaring that he didn't just master one trade, he mastered _all_ of them, serving as a smith, champion, harper, poet, sorcerer, and historian simultaneously. His multi-pantheon lineage is a legendary bridge: he is the son of Cian of the Danann and Ethniu, the daughter of the Fomorian tyrant Balor of the Evil Eye.

When the Fomorians oppressed the land, Lugh took the mantle of supreme war leader. During the Second Battle of Mag Tuired, when Balor unleashed his apocalyptic, blazing death-gaze upon the battlefield, Lugh utilized his legendary slinging skill to hurl a magical sling-stone right through his grandfather's eye, driving the organ out the back of his skull so that its destructive gaze melted the Fomorian army instead. Armed with his infallible, fiery spear (_Gae Assail_) which fought of its own accord and never missed its mark, Lugh ushered in a golden age of law, divine oaths, and unparalleled mastery.
##### Worshippers
Paladins of devotion or the crown, order or light domain clerics, college of lore bards, master artisans, battle masters, and political judges. His temples are grand, sunlit academies and assembly halls located at the highest points of civilized capitals, where grand tournaments of martial capability and artistic craftsmanship are hosted annually.

- Uphold the Binding Oath: Treat your sworn word as an unbreakable cosmic law; expose liars, punish oath-breakers, and ensure that justice is executed with absolute transparency.
- Confront Tyranny Radically: Stand as an unwavering beacon of light against monstrous oppression, deep-seated systemic corruption, and dark, archaic terrors.
