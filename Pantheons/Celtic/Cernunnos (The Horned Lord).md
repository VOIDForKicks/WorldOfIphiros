##### Description/Background
Cernunnos is the ancient, enigmatic Lord of Wild Things, serving as one of the most prominent and elemental figures in Celtic iconography. Classically depicted sitting cross-legged in a meditative pose, he is adorned with majestic stag antlers blooming from his head. He is flanked by wild beasts, most notably a unique, ram-headed horned serpent, and is frequently shown holding or wearing a heavy metal **torc**, the Celtic symbol of high status, nobility, and wealth. In his other hand, he often holds a purse spilling with coins or grain, symbolizing the fertile riches of the earth.

Cernunnos operates completely outside the boundaries of structured cities and imperial law; he is the raw, pulsing heart of the untamed wilderness. He is not a god of malice or mindless savagery, but rather the mediator of the natural cycle, presiding over life, death, hunting, and subsequent regeneration. He represents the line where the physical world meets the spiritual deep, guiding the souls of the dead through the root systems of the ancient forests into the chthonic realms below. His presence is felt in the absolute silence of an old-growth forest and the frantic rush of the autumn rut.
##### Worshippers
Druids (especially Circle of the Shepherd or Moon), rangers, wood elves, oath of the ancients paladins, hunters, and rural communities living on the dangerous edges of the frontier. His shrines are entirely natural spaces: the hollow trunk of a massive, ancient oak tree, a moss-covered stone circle in a deep glen, or a cave hidden behind a waterfall where hunters leave shed antlers, wild berries, and pelts as tokens of gratitude.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Protect the Primal Balance: Defend the wild places of the world from unnatural corruption, systematic over-exploitation, and industrial destruction.
- Honour the Harvest and the Hunt: Take only what you need from the wilderness to survive; give profound thanks to the spirit of the animal that feeds you, wasting nothing.
- Embrace the Cyclical Law: Accept that life, death, decay, and rebirth are the eternal, unchanging truths of the cosmos; do not fear the transition from light to shadow.
