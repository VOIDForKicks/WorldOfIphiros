##### Description/Background
Arawn is the enigmatic, unyielding ruler of Annwn, the subterranean, subterranean-adjacent, or island-based realm of the dead in Welsh Celtic myth. Far from a malicious or fiendish entity, Arawn is depicted as a deeply honourable, highly civilized, and fair monarch who fiercely guards the souls of the deceased. His most famous legend centres on his profound relationship with the mortal prince Pwyll of Dyfed. After an accidental dispute over hunted game, the two rulers struck an extraordinary pact to swap identities, physical appearances, and kingdoms for an entire year.

During this year, Pwyll ruled Annwn and fought a bitter war on Arawn's behalf, successfully slaying Arawn’s great rival, Hafgan, with a single, disciplined strike (as Hafgan could magically survive any subsequent blows). Crucially, Pwyll remained completely chaste out of respect for Arawn, turning his back to Arawn’s queen every single night. When the year ended and they reverted to their true selves, Arawn discovered Pwyll’s absolute fidelity, cementing an eternal, unbreakable bond between the mortal world and the dead. Arawn rides through the night skies accompanied by the _Cwn Annwn_ (the Hounds of Annwn), spectral, snow-white hunting hounds with blood-red ears whose distant baying signals the impending death or harvesting of wicked souls.
##### Worshippers
Grave domain clerics, twilight domain sentinels, monster hunter rangers, oath of devotion paladins, and local executioners or undertakers who view death as a necessary, quiet transition. His shrines are solitary stone dolmens built inside dense, autumn forests, or dark, quiet underground vaults where his followers leave offerings of raw game, dark wines, and silver teeth.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets
- Guard the Threshold of Death: Ensure that the departed cross safely into the afterlife; hunt down and permanently destroy any undead, necromancers, or parasitic spirits trying to escape or manipulate the natural cycle.
- Keep Absolute Loyalty: Honour your oaths and contracts with flawless precision; a broken promise is a stain on your soul that will face judgment in the underworld.
- Track the Corrupt: Use the patience and methodical focus of the cosmic hunt to track down tyrants, betrayers, and monsters who think they can hide from their ultimate fate.
