##### Description/Background:
In the mythological cycles of Ireland, Ecne is the literal personification of wisdom, high knowledge, and enlightenment. While often spoken of as a singular entity, Ecne has a deeply unique and fascinating lineage: in some traditions, he is considered the son of three brothers of the Tuatha Dé Danann (Brian, Iuchar, and Iucharba), effectively representing a divine trinity where three minds unite to birth absolute intellect and poetry.

Unlike gods who hoard secrets or hoard knowledge to rule, Ecne represents the fluid, spark-like nature of inspiration and understanding. His wisdom is deeply tied to the ancient concept of the _Salmon of Knowledge_, which fed on hazelnuts dropping from sacred trees into a pool of cosmic insight. Ecne is a calm, analytical deity who values truth over emotion, logic over brute strength, and the meticulous preservation of history.
##### Worshippers: 
Scholars, wizards, bards, judges, and historians. His places of worship are quiet, structured environments, grand libraries, secluded cliffside monasteries, or tranquil springs surrounded by ancient hazel wood trees.
##### Cleric/Paladin Tenets:
- Seek the Truth: Never accept a lie or half-truth; actively pull back the veil of ignorance wherever it rests.
- Preserve the Record: Treat historical facts and scrolls as sacred relics. To destroy knowledge is the ultimate sin.
- Contemplate Before Action: Never act out of unbridled anger or haste. Analyse the board, understand your enemy, and move with precise calculation.
